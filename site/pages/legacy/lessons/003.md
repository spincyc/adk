# Legacy lesson 003: common-cathode RGB LED

**Frozen and unsupported.** This lesson uses the retired `adk::led::Rgb` and
`adk::color` preview. New work should compose supported digital and PWM output
components.

The circuit drove a verified common-cathode RGB LED through three independent
330 Ω resistors on D6, D5, and D3. Its durable lessons are:

- verify the package pinout rather than assuming lead order;
- protect every color channel independently;
- distinguish PWM duty cycle from PWM frequency; and
- use an instrument before claiming a measured waveform or frequency.
