# Legacy lesson 001: Mega built-in LED

**Frozen and unsupported.** This lesson uses the retired global lifecycle and
`adk::led::Mono` preview. Use its observation method and electrical guidance,
but use the supported digital-output lesson for new work.

The exercise blinked `LED_BUILTIN` with a 100 ms high interval and a 100 ms low
interval. Its useful ideas remain:

- distinguish a commanded state from an observed result;
- calculate period and frequency before building;
- repeat observations; and
- state the limits of visual evidence.
