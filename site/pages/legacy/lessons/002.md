# Legacy lesson 002: two independent LEDs

**Frozen and unsupported.** This lesson uses two retired `adk::led::Mono`
objects. Use the supported digital-output interfaces for new circuits.

The circuit used separate current-limited branches on D4 and D7. Its durable
lessons are:

- every LED branch needs its own resistor;
- a shared ground does not imply shared control;
- polarity and breadboard connectivity must be verified; and
- faults should be isolated one branch at a time.
