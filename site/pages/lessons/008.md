# Calibrate and stabilize a sampled signal

Status: **Host verified; physical acceptance pending**<br>
Energy class: **E1** — low-voltage Mega analog input and current-limited LEDs

This lesson separates facts from interpretation. A raw sample records what the
ADC observed. Calibration maps that count into a named range. Filtering changes
only when the application supplies a sample, so a recorded sample sequence can
be replayed exactly.

The narrative pipeline is `AnalogInput → LinearCalibration → MovingAverage →
Deadband → PwmOutput`. The controlled 10 kΩ potentiometer remains on A0 so the
learner can study software behavior before lesson 009 adds photoresistor
variability.

TP1 is the input-voltage test point; the D6 witness LED exposes the stabilized
output. Step the potentiometer, predict the immediate raw change and slower
filtered response, then interpret disagreement at the first point in the chain
where evidence diverges.

The canonical run begins with a measured 250 ms D13 acquisition pulse and
750 ms dark separator. It then emits optional CSV rows in
`raw,calibrated,average,held` order while TP-A0, the D6 waveform, and LED
brightness provide the required non-Serial observations. A fixed 120-second
deadline shuts the circuit down without user input and provides a 250 ms
analyzer window before the sketch halts; reset begins a fresh timed run.

The exact net is Mega 5 V to the potentiometer outer-high terminal, center
wiper to A0/TP-A0, and outer-low terminal to GND. The witness net is D6 through
the measured resistor to LED anode, then LED cathode to GND. Record the
regulated 5 V source rating and LED identity/forward voltage; proceed only
when the worst-case calculated LED current is at most 15 mA.

The printable lesson requires exact board, supply, component, meter, analyzer,
and probe identities. A one-pin-at-a-time 10 kΩ/10 kΩ midpoint fixture checks
released pins in separate runs. Install or move it only with USB removed, and
never open ground while powered. A compile-time injected D6 claim conflict
proves acquisition rollback releases A0 and D13; returning to Lesson 007 is
separately named configuration restoration. A distinct upstream USB-removal
procedure records prediction, observation, and interpretation without
confusing de-energization with high impedance. None of those physical checks
is claimed complete here.

Open, short, saturation, invalid calibration, and restart are explicit cases.
Serial logs may show exact values but are not required to observe success.
Keep the lesson 007 10 kΩ potentiometer and resistor-limited LED circuit so
only the interpretation changes. A rail reading is a suspected fault, not a
complete diagnosis, and a disconnected analog lead can float to a plausible
value. Filtering never makes that electrical ambiguity safe or valid.
