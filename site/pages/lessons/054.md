# Lesson 054 — inert IR command translator

Status: **E0 copied-fixture integration; exact receiver, emitter, display, and
bench acceptance open**

Energy class: **E0 transactional policy**
Safety boundary: **copied receive evidence and inert carrier-envelope intent
only; no receiver sampling, carrier output, emitted light, or device control**

`InertIrTranslator` composes the copied receive evidence from Lesson 052 with
the immutable local emission catalog from Lesson 053:

`valid attributable receive symbol → different allowlisted local symbol`

The project owns its decoder, fixed 100-word receive storage,
`CapturedIrEvidence`, and `KnownIrEmissionPolicy`. It owns no receiver,
emitter, pin, timer, interrupt, carrier endpoint, keypad, display, LED, bus,
supply, or optical power path.

## A translator, not a replay bridge

The mapping is fixed in firmware and deliberately changes the symbol:

| Valid received symbol | Local transmit symbol |
|---|---|
| `StationPing` | `StationReady` |
| `StationReady` | `StationAcknowledge` |
| `StationCancel` | no transmission; cancellation dominates |
| `StationAcknowledge` | `StationPing` |

Only a `KnownValid`, `IntegrityVerified` record from the configured qualified
source can select a mapping. The receive tuple, source constraint, and output
symbol are covered by the immutable mapping digest.

Repeat, unknown, malformed, truncated, overflowed, stale, source-faulted,
self-echo, and unlisted valid records remain visible as evidence but never
become transmit authority. Captured pulse words cannot be supplied as a local
command, and there is no independent local-selection or arbitrary-waveform
API. The project is not a learning remote, universal remote, or
unknown-protocol bridge.

## One atomic update envelope

`IrTranslatorUpdateInput` is the sole coordinator ingress. One copied envelope
can contain a cancellation, a prepared commit, receive evidence, and actual
emission evidence at one supplied microsecond. The translator validates the
complete envelope before mutation, then applies a fixed precedence:

1. reject a structurally invalid envelope without mutation;
2. preserve an already-latched dependency or safety fault;
3. apply matching cancellation and invalidate outstanding candidates;
4. copy and classify receive evidence;
5. correlate actual-emission evidence;
6. commit a still-current parent and child candidate and advance them; and
7. publish presentation intent last.

Cancellation therefore cannot be hidden by malformed receive evidence or an
emission transition at the same timestamp. Those independent receive and
emission dispositions remain observable even when cancellation dominates.
Splitting co-inputs into a caller-chosen order cannot change the result because
there are no separate receive, cancel, commit, or emission entry points.

## Bound parent and child candidates

`prepareTranslation(operationId, now)` uses only the currently admitted
Lesson 052 evidence generation and the fixed mapping. It reserves one parent
candidate and the exact Lesson 053 child candidate.

The returned `IrTranslatorPreview` binds:

- translator owner, instance epoch, configuration revision, and mapping
  digest;
- parent generation, operation identity, and copied-input digest;
- receive provenance and exact Lesson 052 evidence generation;
- received and different transmitted symbols;
- immutable emission-catalog revision and digest; and
- the complete Lesson 053 preview.

`canCommit()` checks every binding before mutation. The caller can return the
preview only in the atomic update envelope at the same microsecond. Foreign,
stale, reconstructed, reused, reset-invalidated, and shutdown-invalidated
previews reject. A simultaneous valid cancellation invalidates both parent and
child candidates before either can commit.

After complete preflight, the child commit and parent publication describe one
attributable operation. A rejection changes neither child nor parent. This is
a bounded transaction over semantic values, not evidence that an electrical
endpoint emitted anything.

## Actual emission is separate evidence

A policy commit time is not optical start, and logical completion is not
endpoint completion. Round-trip correlation begins only after
`IrEmitterEvidence` supplies the exact qualified source, transaction, actual
start, actual completion, and status. E0 replays explicitly synthetic values;
a future E1 adapter must obtain them from a qualified endpoint.

The half-duplex self-echo interval is:

```text
[actualStartedAt, actualCompletedAt + echoGuard)
```

A receive observation inside that half-open interval is copied and classified
`SelfEchoSuppressed`. It cannot complete the round trip. Exactly at the guard
end, the observation becomes eligible for the response interval:

```text
responseStart = actualCompletedAt + echoGuard
response interval = [responseStart, responseStart + responseWindow)
elapsed = receiveObservedAt - actualCompletedAt
```

One tick before `responseStart` is still self-echo. Exactly at
`responseStart` is eligible. One tick before the exclusive response end is
eligible; exactly at the end is a timeout. `elapsed` includes the echo guard
because it retains latency from actual completion.

All interval arithmetic follows the unsigned modular half-range rule.
Overflow, regression, or exact-half-range ambiguity rejects or faults without
publishing a partial result. The implementation never shortens an ambiguous
window or treats it as immediately due.

Lesson 052 supplies an observation time, not a capture-start time. E0 therefore
proves deterministic observation-time correlation only. It makes no claim
about optical arrival, receiver latency, carrier accuracy, or physical
round-trip timing.

## One complete round-trip record

`IrRoundTripResult` remains canonical and incomplete until all attribution
checks pass. A successful result publishes, in one mutation:

- operation identity and transmitted symbol;
- emission-catalog identity and transmitter source;
- actual start and completion times plus modular elapsed duration;
- exact receive disposition and categorical evidence strength;
- complete receive provenance and evidence generation; and
- the correlation disposition and status.

Payload equality alone is insufficient. A response must come from the
qualified source, be valid and integrity-verified, fall outside self-echo and
inside the response window, map to the expected symbol, and match the exact
operation, mapping, catalog, transmitter, and actual-emission evidence.
Fields from different updates or operations can never be assembled into one
successful record.

## Named result cells

The canonical E0 sketch replays the complete staged path: admit a synthetic
`StationPing`, prepare and commit its `StationReady` translation, record
synthetic actual-emission evidence, suppress a `StationPing` observation one
tick before the echo-guard boundary, then accept a `StationReady` response
exactly at the response-window start.

Its separate named cells retain:

- translation operation, received symbol, transmitted symbol, and
  disposition;
- receive sequence, source, disposition, strength, and status;
- transmit transaction, symbol, source, inert envelope intent, and status;
- independent receive, transmit, and translator fault status;
- suppressed-echo count and disposition; and
- round-trip operation, elapsed microseconds, transmitted symbol,
  correlation disposition, status, and completion.

Additional cells retain initialization, fixture-validation, prepare, and
commit results. Together they make the sketch's acquire, configure, observe,
decide, actuate, actual-emission, self-echo, response, and round-trip stages
inspectable without Serial.

The sketch remains one canonical replay, not the entire verification matrix.
Cancellation collisions, every malformed and unknown classification, forged
candidates, timing ambiguity, and fault permutations remain executable host
tests. Compiling the sketch does not claim that a powered Mega executed it or
that any optical activity occurred.

A future E1 presentation keeps receive, transmit, and fault indications
distinct. Receive activity remains visible for unknown or self-echo-suppressed
evidence; transmit activity follows only an admitted local envelope; and fault
presentation cannot grant authority, cancel work, change correlation, or
disguise inactive intent.

## Deterministic replay

Executable host tests cover every fixed mapping, proof that every transmitted
symbol differs from its input, and every receive disposition. They also cover
all update presence combinations and same-time permutations of cancellation,
commit, receive, and actual-emission evidence.

Boundary cases include candidate forgery and staleness, reset and shutdown,
source and configuration mismatch, self-echo and response-window edges,
timeout, rollover, ambiguous modular time, missed service, and atomic
field-by-field round-trip publication. The suppressed-echo counter's
saturating invariant is proved without pretending to replay 4,294,967,296
events: a compile-time assertion proves that its helper maps `UINT32_MAX` to
`UINT32_MAX`, while executable replay proves the reachable ordinary increment
path. Two independent instances replay the same copied inputs to identical
field values.

Late service immediately derives the intent true at the supplied time; missed
carrier intervals are never replayed as a catch-up burst. A future qualified
electrical endpoint must independently expire to off if service is missed.

## Resource proof

With Arduino AVR core 1.8.8 and AVR GCC 7.3.0, the canonical compile-only Mega
sketch measures **16,162 bytes of flash** and **1,343 bytes of static SRAM**.
The measured maximum E0 composition, including the existing Lesson 025
capture/interrupt state and the translator fixture, measures **21,864 bytes of
flash** and **3,531 bytes of static SRAM**. Both remain inside the reviewed
Lesson 054 hard gates of 32 KiB flash and 4,096 bytes static SRAM.

`InertIrTranslator` measures **407 bytes**, excluding its required
caller-owned **400-byte** `uint32_t[100]` receive buffer. The buffer remains
caller-owned so its lifetime and storage cost are explicit; the initialized
translator writes it exclusively. The object plus required buffer therefore
occupy **807 bytes**, but this does not turn the buffer into hidden coordinator
state or relax the reusable-child size gates.

The conservative maximum synchronous path plus interrupt reserve is
approximately **888 bytes**. This misses the reviewed **800-byte target** but
passes the **1,024-byte hard gate**. Against the Mega 2560's 8,192-byte SRAM,
the maximum measured static composition leaves **3,773 bytes** after that
stack reserve:

```text
8,192 - 3,531 - 888 = 3,773 bytes
```

These measurements prove fixed-storage packaging and memory margin. They do
not prove runtime stack high-water behavior, electrical timing, endpoint
expiry, receiver fidelity, emitted optical power, or physical round-trip
operation.

## Use with

At E0, use this project with the [Lesson 052 copied IR evidence](052.md) and
[Lesson 053 known local emission policy](053.md). Supply only checked-in or
synthetic receive frames, synthetic actual-emission evidence, explicit
microsecond time, and named memory result cells. That composition proves
copying, fixed authority, atomic coordination, cancellation precedence,
self-echo classification, and deterministic attribution without hardware.

E1 may connect only separately qualified exact receiver, emitter and driver,
carrier endpoint, keypad, indicators, LCD, board, and supply. That work needs
primary component evidence, an authoritative formal schematic, complete pin,
timer, interrupt, bus, memory, current, and power budgets, acquisition
rollback, default-off behavior, and a recorded bench acceptance result.

Current limiting plus bounded duty and duration reduce exposure; they do not
establish eye safety, and this lesson makes no eye-safety claim. The exact
qualified adjacent receiver is the future authoritative optical-activity test
point. A phone camera is at most a qualitative hint. Unknown-protocol replay,
unidentified emitters, access-control commands, high-power or long-range
transmission, and pyrotechnic or launcher control remain prohibited.
