# Lesson 050 — bounded logical homing

Status: **E0 synthetic replay; qualified inputs, powered motion, and bench
acceptance open**

Energy class: **E0 copied-evidence policy**
Safety boundary: **memory-only signed-step and stop intent; no sensor endpoint,
coil driver, motor, or moving mechanism**

`BoundedHomingPolicy` converts copied home and independent-stop evidence into
one bounded semantic step request at a time:

`qualified evidence + explicit command + explicit time → logical step/stop intent`

At E0, the policy owns no pin, timer, interrupt, bus, sensor, driver, motor,
Lesson 047 child, coil pattern, supply, callback, heap allocation, or physical
position. “Home,” “position,” and “movement” on this page describe a
deterministic software fixture only.

## Position starts unknown

Initialization establishes neither a physical origin nor a remembered
position. It begins in `PositionUnknown`, asserts stop intent, and publishes
no step request. A caller must explicitly request homing before the policy can
establish session-local logical zero.

The configuration fixes:

- inclusive negative and positive logical travel bounds around zero;
- a home search direction of exactly `-1` or `+1`;
- independent release and search step limits;
- independent release and search duration limits;
- a nonzero step cadence; and
- evidence freshness and input-skew limits.

The home coordinate is exactly zero. Invalid, unrepresentable, half-range, or
zero-duration configuration is rejected before the policy starts. Reset,
shutdown, restart, source fault, or interrupted travel makes position unknown
again. No state survives power loss.

## Release first, then acquire an edge

Homing uses a qualified transition rather than trusting an active level:

1. If home is active when the request begins, the policy enters
   `SeekingHomeRelease` and requests bounded steps opposite the configured
   search direction.
2. A qualified release changes the phase to `SeekingHome`.
3. If home is initially inactive, the policy starts directly in
   `SeekingHome`.
4. The first qualified inactive-to-active edge in the search direction
   establishes logical position zero and increments a nonzero home epoch.

The initial sample, release, and acquisition edge must retain the same source
kind, source ID, configuration revision, and nonzero qualification epoch. A
repeated active level is not an edge. An unqualified inactive value is not a
release. Changing provenance or missing the edge cannot be repaired by
silently accepting a later level.

Release and search are bounded separately by both issued logical steps and
elapsed time. The exact configured boundary is accepted; the next frame
fails. Exhausting release produces `HomeStuckActive`. Exhausting search
without a qualified edge produces `HomeNotFound`. Neither outcome retries
automatically.

## Preview, validate, commit

Each candidate update is side-effect free until committed. `preview()` checks
the complete copied frame, command, source history, time, arithmetic, and
state transition. `canCommit()` proves that the candidate belongs to the
unchanged policy generation. `commit()` then publishes the complete
`HomingSnapshot` atomically.

The snapshot makes the distinction between state and intent explicit:

| Field | Meaning at E0 |
|---|---|
| `logicalPosition` | volatile fixture coordinate |
| `positionKnown` | a qualified synthetic home edge established this session's zero |
| `stepRequested` | one semantic request is due |
| `requestedStepDirection` | signed direction `-1` or `+1`, not a coil vector |
| `stopIntent` | software intent to inhibit downstream motion |
| `homingSteps` | bounded requests issued during the active home attempt |
| `homeEpoch` | nonzero identity of the current session-local home result |

One accepted update emits at most one due step request. Delayed calls never
burst or skip inferred physical motion. The policy advances only its own
logical fixture coordinate when its request is accepted; it does not observe
an applied motor step.

Lesson 051 may jointly preflight this semantic request with its separately
owned Lesson 047 sequencer. Lesson 050 does not contain, call, or reinterpret
that sequencer.

## Bounded logical travel

Only a homed session accepts a target. A target must lie inside the inclusive
configured bounds. Zero-distance commands complete without a step. Otherwise,
the policy emits one signed request per due accepted frame until the logical
fixture reaches the target.

Checked subtraction precedes addition at every signed boundary. The immutable
`excursionBounds()` result covers zero, the largest possible release
excursion, and the largest possible search excursion without narrowing until
the range is proven representable.

These checks prove bounded integer behavior. They do not prove that a shaft
turned, a mechanism reached a location, a switch changed electrically, or a
motor stopped.

## Independent stop and fault behavior

A separately admissible, qualified active stop dominates every ordinary
request, evidence fault, due step, home edge, final step, and target. It
clears the step request and asserts stop intent in the same commit, even when
unrelated home or command fields are malformed.

If stop interrupts homing or logical travel, the policy enters `Stopped` and
forgets position. A stop while idle in `Homed` retains the known logical
coordinate because no motion was in progress. After release, an explicit new
home request can recover from `Stopped`.

A malformed stop channel cannot be trusted as inactive, so complete frame
rejection wins. Other admitted source or evidence failures enter `Fault`,
assert stop intent, and invalidate the home epoch. Fault recovery requires
shutdown and initialization; no timeout or later sample silently clears it.

`shutdown()` is idempotent and publishes an inert, unknown-position software
state. Software stop intent is not an emergency stop, motor-power removal, or
electrical safe-state proof.

## Deterministic replay

The canonical compile-only Mega sketch records phase, known-position,
signed-step request, stop intent, fault, and home epoch in fixed result cells.
Its predictions cover release-first homing, initially inactive homing,
missing or stuck home, bounded travel, interruption, restart, and recovery.

Executable host tests inspect the same public snapshot:

| Replay stage | Predict | Observe in executable host tests | Interpret |
|---|---|---|---|
| initialize | unknown position and asserted stop intent | `PositionUnknown`, `positionKnown == false`, no step request | no origin was invented |
| active start | bounded release request opposite search | `SeekingHomeRelease` and signed request | an active level was not mistaken for an edge |
| qualified release | search begins | `SeekingHome` and retained provenance | release and acquisition remain distinct |
| qualified acquisition edge | logical zero becomes known | `Homed`, position `0`, nonzero home epoch | synthetic edge established this session's fixture origin |
| target travel | at most one signed request per due frame | position, cadence, and signed request fields | bounded logical intent progressed without a motion claim |
| active stop | request clears and stop intent asserts | `Stopped`, no step request, asserted stop intent | independent stop dominated in software |
| interrupted restart | position is unknown | cleared epoch and `positionKnown == false` | stale logical position was not restored |

Compilation proves that the API, fixture, and named result schema fit the Mega
package. It does not execute the fixture or prove that a powered board
populated the result cells. A powered debugger, qualified home adapter, or
energized indicator is later evidence, not E0 evidence.

## Resource and evidence gates

The E0 implementation uses fixed-size state, bounded per-call work, and no
dynamic allocation. It claims zero pins, ADC channels, timers, interrupts,
buses, resource-registry entries, supplies, sensors, drivers, coils, or
physical current paths. `BoundedHomingPolicy` measures **126 bytes** against
its **112-byte target** and **128-byte hard ceiling**; `HomingPreview` measures
**92 bytes**. The canonical Mega sketch measures **8,272 bytes flash** and
**574 bytes static SRAM**, inside its respective **16,384-byte** and
**1,024-byte** ceilings. Its conservative stack/ISR gate reserves at least
**448 bytes** and leaves more than the required **768 bytes** after globals
and that allowance.

E1 may add separately qualified copied-home and copied-stop producers plus a
qualified non-motion observation path. It still cannot claim physical home or
energize a motor.

E2 requires an exact identified motor and driver, reviewed clamp topology,
separately switchable current-limited actuator supply, measured current and
thermal limits, mechanical restraint, independent stop and power removal,
and a recorded physical homing acceptance result. Only that evidence can
support a physical-position claim.
