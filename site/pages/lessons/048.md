# Lesson 048 — transactional kinetic light sculpture

Status: **E0 synthetic replay; powered inputs, indicators, driver, motion, and
bench acceptance open**

Energy class: **E0 copied-evidence project**
Safety boundary: **memory-only light and coil intent; no powered endpoint or
moving mechanism**

`KineticLightSculpture` composes the copied interaction evidence from Lesson
046 with the bounded logical sequence from Lesson 047:

`touch + direction + independent stop → authorize → sequence → mirror intent`

At E0, the sculpture owns two pure policies by value and publishes one
replayable `SculptureSnapshot`. It owns no pin, ADC channel, timer, interrupt,
bus, endpoint, input device, shift register, LED, driver, supply, motor, stop
switch, position sensor, callback, or heap allocation.

## One authorization, one selector, one stop

Touch is the sole authorization for one fixed motif. Direction does not grant
authority; it selects the motif associated with an authorized touch:

- the qualified touch event supplies explicit operator authorization;
- the direction record selects one of eight fixed motifs and its logical
  direction; and
- a separately qualified stop record can inhibit the entire project without
  passing through the touch policy.

This is bounded software authorization, not authentication, an interlock, or
an emergency-stop claim. The independent stop input has higher precedence
than the touch authorization and direction selection, but firmware cannot
remove physical motor power.

## One-frame staged authorization

A newly committed touch event does not start motion in the same frame. It
creates a one-entry `pendingAuthorization` record containing the originating
frame, contact and directional source identities, both source sequences,
direction, status, and `Pending` disposition.

An ordinary healthy next accepted strictly forward frame resolves the record
as `Accepted` and starts its motif. A complete-endpoint bound rejects it as
`BoundRejected`. Stop, fault, command expiry, or shutdown may instead resolve
it as `Inhibited`. The result moves to `lastTerminalAuthorization` with
exactly one outcome:

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Accepted` | both policy candidates committed and the bounded motif started |
| `Inhibited` | stop, source fault, expiry, shutdown, or reset prevented motion |
| `BoundRejected` | the complete logical endpoint would exceed the configured travel range |

An exact repeated frame cannot authorize twice. A frame that resolves an
older authorization cannot enqueue a replacement. A later attempt first needs
contact release, requalification, and a fresh touch event. Initialization and
power-loss restart clear both authorization records and restore no position
claim. A terminal record remains visible on its resolving frame and clears on
the next accepted forward frame unless that frame replaces it with a newer
terminal result.

## Transaction before publication

When stop is inactive, the project validates the complete frame, previews both
child policies, and checks both opaque candidates before committing either.
Malformed frame identity, chronology, provenance, source shape, time, or skew
is rejected without changing either child or the published project snapshot.

After preflight, interaction commits first. A healthy result permits the
already-preflighted sequence commit. An admitted stale, stuck-active, or
producer-fault result is retained as evidence while motion is cancelled
all-off. No intermediate child state, speculative light value, or speculative
coil value is published.

The project therefore distinguishes:

- **structural rejection:** no policy or project mutation;
- **admitted source fault:** interaction evidence is retained, motion is
  inhibited, and `Fault` latches;
- **active stop:** motion is inhibited immediately and `Stopped` remains
  distinct from `Fault`; and
- **ordinary progress:** at most one due logical step is committed per call.

## Stop, fault, and restart

Simultaneous events follow fixed precedence:

1. valid active independent stop;
2. malformed stop evidence, which fails closed;
3. unrelated structural invalidity;
4. admitted child or source fault;
5. command expiry;
6. completion;
7. a fresh touch queued for the next frame;
8. direction change; and
9. ordinary progress.

A valid stop dominates malformed touch or directional evidence, completion,
and source faults. It enters `Stopped`, clears coil and mirror intent, and
terminalizes any pending authorization as `Inhibited`. Stop release alone
cannot restart motion; after release, a newly qualified touch must stage a
fresh authorization before the project can run again.

A malformed stop channel cannot be trusted as inactive. It enters `Fault`,
uses the last valid project time to stop the sequence when available, and
publishes all-off intent. A recognized producer fault retains its exact
status and source attribution. Fault recovery requires reinitialization.

`shutdown()` is idempotent. It clears every live intent, inhibits a pending
authorization, resets both policies, and publishes `Inactive`. It is a
software-state transition, not proof that a driver de-energized or that load
power was removed.

## Fixed motifs and logical travel

The project accepts no arbitrary script or caller-supplied coil pattern.
Direction selects this fixed table:

| Direction | Logical direction | Steps | Interval |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| North | forward | 8 | 120 ms |
| NorthEast | forward | 12 | 100 ms |
| East | forward | 16 | 80 ms |
| SouthEast | forward | 12 | 100 ms |
| South | reverse | 8 | 120 ms |
| SouthWest | reverse | 12 | 100 ms |
| West | reverse | 16 | 80 ms |
| NorthWest | reverse | 12 | 100 ms |

The canonical sequence allows 60–250 ms intervals, a 2,000 ms maximum command
age, and logical position bounded to **[-256, 256]**. The complete motif
endpoint must fit before motion is admitted. An over-bound motif consumes no
step, no travel, and no motif count; it raises `travelLimit` and requires a
new opposite-direction authorization.

Logical position is only a volatile software count from initialization. It is
not a measured shaft angle, physical location, limit switch, or homing result.
The project does not persist position across reset or power loss.

## Exact light mirror

`SculptureLightIntent::shiftRegisterBits` is a project-local memory value. Its
low four bits exactly equal the committed
`StepperSequenceSnapshot::coilIntent`, and its high four bits remain zero.
Stop, fault, reset, and shutdown publish zero in both fields.

The mirror never displays a speculative preview. Equality proves that the two
published software-intent records agree; it does not prove GPIO state, coil
current, shaft movement, direction, torque, a physical stop, or an electrical
safe state. A future E1 renderer may present this value only through already
qualified, resistor-limited indicators.

## Deterministic replay

The canonical compile-only sketch defines a fixed fixture and named
`EvidenceCell` results for staged authorization, one due logical frame, stop
dominance, restart, producer fault, and another restart. Compilation proves
that fixture and result schema fit the Mega package; because the E0 sketch is
not executed on a powered board, compilation does not claim that those memory
cells were populated.

Executable deterministic host tests drive the same public project API and
inspect `SculptureSnapshot` values directly:

| Replay stage | Predict | Observe in executable host tests | Interpret |
|---|---|---|---|
| initialize | `Ready`, all motion intent zero | snapshot phase and zero motion/light fields | pure policies started without acquiring hardware |
| touch qualification | authorization becomes pending | snapshot pending flag and complete authorization provenance | touch and direction were retained without starting motion |
| next forward frame | authorization becomes `Accepted` | snapshot terminal disposition and motif count | one pending record was consumed exactly once |
| first deadline | one logical frame advances | snapshot coil intent `0001` and equal light-intent field | committed motion and mirror records agree |
| active stop | `Stopped`, both intent fields zero | snapshot stop status, phase, coil intent, and light intent | stop dominated and inhibited further software motion |
| producer failure | latched `Fault`, all intent zero | snapshot project, interaction, and motion statuses | fault attribution remained visible while motion failed off |
| restart | `Ready`, no restored authorization or position | snapshot cleared records and all-off fields | volatile state was not presented as recovery or homing |

The compile-only fixture's `predictionPass` expression defines expected
operation status, phase, project status, pending state, terminal disposition,
coil intent, and exact mirror equality for every row. The executable host
tests provide the actual E0 snapshot evidence. The named result cells are the
sketch's non-Serial memory observation design for a later qualified execution;
they are not claimed as observed merely because the sketch compiled. Serial
output is optional supporting evidence.

Mega compilation is packaging evidence only. A powered debugger would be
powered hardware work, not E0 evidence, and no debugger observation is claimed
here.

## Resource proof

The implementation uses fixed storage, constant-size motif data, bounded
per-call work, and no dynamic allocation. At E0 it claims zero pins, ADC
channels, timers, interrupts, buses, resource-registry entries, or physical
current paths. A single update advances at most one logical sequence frame;
catch-up requires explicit repeated calls.

The AVR `KineticLightSculpture` object measures **502 bytes**, below its
**512-byte target** and **640-byte hard ceiling**.
With Arduino AVR core 1.8.8 and AVR GCC 7.3.0, the complete canonical Mega
sketch measures **22,216 bytes of flash** against its **28,672-byte ceiling**
and **1,470 bytes of static SRAM** against its **2,048-byte ceiling**. These
figures prove packaging and static size only. The compiler-derived conservative
stack-plus-ISR bound is **687 bytes**, leaving **6,035 bytes** after static
storage. This is static compiler/link evidence, not a measured runtime
high-water mark. None of these figures proves electrical timing, endpoint
behavior, or physical motion.

## Open E1 and E2 gates

No powered wiring table, pin assignment, driver topology, or formal electrical
schematic is published at E0.

E1 remains open for exact input-module identity; input voltage, polarity,
pull, noise, and qualification; a powered Mega; a qualified shift-register or
LED mirror; independent stop indication; pin and current budgets; acquisition
rollback; visible all-off behavior; reset; and recorded bench acceptance.
Indicator output remains diagnostic and cannot become stop authority.

E2 remains open for exact motor and driver identity; winding voltage and
topology; clamp path; separate current-limited load supply; common-reference
review; measured coil and aggregate current; driver dissipation; thermal and
cadence limits; a stable lightweight fixture; guarded travel; physical
direction and step evidence; fault injection; logic-unplugged behavior;
de-energized startup and shutdown; and independent physical power removal.

The likely future 28BYJ-48 and ULN2003 combination is not qualified by a kit
name. Until its delivered specimens and electrical evidence pass those gates,
the lesson makes no powered-driver, movement, direction, position, holding,
stall, thermal, or safe-state claim.
