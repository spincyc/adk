# Lesson 061 — qualifying copied resistive-probe observations

Status: **E0 deterministic copied-evidence policy; exact conductive-probe
fixture and bench acceptance open**

Energy class: **E0 copied values only**
Safety boundary: **no ADC channel, GPIO pin, switched excitation, probe,
sensor supply, liquid, display, clock, or powered circuit**

`ResistiveProbeObservationPolicy` qualifies one complete, copied acquisition
cycle:

`sample identity + energized/off readings + excitation evidence + supplied time → one attributed observation`

The policy does not energize a probe, read an ADC, identify a liquid, estimate
depth, or prove that a physical circuit switched off. Its E0 evidence consists
of synthetic samples and named memory result cells.

## Why the off reading matters

A conductive probe can corrode when it is energized continuously. A single
energized ADC value also cannot reveal whether excitation remained on, a line
was backfed, or a circuit failed to discharge. Every Lesson 061 sample
therefore carries both:

- `energizedRaw`, captured during the named acquisition cycle; and
- `dischargedRaw`, captured after the producer reports excitation off.

The sample also reports the cycle duration, excitation-on duration, and an
explicit `excitationObservedOffAfterSample` flag. At E0 these are copied test
facts, not measurements made by ADK. A later E1a fixture must independently
measure the complete excitation waveform, current, corrosion duty, discharge,
and powered-off behavior.

## Fixed public contract

`ResistiveProbeSample` copies the nonzero source ID, configuration revision,
calibration revision, producer sequence, observation time, both raw values,
excitation timing, off evidence, and producer `Status`.

`ResistiveProbeConfig` fixes the declared ADC maximum, dry and wet references,
the disconnected and discharged limits, ordered damp and wet thresholds,
maximum age, maximum on-time, and maximum per-cycle duty. The dry and wet
references must differ; either calibration slope is valid.

`ResistiveProbeObservation` returns the complete copied sample plus:

- `normalizedPermille`, monotonic from the dry reference toward the wet
  reference and bounded to `0..1000`;
- `observedCycleDutyPermille`, computed from this sample only;
- `quality`, one of `Unqualified`, `Dry`, `Damp`, `Wet`, `Saturated`,
  `Disconnected`, `ExcitationFault`, `Stale`, or `ProducerFault`;
- the supplied-time age; and
- a structural/lifecycle `Status`.

The policy surface is deliberately small:

```cpp
explicit ResistiveProbeObservationPolicy (
    const ResistiveProbeConfig& config) noexcept;

Status initialize  () noexcept;
void   reset       () noexcept;
Status update      (
    TimePoint now, const ResistiveProbeSample& sample) noexcept;

ResistiveProbeObservation snapshot    () const noexcept;
bool                      initialized () const noexcept;
```

Construction is inert. The policy copies its fixed configuration and latest
observation, retains no caller pointer, allocates no heap memory, invokes no
callback, owns no endpoint, and reads no clock. It is non-copyable and
non-movable.

## Exact classification boundaries

Configuration is rejected unless the ADC maximum is nonzero; both calibration
references and every raw limit are inside the declared ADC domain; dry and wet
references differ; thresholds are ordered inside `0..1000`; cycle constraints
are nonzero and modular-time limits remain below the half range.

For each update, the policy applies this precedence:

1. validate identity, configuration/calibration provenance, sequence, supplied
   time, raw domain, and excitation-cycle structure;
2. retain producer failure as `ProducerFault`;
3. report `ExcitationFault` for missing off evidence, excessive discharged
   reading, excessive on-time, or excessive per-cycle duty;
4. report `Saturated` for exact ADC full scale, or `Disconnected` only when
   both energized and discharged readings are at or below
   `disconnectedMaximum`;
5. report `Stale` when age exceeds `maximumAge`; and
6. normalize along the configured calibration slope and classify the ordered
   dry, damp, and wet ranges.

The maximum-age boundary is inclusive: age equal to `maximumAge` remains
current; the next representable age is stale. The on-time and duty maxima are
also inclusive. The duty calculation widens before evaluating
`excitationOnTime * 1000 / cycleTime`.

Full-scale energized input is always `Saturated`; it is never silently called
“very wet.” The disconnected rule is intentionally narrow and exact. Values
outside those two predicates follow the calibrated slope even when real-world
contamination, geometry, or ionic content could make that interpretation
misleading. This policy is a bounded observation classifier, not a liquid
identity or depth instrument.

## Ordering and supplied time

`TimePoint now` is the only clock. Equal timestamps are idempotent only for a
byte-identical duplicate. Changed duplicates, backward time, exact
half-range ambiguity, sequence regression, and changed
source/configuration/calibration identity reject atomically. Producer sequence
zero is always invalid. Accepting `UINT32_MAX` latches sequence exhaustion:
every later update, including a duplicate, returns `CapacityExceeded` without
changing the snapshot. Only explicit `reset()` clears that latch and begins a
new volatile sequence epoch.

A rejected update leaves the prior snapshot byte-for-byte intact. `reset()`
discards the qualified observation and ordering history while preserving the
validated configuration and initialized lifecycle. `initialize()` is
idempotent after that lifecycle has started. The canonical replay records
separate initialization and reset/unqualified cells, then accepts a fresh
sequence after reset.

## Deterministic fixture tour

Use a declared 10-bit ADC domain (`adcMaximum = 1023`) with distinct dry and
wet references, ordered thresholds, and a nonzero cycle. Predict the complete
observation before each call:

| Fixture | Predict | Observe | Interpret |
|---|---|---|---|
| dry reference | normalized value at the dry endpoint | normalized, quality, age, and copied source cells | calibration direction is explicit |
| damp/wet threshold neighbors | values immediately below, at, and above each threshold | normalized and quality cells | boundary ownership has no gap |
| reversed calibration | dry reference greater than wet reference | the same dry-to-wet quality progression | either monotonic ADC slope is supported |
| two low readings | energized and discharged values at the disconnected limit | `Disconnected` and exact copied readings | only the fixed two-low predicate fired |
| rail input | energized value exactly `adcMaximum` | `Saturated` | rail saturation did not become wet |
| bad discharge | missing off flag or discharged value above its maximum | `ExcitationFault` | off evidence dominates liquid class |
| duty edge | on-time/duty at the configured maximum, then one unit above | accepted edge, then `ExcitationFault` | this is one-cycle evidence only |
| age edge | age at `maximumAge`, then one unit older | current quality, then `Stale` | freshness uses an inclusive maximum |
| producer failure | structurally valid sample with failed producer status | `ProducerFault` with source attribution | producer health cannot become `Healthy` |
| duplicate/regression | replay the same sample, alter it, then regress sequence/time | idempotence followed by atomic rejection | ordering history remains stable |

Host tests additionally cover every ADC endpoint, contamination-drift ramps,
open/stuck/backfeed cases, exact threshold neighbors, time rollover,
half-range ambiguity, reset, latched sequence exhaustion, lifecycle misuse,
and byte-identical full replay.

## Compile-only Mega replay

The canonical Mega 2560 sketch is an inert replay. It creates fixed synthetic
samples, supplies time explicitly, and copies each result into named volatile
cells. Those cells are the non-Serial E0 observation path:

| Stage | Predict | Observe | Interpret |
|---:|---|---|---|
| initialize | no qualified sample | lifecycle and `Unqualified` cells | policy is ready; no resource was acquired |
| dry-to-wet sweep | monotonic normalized results | raw, normalized, quality, and provenance cells | copied calibration behavior matches the host trace |
| discharge fault | liquid class suppressed | off-evidence and `ExcitationFault` cells | excitation safety evidence has precedence |
| stale sample | prior source remains attributable | age and `Stale` cells | invalid freshness cannot look healthy |
| initialize, then reset | both snapshots are unqualified; initialization remains valid after reset | separate initialization and reset cells, initialization status, and the following fresh-sequence result | volatile policy history cleared; no physical switch claim |

Predict the named cells before each call and compare them with deterministic
host output. Mega compilation is packaging evidence only. Do not upload or run
the E0 sketch on a powered Mega, connect a probe, place electronics near
liquid, or infer any physical safe state from the result cells.

## Resource gates

Lesson 061 passes the exact ordinary-sketch and isolated no-LTO AVR
measurements:

| Measurement | Result | Gate |
|---|---:|---:|
| ordinary Mega sketch flash | 5,662 B | 8 KiB target; 12 KiB hard limit |
| ordinary Mega sketch static SRAM | 624 B | 768 B target; 1,024 B hard limit |
| isolated no-LTO probe flash | 3,566 B | measured |
| isolated no-LTO probe static SRAM | 169 B | measured |
| conservative synchronous stack | 123 B | 320 B target; 448 B hard limit |
| policy object | 69 B | 192 B target; 256 B hard limit |
| caller-owned input/output buffers | 27 B + 37 B = 64 B | measured separately |
| residual museum-case SRAM | 7,772 B | after static SRAM, stack, and the specified 128 B ISR reserve |

The gate also checks the public enum and structure ABI, ownership, retained
return stack edges, and toolchain fingerprint. The ordinary sketch and
isolated no-LTO probe are different measurement boundaries, so their flash and
static-SRAM figures must not be added or described as one aggregate. These
compiler measurements are not runtime stack, electrical, corrosion,
liquid-detection, or physical-acceptance evidence.

E0 hardware-resource acquisition evidence is exactly “no resources
requested.” Its separate safe-state evidence is the reset/unqualified memory
snapshot. Neither statement proves a physical probe is de-energized.

## E1a remains open

No wiring table or formal schematic is authorized at E0. E1a may begin only
after an exact conductive-probe specimen and its adapter are identified. Its
evidence must cover:

- module markings, PCB revision, pin order, supply and output limits;
- switched-excitation topology, ADC reference, source impedance, settling,
  powered-off behavior, backfeed, and complete discharge;
- peak and average current, excitation waveform, cycle duty, long-run
  corrosion behavior, and drying procedure;
- spill containment, separation of liquid from the Mega and host, and
  physical power removal;
- calibration repeatability across contamination, geometry, and the intended
  liquid range; and
- an independent non-Serial observation path plus separate acquisition,
  rollback, shutdown, and bench-acceptance records.

Until that evidence exists, Lesson 061 makes no claim about liquid identity,
depth, leak absence, preservation suitability, corrosion lifetime, or the
authorized Water Level product family’s electrical behavior.
