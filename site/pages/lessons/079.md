# Lesson 079 — bounded low-side driver policy

Status: **Host-verified E0 copied-evidence policy; exact powered specimens and
bench acceptance remain open**

Energy class: **E0 immutable declarations, supplied time, checked integer
arithmetic, and logical drive intent only**
Safety boundary: **no GPIO endpoint, pin, resource claim, clock, timer,
supply, transistor, diode, load, storage, or powered fixture**

`BoundedLowSideDriverPolicy` is the software boundary for asking one
declared active-high bare-NPN low-side configuration whether a bounded logical
request fits its declared evidence:

`immutable descriptor + attributable request + supplied time → all-off or bounded logical intent`

The result is intent data. It does not switch a transistor, source base
current, energize a load, verify a flyback diode, observe a pin, or establish
that a physical specimen matches the declaration.

## One topology, not a generic driver

The policy represents exactly one active-high bare-NPN low-side topology.
There is no polarity option and no PNP, MOSFET, Darlington, high-side,
H-bridge, relay-module, or generic three-pin-module interpretation.
`outputLevelHigh` means the logical level a future, separately qualified
endpoint would be asked to produce. It is not an observed voltage.

`LowSideDriverDescriptor` binds the complete declaration:

- schema, specimen-family, specimen, electrical-evidence, source-packet, and
  configuration identities and revisions;
- resistive-indicator or inert-inductive energy classification;
- flyback requirement, declaration, exact diode identity and revision,
  orientation, return, repetitive-reverse voltage, and forward-current
  rating; and
- supply, reserved-supply, fixture, device, GPIO-source, forced-gain,
  resistor, tolerance, voltage, active-duration, and duty-window ceilings.

An inductive declaration requires complete, nonzero flyback fields. A
resistive-indicator declaration requires canonical absence: flyback is not
required, the declaration is absent, and every diode field is zero. E0 checks
internal consistency and conservative arithmetic. It neither authenticates
the parts nor observes their placement or behavior.

The descriptor identity is CRC-32/ISO-HDLC over the literal eight-byte domain
tag `ADK79DSC` followed by every descriptor field in canonical little-endian
order. The polynomial is `0x04C11DB7`, input and output are reflected, and
the initial and final XOR are `0xFFFFFFFF`. A zero digest is valid; it is not
a missing-value sentinel. The digest detects accidental correlation failure
but never replaces fieldwise identity or physical evidence.

## Exact current-budget arithmetic

All current values are integer microamps, voltages are integer millivolts,
resistance is integer ohms, and time uses supplied `TimePoint` and `Duration`.
There is no floating point:

```text
ceilDiv(n, d) = n / d + (n % d != 0), only when d != 0
availableSupplyUa = supplyLimitUa - reservedSupplyUa
availableDeviceUa = min(deviceContinuousUa, fixtureLoadCeilingUa)
requestedBaseUa = ceilDiv(requestedLoadUa * forcedGainDenominator,
                          forcedGainNumerator)
baseResistorDropMv = max(0, logicHighMinimumMv - baseEmitterMaximumMv)
baseResistorLimitedUa = floor(baseResistorDropMv * 1000 /
                              baseResistanceOhms)
admittedBaseUa = min(baseResistorLimitedUa, gpioSourceCeilingUa)
admittedLoadUa = min(requestedLoadUa, availableSupplyUa,
                     availableDeviceUa,
                     floor(admittedBaseUa * forcedGainNumerator /
                           forcedGainDenominator))
```

Every multiplication is widened to `uint64_t` and checked. Subtraction checks
its minuend first. Divisors and required ceilings must be nonzero, and every
final value must fit `uint32_t`. Required base current rounds up; supported
load current rounds down. Overflow, underflow, division by zero, an
unrepresentable result, or a request above any binding ceiling rejects
without mutation. Saturation is not evidence.

An off request has canonical zero load and duration. `logicalActive == false`
always produces all-off intent. An active request has a nonzero duration no
greater than `maximumActiveDuration`.

## Lifecycle, provenance, and precedence

Construction is inert. `initialize()` validates the immutable descriptor and
publishes canonical off intent. `beginSession()` requires initialized/off
state and fresh nonzero session and run identities. Requests and controls bind
lifecycle generation, session, run, step, source and source-configuration
revision, request or control identity, sequence, supplied observation time,
and complete producer status.

The first matching condition controls:

| Priority | Condition | API result | Published domain intent |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | malformed enum, descriptor, arithmetic, duration, or value | `InvalidConfiguration` or `InvalidArgument` | no mutation |
| 2 | lifecycle, identity, revision, correlation, session, run, step, or request mismatch | `InvalidArgument` | no mutation |
| 3 | cancellation or shutdown | `Ok` | canonical all-off cancellation or shutdown |
| 4 | attributable producer or all-off failure | `Ok` | off `Fault` with producer attribution |
| 5 | ineligible source, unsafe budget, or missing required flyback declaration | `Ok` | off `Rejected` with a named reason |
| 6 | sequence or timestamp discontinuity, expiry, or reservation-capacity exhaustion | `Ok` | off with the named reason |
| 7 | complete, current, correlated request | `Ok` | bounded active or canonical off intent |

API-invalid input never becomes a domain observation. A well-formed unsafe
request is attributable terminal evidence and returns `Status::Ok` with a
rejected intent. Every mutation stages a complete candidate; structural or
correlation failure leaves the object and caller output byte-for-byte
unchanged.

Cancellation takes a complete `LowSideControl`, not identifier-only input.
Confirmed off produces `Cancelled`. A producer failure that prevents
commanding or confirming off produces `Fault`, preserves cancellation as the
cause, and keeps the producer status attributable. Reset and shutdown leave
published intent off; shutdown is idempotent.

## Expiry and bounded duty history

Admission sets `expiresAt = observedAt + requestedActiveDuration` only after
rejecting wrap-unsafe or modular-half-range ambiguity. `update(now)` uses
caller-supplied time and expires at equality. Replaying a request never
extends its deadline, and a new active request cannot replace an outstanding
active request without explicit cancellation or a canonical off request.

A fixed eight-entry ring retains admitted active intervals
`[start, expiresAt)`. Entries ending at or before the rolling-window start are
pruned. Remaining overlap plus the candidate duration is admitted only when:

```text
reservedTicks * 1000 <= dutyWindow.ticks() * maximumDutyPermille
```

Equality passes; one tick or permille over rejects. Early off or cancellation
shortens the current interval while retaining its used portion in history. A
ninth still-overlapping reservation does not overwrite history: it publishes
off `Rejected/CapacityExceeded` for that attributable request.

## Public API

The policy is noncopyable, nonmovable, heap-free, callback-free,
clock-free, and hardware-free:

```cpp
struct BoundedLowSideDriverPolicy
{
    explicit BoundedLowSideDriverPolicy (
        const LowSideDriverDescriptor& descriptor) noexcept;

    Status initialize  () noexcept;
    void   shutdown    () noexcept;
    bool   initialized () const noexcept;

    Status beginSession (uint32_t sessionId, uint32_t runId) noexcept;
    Status apply        (const LowSideDriveRequest& request,
                         LowSideDriveIntent& intent) noexcept;
    Status update       (TimePoint now,
                         LowSideDriveIntent& intent) noexcept;
    Status cancel       (const LowSideControl& control,
                         LowSideDriveIntent& intent) noexcept;
    Status reset        () noexcept;

    LowSideDriveIntent snapshot () const noexcept;
};

Status validateLowSideDriverDescriptor (
    const LowSideDriverDescriptor& descriptor) noexcept;
uint32_t lowSideDriverDescriptorIdentityDigest (
    const LowSideDriverDescriptor& descriptor) noexcept;
```

`LowSideDriveIntent` carries the lifecycle and complete descriptor/session/
request correlation, state and reason, logical-active and logical-level
intent, required and admitted current values, deadline, and producer status.
Rejected, cancelled, fault, reset, and shutdown results always carry all-off
intent.

## Deterministic replay

The E0 experiment will use synthetic descriptor, request, and control values
only. No circuit is connected and no physical fixture is authorized.

| Predict | Observe in copied result cells | Interpret |
|---|---|---|
| a request exactly on every current ceiling is admitted | required base, admitted base/load, deadline, state, reason | conservative integer equality passed |
| one binding ceiling is exceeded | off, `Rejected`, and the named budget reason | a typed rejection did not become a GPIO action |
| an inductive declaration omits or reverses required flyback facts | descriptor rejection or off `FlybackMissing` | copied declarations did not authenticate a diode |
| an active interval reaches its deadline | off `Expired` at equality | supplied time removed authority without an owned clock |
| cancellation confirms off | off `Cancelled` with full control provenance | identifier-only cancellation was not accepted |
| cancellation collides with an off failure | off `Fault` retaining cancellation cause and producer status | stop dominance did not hide failed safe return |
| the ninth reservation still overlaps | prior eight reservations unchanged; new result off `CapacityExceeded` | bounded storage did not silently discard duty history |
| malformed or mismatched input is applied | caller output and policy state remain byte-identical | API failure did not become domain evidence |

The compile-only Mega replay exposes named volatile source, drive,
observation, safe-state, and overall replay cells as its non-Serial E0
observation path. It runs the production policy through initialization, a
bounded active request, canonical off restoration, an over-budget rejection,
and shutdown. Resource-acquisition evidence is separately zero endpoint and
registry calls. Safe-state evidence is the published all-off intent after
restoration and shutdown. Neither observation proves that a physical pin is
low, a supply is removed, or a load is safe.

## Verification and open acceptance

The host matrix exercises descriptor encodings and cross-fields, canonical
flyback absence and presence, descriptor-digest goldens and one-field
mutations, current arithmetic and overflow boundaries, active/off/fault
intent, expiry, cancellation, sequence and time discontinuity, duty-window
equality and excess, reservation capacity, replay, reset, shutdown, and
nonmutation. The canonical Mega sketch compiles and executes the production
policy over copied values only.

The ordinary Mega 2560 build measures 11,560 B flash and 561 B static SRAM.
The isolated no-LTO resource probe measures:

| Metric | Measured | Target | Hard limit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| flash | 12,974 B | 10 KiB | 14 KiB |
| static SRAM | 561 B | 768 B | 1,024 B |
| synchronous stack | 320 B | 320 B | 448 B |
| policy object | 240 B | 192 B | 256 B |
| descriptor value | 96 B | 96 B | 128 B |
| residual Mega 2560 SRAM after static, stack, and 128 B reserve | 7,183 B | at least 4,096 B | at least 3,072 B |

Flash is 2,584 B above its target and 1,512 B below its hard limit. The
policy object is 48 B above its target and 16 B below its hard limit. Both
target misses received independent fingerprint-bound review; no hard limit
misses, and synchronous stack meets its target exactly. The resource result is
bound to compiler, core, flags, sources, fixture, and call graph fingerprint
`4972bd9733d608d52ac81bfb1320e61088b10c3e59910f3be8c439b5838d33e7`.
These measurements establish compiler/linker and storage cost only, not
electrical behavior or physical acceptance.

E1 is strictly unpowered identity and source work: both-face photographs,
markings, primary sources, traced pin order and populated topology, passive
continuity/resistance/diode-mode observations, energy classification, and one
reviewed specimen descriptor. E1 applies no voltage and creates no powered
observation.

The transistor-switched fixture is E2b. It requires the exact transistor,
diode, load, supply, and fixture; a formal authoritative schematic; base and
load current limits; independent current limiting and power removal; named
test points; rollback; stored-energy disposition; separate
resource-acquisition and safe-state evidence; and a recorded bench acceptance
result. PN2222 and S8050 are distinct variants whose names do not select a
pinout or rating.

Mains, relay contacts, motors, fans, solenoids, heaters, lasers, ignition,
launchers, pyrotechnics, combustion, gas exposure, medical or physiological
use, body contact, food use, unknown emitters, unattended operation, and
arbitrary three-pin-module support remain excluded.
