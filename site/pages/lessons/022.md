# The memory trail

Prerequisites: **Lessons 001–021**

Status: **host verified; physical adapters absent**
Energy: **E1 — USB-powered Mega and one resistor-limited external LED**

Turn a potentiometer and watch its value become a visible record. D13 pulses
for roughly 0.1–0.9 seconds as the knob moves across the lesson scale. Every
eighth successful record produces a 1.2-second flourish after the in-memory
storage owner restarts.

This is deliberately a model-first lesson. There is no RTC module, SD card,
soil probe, or physical I2C/SPI peripheral. The authorized Elegoo set contains
no removable-storage specimen. The sketch generates no bus waveform and makes
no claim about a physical clock, media durability, or power-loss retention.
An RTC can be considered only after its exact revision, markings, voltage,
address, and primary datasheet are known.

## Wire the complete physical circuit

| Name | Mega pin | Exact connection |
|---|---:|---|
| TP-M | A0 / D54 | 10 kΩ potentiometer center wiper |
| Potentiometer rails | 5 V, GND | one outer leg to each rail |
| Activity | D13 | built-in LED only |
| Fault | D12 | D12 → 1 kΩ → LED anode; cathode → GND |
| Model-only labels | D20/D21, D49–D52 | leave physically empty |

Unplug USB before moving wires. Confirm the potentiometer's center wiper from
its documentation or with a meter. Stop for heat, smell, smoke, repeated USB
resets, or a part that disagrees with the wiring description.

## Four quick stages

1. Upload the canonical sketch. D13 begins pulsing after the first 1.5 seconds;
   D12 stays dark.
2. Turn the knob. A raw count near 200 gives about 0.1 seconds, 500 gives about
   0.5 seconds, and 800 gives about 0.9 seconds.
3. Count eight records. The eighth earns a 1.2-second pulse after the fixed
   in-memory owner restarts and preserves its committed size.
4. At the fixed two-minute deadline, both indicators go inactive for 250 ms,
   resources release, and the sketch waits for Reset. Input cannot extend the
   run.

The D13 reward depends on a valid A0 sample, valid simulated clock state,
recording-only I2C and SPI calls, append, and successful `sync()`. It supports
that deterministic software path only.

## Model contract

The 11-byte record contains raw simulated Unix seconds, explicit clock state,
raw ADC count, lesson-scale permille, sample state, and one synthetic metadata
token. `append()` stages bytes; `sync()` advances the committed prefix.
Restarting the owner discards an unsynchronized suffix. Reset or power removal
clears this RAM-backed model.

The automated repository tests cover address and length boundaries, resource
conflicts, injected transport failures, chip-select restoration, every partial
append offset, failed sync, capacity exhaustion, clock states, restart,
shutdown, destruction, and replay. They remain behind the scenes; the learner
advances by making the visible stages work.
