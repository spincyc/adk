# Lesson 080 — small-indicator semantics policy

Status: **Host-verified E0 copied-evidence semantics; exact powered specimens
and bench acceptance remain open**

Energy class: **E0 immutable declarations, copied drive intent, copied
observation evidence, and semantic result data only**
Safety boundary: **no GPIO endpoint, pin, resource claim, clock, scheduler,
waveform, supply, indicator, sound, light, display, storage, or powered
fixture**

`SmallIndicatorSemanticsPolicy` asks whether one attributable copied semantic
request and one independently attributable copied observation agree with one
copied Lesson 079 drive intent and one closed indicator declaration:

`immutable descriptor + drive intent + semantic request + observation + supplied time → semantic result`

An `Accepted` result means only that those copied values satisfy the policy.
It does not establish that a buzzer sounded, an LED emitted light, a supply
was present, a safe state was reached electrically, or a physical specimen
matches the declaration.

## Five exact semantic families

The policy is deliberately closed to five kinds. It is not an adapter for an
arbitrary three-pin module, and a kind name does not infer pin count, pinout,
populated parts, polarity, voltage, current, or physical behavior.

| Kind | Autonomy | Required safe-state meaning | Active high | Exact declared channels | Resistor | Driver |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| `ActiveBuzzer` | `FollowsDrive` | `DriveInactive` | yes | Sound | no | yes |
| `ActiveBuzzer` | `AutonomousWhileEnabled` | `UnpoweredRequired` | yes | Sound | no | yes |
| `TrafficLight` | `FollowsDrive` | `DriveInactive` | yes | Red, Amber, Green | yes | no |
| `DualColorLed` | `FollowsDrive` | `DriveInactive` | yes | Red, Green | yes | no |
| `AutoFlashLed` | `AutonomousWhileEnabled` | `UnpoweredRequired` | yes | Red, Green, Blue | yes | no |
| `VoltageIndicator` | `ObservationOnly` | `HighImpedanceRequired` | yes | Voltage | yes | no |

Every unlisted combination is invalid. The populated-resistor and
populated-driver columns are exact declarations, not minimum requirements.
E0 checks their internal consistency but does not inspect a board or fill in
missing evidence.

`VoltageIndicator` is observation-only and never receives drive authority.
`AutoFlashLed` is autonomous while enabled. `ActiveBuzzer` is valid only in
the two rows shown. Adding another kind requires a new design and evidence
pass rather than a permissive fallback.

kind, channel marks, autonomy, and safe state; a crossed-out generic three-pin
card is outside the set.](../downloads/assets/080-closed-kinds-pencil.png)

## Request-owned channel selection

The six named channel bits are `Red`, `Amber`, `Green`, `Blue`, `Sound`, and
`Voltage`. Bits 6 and 7 are reserved and must remain zero. Selected, observed,
and semantic masks must be subsets of the descriptor's exact declared mask.

`SmallIndicatorSemanticRequest` owns the expected semantic selection. The
observation being judged cannot author its own expectation:

- active follows-drive requests select exactly one declared channel; off
  requests select zero;
- an active Buzzer selects `Sound`, while Traffic Light and Dual Color LED
  requests select one exact color;
- autonomous-while-enabled requests select the descriptor's complete mask as
  enable authority, while observations may report one nonzero subset; and
- observation-only requests select zero and never gain drive authority.

| Observation | Canonical mask rules |
|---|---|
| `NotObserved` | observed mask is zero |
| `Inactive` | observed mask is zero |
| `Active` | observed mask is one nonzero declared subset |
| `Alternating` | observed mask is one nonzero declared subset |
| `Fault` | observed mask is zero and producer status is non-OK |

The table describes structurally attributable rows, not already-successful
semantics. It intentionally leaves disagreement reachable. Within a
well-formed row, a follows-drive mask different from the request selection
becomes `ObservationMismatch`; transition evidence for follows-drive or
observation-only becomes `UnexpectedAutonomy`; and autonomous active or
alternating evidence without transition evidence becomes
`AutonomousWaveformMissing`. A non-fault row with non-OK producer status, a
fault row with OK status, or an outside/reserved mask is malformed and
returns `InvalidArgument` without mutation.

For the single-channel Buzzer and Voltage rows, `copiedLevelHigh` must agree
with whether the observed mask is nonzero; disagreement is a reachable
`PolarityMismatch`, not erased during structural validation. Multichannel
rows use their masks as the sole color authority and a true copied level is
likewise a polarity disagreement. These rules preserve bounded color meaning
without inventing per-channel endpoints.

## Identity, time, and failure precedence

The immutable descriptor binds the indicator specimen, electrical-evidence
revision, source packet, configuration, and the expected Lesson 079 driver
specimen and policy fields. It also binds the complete Lesson 079 descriptor
identity digest. Partial field agreement cannot hide a different
specimen-family reference, source packet, topology, or budget declaration.
A zero digest is valid and does not mean “missing.”

The semantic request and observation both repeat lifecycle, session, run,
step, and request correlation, but they are independently attributable
streams. Each carries its own nonzero source identity, source-configuration
revision, sequence, supplied time, and complete producer status. Their
sources latch independently and may differ. Control belongs to the
request/control producer; it cannot borrow observation-source authority.

`policySequence` is the common apply/cancel stream.
`requestSequence` and `observationSequence` are separate contiguous producer
streams, all beginning at one. The copied Lesson 079 intent has no source,
sequence, or observation-time field; correlation uses its descriptor digest,
explicit driver identities, lifecycle/session/run/step, and request ID.

The first matching condition controls:

| Priority | Condition | API result | Published domain result |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | malformed enum, descriptor, mask, or canonical field | `InvalidConfiguration` or `InvalidArgument` | no mutation |
| 2 | lifecycle, identity, revision, session, run, step, source, or correlation mismatch | `InvalidArgument` | no mutation |
| 3 | supplied cancel or shutdown | `Ok` | canonical cancelled/shutdown result, unless off confirmation failed |
| 4 | request, drive, or observation producer failure, in that order | `Ok` | `ProducerFault` with the selected status |
| 5 | mapped Lesson 079 terminal state or source ineligible | `Ok` | exact named terminal result |
| 6 | policy/request/observation discontinuity, chronology failure, or stale evidence | `Ok` | named rejection |
| 7 | warm-up, settling, or required observation incomplete | `Ok` | `Incomplete` |
| 8 | polarity, safe-state, autonomy, or expected/observed mismatch | `Ok` | named semantic rejection |
| 9 | complete matching copied evidence | `Ok` | `Accepted` |

Chronology is `session start ≤ request time ≤ observation time ≤ now`, with
each comparison forward-or-equal inside the modular half range. Warm-up is
computed from session start to `now`; settling from request to observation;
freshness from observation to `now`. No copied “satisfied” Boolean can waive
those calculations. Age equal to `maximumObservationAge` is current; one tick
later is stale. Known-zero warm-up or settling requires no delay.

The only accepted Lesson 079 pairs are exact: off/none, off/expired,
requested/none, the named rejected reasons, cancelled/cancelled,
fault/producer-fault, fault/cancelled, and shutdown/none. Each maps to its
specific Lesson 080 disposition and reason before observation semantics.
Unlisted state/reason/activity/level/status combinations are malformed.
Plausible observation evidence cannot erase a drive terminal result.

An immediate duplicate of the complete apply tuple, including `now`, is
idempotent only when every field is identical. Changed duplicates reject
without mutation. Gaps, regressions, exhaustion, or timestamp discontinuity
publish a terminal rejection, cache that rejected tuple only for identical
replay, and do not advance accepted anchors; reset is the recovery boundary.
Structural and correlation failures leave both policy state and caller output
byte-for-byte unchanged.

## Public API

The policy is stateful, heap-free, callback-free, clock-free, and
hardware-free:

```cpp
struct SmallIndicatorSemanticsPolicy
{
    explicit SmallIndicatorSemanticsPolicy (
        const SmallIndicatorDescriptor& descriptor) noexcept;

    Status initialize  () noexcept;
    void   shutdown    () noexcept;
    bool   initialized () const noexcept;

    Status beginSession (uint32_t sessionId, uint32_t runId,
                         TimePoint startedAt) noexcept;
    Status apply        (const LowSideDriveIntent& drive,
                         const SmallIndicatorSemanticRequest& request,
                         const SmallIndicatorObservation& observation,
                         TimePoint now,
                         SmallIndicatorSemanticResult& result) noexcept;
    Status cancel       (const SmallIndicatorControl& control,
                         SmallIndicatorSemanticResult& result) noexcept;
    Status reset        () noexcept;

    SmallIndicatorSemanticResult snapshot () const noexcept;
};

Status validateSmallIndicatorDescriptor (
    const SmallIndicatorDescriptor& descriptor) noexcept;
```

Construction copies one immutable descriptor. `initialize()` validates it;
`beginSession()` establishes one fresh session per lifecycle generation and
the supplied start time. `apply()` interprets copied values only. Cancellation takes complete
control provenance rather than an identifier alone. Confirmed off produces a
cancelled result; a producer or endpoint failure that prevents confirming off
remains a fault and cannot be hidden by the cancellation request. Reset
advances lifecycle generation and clears all three stream anchors. Shutdown
is terminal and idempotent; reinitialization advances the generation before
another session.

## Predict, observe, interpret

The E0 experiment uses a fictional synthetic descriptor and copied values.
No circuit is connected and no physical fixture is authorized.

| Predict | Observe in the returned semantic result | Interpret |
|---|---|---|
| a request selects Green and copied follows-drive evidence reports Green | `Accepted`, Green semantic mask | expectation came from request authority, not the observation |
| a request selects Green and copied evidence reports Red | `ObservationMismatch` | well-formed disagreement remained classifiable |
| an autonomous indicator changes among declared channels | acceptance only with copied transition evidence | the policy observed a declaration, not a waveform |
| a voltage indicator is given active drive authority | named rejection | observation-only semantics stayed observation-only |
| computed warm-up or settling is incomplete | named `Incomplete` result | copied assertions could not waive supplied chronology |
| age is exactly the freshness limit | current result | equality is the documented boundary |
| age is one tick beyond the limit | stale result | supplied time, not an owned clock, removed authority |
| safe-state evidence disagrees | `SafeStateMismatch` | admission did not substitute for a safe return |
| cancellation collides with failed off confirmation | producer fault with cancellation cause retained | stop dominance did not hide failure |
| malformed or mismatched copied input is applied | output and policy remain byte-identical | API failure did not become domain evidence |

The canonical Mega example is a compile-only replay. Its non-Serial E0
observation path is caller-inspectable semantic presentation intent, not an
LED, buzzer, display, or electrical test point. Serial may explain the values
but cannot turn them into physical evidence.

## Verification and resources

The deterministic host matrix covers all descriptor cross-products, five
explicit fixtures, request-selected colors, every observation row and
reachable semantic mutation, every canonical and malformed Lesson 079 pair,
digest and identity correlation, independent request/observation source
latching, all three sequence streams, freshness and modular-time boundaries,
duplicates and terminal discontinuities, computed warm-up and settling,
polarity, autonomy, safe state, both canonical cancel tuples, producer-fault
precedence, lifecycle, restart, reset, shutdown, canaries, and atomic
nonmutation.

The ordinary Mega 2560 build measures 10,866 B flash and 520 B static SRAM.
The isolated no-LTO resource probe measures:

| flash | 14,910 B | 14 KiB | 20 KiB |
| static SRAM | 683 B | 1,024 B | 1,536 B |

Flash is 574 B above its target and 5,570 B below its hard limit; that target
miss received independent fingerprint-bound review. Static SRAM, synchronous
stack, policy object, evidence value, and every hard gate pass. The resource
result is bound to compiler, core, flags, sources, fixture, and call graph
fingerprint
`b0e4ee371afa072c9e2b1c0afabffe3c2a4a31f1f613da72225b2958ebfd4e7f`.
These measurements establish compiler/linker and storage cost only, not
electrical behavior or physical acceptance.

## Open physical acceptance

E1 remains strictly unpowered: exact specimen identity, both-face
photographs, markings, primary sources, traced pin order and populated
topology, passive continuity/resistance/diode-mode observations, energy
classification, and a reviewed descriptor. E1 applies no voltage and records
no powered behavior.

Any energized direct or independently current-limited indicator fixture is
E2a. A transistor-switched, externally powered, or inductive fixture is E2b.
Each requires an exact specimen and adapter, authoritative schematic or
reviewed trace, resource and rail map, limits, independent current limiting
and power removal, rollback, named test points, non-Serial observation, safe
state, and a recorded bench result. The indicator under qualification cannot
also be the independent observation proving itself.

E2c presentation or persistence is separately selected and accepted. No E0
semantic result, E1 record, compile, test, resource probe, HTML page, or PDF
grants powered support.

Mains, relay contacts, motors, fans, solenoids, heaters, lasers, ignition,
launchers, pyrotechnics, combustion, gas exposure, medical or physiological
use, body contact, food use, unknown emitters, unattended operation, and
arbitrary three-pin-module support remain excluded.
