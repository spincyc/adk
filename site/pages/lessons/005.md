# Passive piezo sounder

Status: **host verified; hardware experimental**<br>
Energy class: **E1** — low-voltage Mega GPIO and one passive piezo

`PiezoSounder` owns its output pin and Timer2. It never blocks: `play()` records
an explicit start time and duration, while the application advances it with
`update()`.

```cpp
sounder.play
(
    440,
    adk::Duration  (250),
    adk::TimePoint (millis ())
);

sounder.update (adk::TimePoint (millis ()));
```

Use the specified passive piezo and series resistor. A dynamic speaker is not a
substitute. The PDF contains the literal D6 circuit, timer-conflict predictions,
fake-hardware traces, safe fault exercises, and a complete physical acceptance
card. The finite canonical sketch gives resource acquisition an independent
250 ms TP-ACQ/D13 pulse, waits through a 750 ms dark separator, produces one
440 Hz cue, and reaches controlled shutdown within 2 seconds.

The physical card names TP-SND/D6 and requires high-impedance waveform evidence
for primary behavior plus a measured 10 kΩ weak-pull method for D6's released
safe state. TP-ACQ/D13 is not treated as an abstract LED: the card requires the
exact retained Mega revision's factory-LED branch, resistor/LED/buffer identity,
branch-current calculation, and inclusion in all GPIO current budgets. Its
post-shutdown high-impedance check uses a separately calculated weak-bias
fixture whose driven and released voltage ranges must be safe and
non-overlapping after the factory load, leakage, instrument impedance, and
tolerances are included. It separately records exact board/transducer identity,
supply and instrument identity, reset, stop conditions, power removal, and
predict/observe/interpret evidence. Physical work remains pending until a
retained specimen and bench observations fill and review that card.
