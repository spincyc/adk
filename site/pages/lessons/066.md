# Lesson 066 — thermal-gradient mapper

Status: **Host verified E0 deterministic project composition; exact probes,
powered single-wire adapter, presentation, recording media, thermal accuracy,
and bench acceptance open**

Energy class: **E0 copied observations, copied controls, and volatile intent
only**
Safety boundary: **no sensor, GPIO pin, bus, pull-up, supply rail, clock, LCD,
LED, recorder, RTC, storage medium, heated object, immersion, or powered
fixture**

`ThermalGradientMapper` projects one complete copied Lesson 065 snapshot into
an explicitly configured spatial order:

`qualified probe set + copied page/record controls + supplied time → conservative adjacent gradients + inert pages + volatile record intent`

The mapper does not poll probes, sort them by temperature, operate a display,
read a clock, or write a file. Its output is deterministic policy evidence,
not proof of a physical temperature gradient or a durable record.

## Spatial order is configuration

Lesson 065 always supplies four configured ROM identities. The mapper selects
an ordered subset of two through four of those identities. That list is the
spatial model for the entire initialized lifecycle: for example, left to
right across a tabletop or near to far from a room-temperature object.

Every selected ROM must occur exactly once in the Lesson 065 source
configuration. A configured but unselected source remains valid and is
ignored by the mapper. A duplicate, zero, or foreign selected identity makes
the mapper configuration invalid.

Spatial order controls:

- mapped slot numbers;
- adjacent pairs;
- probe and gradient page order;
- minimum and maximum tie breaking; and
- record-image layout.

Search order, scratchpad arrival order, ROM numeric order, current
temperature, and display-page selection never reorder the slots. Reverse the
copied discovery order and the mapped result remains byte-for-byte ordered by
configuration.

## Public lifecycle and result

Construction stores fixed configuration but validates no observation.
`initialize(now)` validates the configuration and publishes a qualifying,
inert intent. `reset(now)` advances lifecycle identity, clears accepted-frame,
control, page, and record-sequence history, and returns to qualification.
`shutdown()` publishes inactive presentation intent and emits no record.
Repeated shutdown is idempotent.

```cpp
explicit ThermalGradientMapper (
    const ThermalMapperConfig& config) noexcept;

Status initialize (TimePoint now) noexcept;
Status reset      (TimePoint now) noexcept;

Status update (
    const ThermalMapperEnvelope& envelope,
    ThermalMapperResult&         result) noexcept;

Status shutdown () noexcept;

Status snapshot (
    ThermalGradientIntent& intent) const noexcept;

bool initialized () const noexcept;
```

The mapper is noncopyable and nonmovable. It owns no heap memory, callback,
clock, child pointer, caller buffer, or hardware resource. `update()` fills
one fixed caller-owned `ThermalMapperResult` synchronously and retains no
reference to it. A structural rejection leaves both mapper state and the
caller's result bytes unchanged.

`ThermalMapperResult` contains:

- the complete current `ThermalGradientIntent`;
- at most one fixed `ThermalMapperRecordImage`;
- a `hasRecord` flag; and
- complete status.

Lifecycle misuse and malformed identity, enum, ordering, or time evidence use
`Status`. Structurally valid but unhealthy probe evidence remains visible as
domain state.

## Conservative interval arithmetic

The mapper preserves Lesson 065's signed raw-sixteenth-degree-Celsius
representation. It neither introduces floating point nor silently rounds a
resolution-dependent reading into false precision.

Each mapped probe supplies a closed interval:

```text
[lowerRawSixteenths, upperRawSixteenths]
```

For adjacent configured slots `left` and `right`, widened signed arithmetic
derives:

```text
gradient lower = right.lower - left.upper
gradient upper = right.upper - left.lower
```

Classification uses the complete interval:

| Quality | Required interval |
|---|---|
| `Rising` | lower is at or above the positive meaningful-gradient threshold |
| `Falling` | upper is at or below the negative threshold |
| `Flat` | the complete interval lies strictly inside both thresholds |
| `Indeterminate` | the interval straddles either classification threshold |
| `Fault` | either incident probe is not qualified current evidence |

Threshold equality is intentional: touching the positive threshold is
`Rising`, and touching the negative threshold is `Falling`. An interval that
straddles a threshold is never rounded into a direction.

Overall health is fault dominant. Any mapped probe or pair fault produces
`Fault`. Otherwise, an indeterminate pair or a mixture of rising and falling
pairs produces `Disagreement`; one or more consistently directional pairs
produce `Gradient`; and all-flat pairs produce `Stable`. `Qualifying` exists
only before the first accepted frame.

Minimum and maximum evidence retains the supporting ROM identities and tie
masks. Equal extrema use the lowest configured mapped slot only as the
deterministic primary attribution; the complete tie remains visible.

## Faults cannot masquerade as temperatures

A required mapped slot may be current, pending, stale, missing, disappeared,
CRC-failed, resolution-mismatched, reset-default, implausible-step, or
transport-faulted according to Lesson 065. Only qualified `Current` evidence
participates in gradient arithmetic.

An unhealthy mapped slot:

- sets its bit in the overall fault mask;
- faults every adjacent pair incident to that slot;
- presents an explicit fault token rather than a numeric-only value; and
- remains attributable to its configured ROM.

Healthy nonincident pages may retain their own valid evidence, but every page
also carries the complete overall fault mask. A bad middle probe therefore
faults both neighboring gradient pages without rewriting an independent
healthy endpoint. No missing or stale probe becomes zero, “very cold,” flat,
or cached healthy evidence.

Structural errors reject atomically. A valid child fault commits a visible
fault result. This distinction lets callers separate malformed input from a
well-attributed unhealthy observation.

## Inclusive freshness

The mapper receives its own supplied `TimePoint now`; it never reads an
implicit clock. Every Lesson 065 slot carries an inclusive `freshThrough`
bound. Equality remains fresh. The next representable millisecond is stale.

This makes page and record controls useful without inventing a new
temperature observation. A fresh control edge may act on an unchanged
accepted snapshot at `freshThrough`. It may not refresh, estimate, or extend
that snapshot. Future time, backward time, and exact-half-range ambiguity
reject atomically under the same modular ordering rules.

Published probe and record age is recomputed from each probe's original
`observedAt` at the mapper's supplied `now`. The copied Lesson 065 `age` must
already agree with that chronology when the snapshot is admitted; the mapper
coherence-checks it rather than blindly forwarding it. This preserves both
the child witness and the mapper-time meaning of every displayed or recorded
age.

## Pages are inert presentation intent

Page order is fixed:

1. one overall page;
2. one page for each mapped probe, in configured spatial order; and
3. one page for each adjacent pair.

A fresh `nextEdge` advances exactly one page and wraps from the final page to
the overall page. A held control or byte-identical replay is an accepted
state no-op. A stale control, changed duplicate, regression, or producer
fault rejects atomically and therefore cannot move the page.

The intent identifies the selected page kind and index, ordered probe and
pair evidence, overall health and fault mask, active-slot LED mask, and
whether an LCD should show identity plus age-or-fault content. Its selectors
are mutually exclusive and canonical:

| Page kind | `selectedSlot` | `selectedGradient` |
|---|---:|---:|
| `Overall` | zero | zero |
| `Probe` | selected mapped slot | zero |
| `AdjacentGradient` | zero | selected adjacent pair |

These are semantic values in memory. E0 owns no LCD, LED, refresh schedule,
pin, timer, bus, or electrical safe state.

Page selection occurs after thermal classification. Advancing a page cannot
change a gradient, suppress a fault, or alter record content.

## Copied controls

`ThermalMapperControl` carries owner and source identity, configuration
revision, nonzero sequence, observation time, independent `nextEdge` and
`recordEdge` flags, and producer status. Configuration fixes the expected
control owner, source, revision, and maximum age.

Fresh control sequences advance in modular order. A byte-identical duplicate
is idempotent; a changed duplicate or regression rejects. When both edges are
fresh in one update, page advancement happens first and the record captures
the current accepted thermal snapshot. Page selection is presentation state,
not part of the record witness.

Controls are not thermal evidence. A fresh control can select or record only
the current accepted snapshot, and no control field can qualify a bad probe.

## Volatile caller-owned record intent

A fresh `recordEdge` may produce exactly one versioned
`ThermalMapperRecordImage` in the caller's result. Record creation belongs to
the edge, not to a temperature change, so a fresh edge may capture an
unchanged but still-fresh accepted frame. A new probe frame without a record
edge changes presentation but emits no record.

The record preserves:

- mapper owner, lifecycle, configuration, and nonzero record sequence;
- complete record-edge owner/source/revision/sequence/time;
- Lesson 065 set source, configuration, cycle, and observation identity;
- all four configured Lesson 065 source ROMs, including permitted unmapped
  identities;
- every mapped ROM, interval, resolution, quality, age, conversion/read
  attribution, and status in configured order;
- every adjacent interval, classification, and fault mask;
- overall health and complete fault mask; and
- a deterministic witness digest.

Full fields are authoritative; the digest never substitutes for fieldwise
comparison. The digest covers the complete witness, including all four source
ROMs rather than only the mapped subset. Mapper record-sequence exhaustion
faults before wrapping to zero; the copied control sequence must remain
nonzero and modularly ordered.

A byte-identical replay creates no new record and does not advance the record
sequence. Because `update()` synchronously republishes caller-owned output,
an exact whole-envelope replay of an edge may return `hasRecord` with the same
byte-identical record image again. That is replay of one immutable intent, not
a second record.

This record is volatile intent only. The mapper does not open a file, consult
an RTC, accept a storage receipt, retry, coalesce, recover a torn write,
manage capacity or wear, or assert that any caller preserved the bytes.
Reset and shutdown invalidate the mapper's volatile record and page history
without erasing caller-owned bytes or claiming that a stored record was
erased. Adding acknowledgement, retry, or durability would be a separate
architectural boundary.

## Deterministic replay

The canonical Mega 2560 replay is compile-only and uses copied Lesson 065
snapshots and copied controls. Named volatile result cells are its non-Serial
observation path.

| Stage | Predict | Observe | Interpret |
|---:|---|---|---|
| initialize | qualifying state and inactive outputs | lifecycle, health, and inactive cells | E0 policy initialized; no endpoint was acquired |
| reverse input | configured mapped order is unchanged | ordered ROM and slot cells | discovery order has no spatial authority |
| flat interval | every adjacent interval remains inside the threshold | pair bounds and quality cells | uncertainty was included before classification |
| threshold equality | the pair is directional | exact bounds and direction cell | equality follows the documented rule |
| middle fault | both incident pairs fault | probe, pair, and overall fault masks | invalid evidence did not become a temperature |
| freshness endpoint | unchanged frame remains admissible | `now`, `freshThrough`, and health cells | the inclusive endpoint is still fresh |
| next tick | the affected slot is stale | fault token and mask cells | controls did not extend child freshness |
| next + record | page advances, then one volatile image appears | page, record sequence, and digest cells | one copied edge created intent, not durability |
| reset | page and record history restart under a new lifecycle | lifecycle, page, and sequence cells | stale evidence cannot cross reset |
| shutdown | presentation is inert and no record is emitted | inactive and `hasRecord` cells | logical output stopped; no physical-dark claim |

Before each call, predict the complete slot order, interval classification,
page, masks, and record presence. Compare the named memory cells with the host
trace. Optional Serial output may summarize the same evidence, but it is not
the only proof.

Resource-acquisition evidence at E0 is “zero resources requested.” Safe-state
evidence is the separate inactive presentation intent. Neither observation
proves a powered display is dark or a sensor bus is released.

## Verification scope

The required host matrix covers the two-, three-, and four-slot
configurations; rejection of smaller or larger mappings; input-order
permutations; duplicate, foreign, and missing identities; every Lesson 065
quality; widened arithmetic extremes; threshold boundaries; extrema ties;
fault collisions; page wrap; freshness and modular rollover; control replay
and ordering; caller-buffer canaries; record golden images; reset; shutdown;
and byte-stable replay. The complete matrix and terminal host gates pass.

The canonical Mega replay measures 16,662 bytes flash and 2,210 bytes static
SRAM. Exact no-LTO evidence measures 18,822 bytes flash, 2,210 bytes static
SRAM, 855 bytes conservative synchronous stack, a 448-byte mapper, a 202-byte
envelope, a 377-byte result, and a 229-byte record. The reviewed recurring
Lessons 064–066 composition is 1,943 bytes; phase-local storage is 579 bytes,
lifetime storage is 2,522 bytes, and 4,999 bytes of SRAM remain after the
specified reserves. Six target misses were independently reviewed below their
hard limits.

The terminal evidence fingerprint is
`0e5c142fd8c0970ff529f46b1994b6446fdd2f8c0d00c879d05931f1a79742f7`.

Compilation and host replay establish deterministic E0 behavior only. They do
not establish electrical single-wire timing, probe identity, sensor accuracy,
thermal response, display visibility, record persistence, or physical
safe-state behavior.

## What remains open

Powered work remains separately gated. E1 qualification must identify the
exact DS18B20 specimens, package and pin order, supply mode, pull-up topology,
bus length and capacitance, voltage, current, rise time, conversion timing,
disappearance behavior, and complete shared-fixture resource map. Exact
presentation and optional RTC/media adapters require their own ownership,
rollback, failure, and acceptance evidence.

Any physical experiment also needs an authoritative schematic, unpowered
wiring inspection, independent resource-acquisition observation, physical
safe-state observation, and recorded prediction/observation/interpretation.

This lesson authorizes no immersion, unknown waterproof assembly, hot surface,
heater, flame, ignition, medical or food measurement, preservation claim, or
unattended thermal source. A future qualified tabletop exercise may use only
room-temperature and hand-warmed inert objects within documented specimen and
material limits.
