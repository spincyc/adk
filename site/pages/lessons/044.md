# Lesson 044 — board-frame orientation and presentation intent

Status: **E0 synthetic replay; powered presentation and bench acceptance open**

Energy class: **E0 copied-value policies**
Safety boundary: **memory-only orientation and light/tone intent**

Lesson 044 turns one current copied inertial observation into a bounded
gravity-relative pitch/roll estimate, then turns that estimate into light and
tone intent:

`copied observation → board-frame estimate → bounded presentation intent`

Both policies are pure. They own no sensor, bus, clock, LED, sounder, pin,
timer, or callback. The caller supplies copied values, sensitivity, and the
diagnostic phase. The resulting light and tone records are desired
presentation, not hardware commands.

## Make the board frame explicit

`BoardFrame` names the sensor axes that point right, forward, and up in the
finished tabletop device. Each entry is a signed X, Y, or Z axis. Initialization
accepts exactly the 24 distinct right-handed frames and rejects duplicate,
opposite-reuse, out-of-range, and left-handed mappings.

This separates board mounting from a sensor's register convention. For mapped
right, forward, and up acceleration components `R`, `F`, and `U`, the policy
uses:

```text
horizontal = isqrt(R*R + U*U)
pitch      = atan2(F, horizontal)
roll       = atan2(R, U)
```

Positive pitch means `Forward`; negative pitch means `Backward`. Positive roll
means `Right`; negative roll means `Left`. Pitch and roll are reported in
millidegrees.

## Validate the complete observation

`OrientationPolicy` validates the `InertialObservation` directly. A
`Current` label alone is insufficient. Numeric use also requires:

- an OK observation and sample status;
- `sample.dataReady` and `latestDataReady` both true;
- nonzero configuration, calibration, and freshness-contract revisions;
- a nonzero maximum-age contract;
- no saturation flag; and
- current, nonsaturated quality.

Malformed, stale, saturated, not-ready, or failed evidence produces an
`Invalid` estimate and does not leak angles. A valid vector outside the
configured gravity band, or an angular-rate component beyond the configured
stationarity limit, produces `Unsteady`.

This is deliberate defense in depth: Lesson 044 consumes the complete copied
record rather than trusting one derived field after the underlying record may
have changed.

## Fixed-point angle contract

The implementation uses widened integer squares, a floor integer square root,
and a normalized 16-step integer CORDIC. Tests compare its output with a
high-precision `atan2` oracle across axes, quadrants, small ratios, boundary
values, and deterministic randomized vectors. The measured error bound is at
most 4 millidegrees over the accepted test domain.

Classification reserves that full error bound:

- `Level` requires both absolute angles plus 4 millidegrees to remain within
  the level threshold.
- `BeyondPresentationRange` begins whenever either absolute angle plus
  4 millidegrees exceeds the configured presentation range.
- Values in the level-threshold error band resolve to `Tilted`.
- Pitch and roll magnitudes within 8 millidegrees use the documented
  pitch-first tie.

Outside those tie cases, the larger absolute angle chooses the direction.
This makes classification independent of enum values, color-channel order,
pins, and insertion order.

## Orientation-to-intent table

| Orientation quality | Direction and light intent | Tone intent |
|---|---|---|
| `Level` | `None`; configured level light | disabled |
| `Tilted` | dominant cardinal direction; configured directional light scaled to intensity | one bounded pulse only when direction changes |
| `Unsteady` | `None`; caller-selected complete phase A or B light | disabled |
| `BeyondPresentationRange` | `None`; configured fault-safe light | disabled |
| `Invalid` or failed status | `None`; configured invalid/fault light | disabled |

For a tilted estimate, the unbounded intensity is:

```text
abs(dominant angle) * sensitivityPermille
------------------------------------------------
          fullScaleAngleMilliDegrees
```

The numerator is widened before multiplication. The result is clamped between
the configured minimum and maximum intensity, so values beyond
`fullScaleAngleMilliDegrees` saturate instead of wrapping.
`fullScaleAngleMilliDegrees` is an explicit presentation scale, not a sensor
range or an accuracy claim. Changing it changes how quickly the visual intent
reaches full intensity.

Sensitivity is supplied to each `update()` and must be 1--1000 permille. It is
not constructor state, so the next lesson can re-render a frozen estimate at a
new sensitivity without reconstructing the policy. The caller also supplies
the unsteady diagnostic phase; no hidden clock toggles the result.

## Synthetic replay

The canonical compile-only sketch publishes a fixed intent self-test, then
replays level, four cardinal tilts, diagonal dominance, unsteady,
beyond-range, and invalid frames. It copies complete estimates and
presentation intents into named memory result cells.

| Fixture | Predict | Observe in the host harness | Interpret |
|---|---|---|---|
| level gravity | `Level`, no direction, no tone | zero pitch/roll and configured level light | gravity-relative synthetic level |
| positive forward component | `Tilted` and `Forward` | bounded directional intensity | board-frame mapping, not navigation |
| positive right component | `Tilted` and `Right` | direction-change tone on the transition only | an intent record, not a driven sounder |
| pitch-dominant diagonal | `Forward` | pitch magnitude exceeds roll magnitude | ordinary dominant-axis selection |
| excessive angular rate | `Unsteady` | configured phase B light and no tone | stationarity guard rejected the pose; this replay supplies phase B |
| angle beyond presentation range | fault-safe light and no tone | `BeyondPresentationRange` | bounded presentation stopped |
| stale or not-ready observation | invalid light and no tone | `Invalid` with no numeric angle leakage | freshness is checked directly |

Reset clears the prior-direction history, disables tone intent, and restores a
canonical result. Initialization and reset acquire or release no physical
resource; those memory results do not prove electrical acquisition or a safe
hardware state.

The named result cells are the non-Serial E0 observation path. Mega compilation
is packaging evidence only. Do not execute the sketch on a powered Mega or use
a powered hardware debugger as evidence for this lesson.

## Limits and open gates

Acceleration supplies a gravity-relative estimate only while the fixture is
stationary enough for the configured experiment. Angular rate is used only as
a stationarity guard. It is not integrated or fused.

This lesson makes no yaw, heading, position, velocity, displacement, gesture,
fall, stabilization, attitude-and-heading, or navigation claim. It publishes
no wiring, I2C address, register transaction, endpoint assignment, or formal
schematic.

MPU-6050 and QMI8658 adapters remain separate future work. Each requires the
exact delivered specimen, carrier and chip markings, authoritative electrical
and register sources, voltage and level qualification, mounting evidence, and
independent bench acceptance. Physical RGB, mono-LED, sounder, resistor,
timer, pin, current, and safe-state evidence remains E1 work.
