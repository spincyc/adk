# Lesson 068 — configured inertial-record qualification

Status: **Host verified E0 deterministic component; exact powered adapters
and bench acceptance open**

Energy class: **E0 copied normalized records and caller-owned qualification
evidence only**
Safety boundary: **no sensor, GPIO pin, interrupt, I2C bus, address probe,
supply rail, clock, calibration store, display, recorder, storage medium,
moving object, or powered fixture**

`InertialRecordQualificationPolicy` evaluates a bounded stationary trace from
one explicitly configured Lesson 067 record source:

`copied source-frame records + one configured proper rotation + supplied time → attributable terminal qualification evidence`

The policy does not discover a sensor, choose between sources, vote, switch,
retry after a bad sample, or operate a device. A successful synthetic trace
shows that copied records satisfy a deterministic qualification contract. It
does not prove that an MPU6050, QMI8658, carrier board, mounting, conversion
scale, or physical calibration is correct.

## One source means one complete domain

Configuration identifies exactly one source and interpretation. The domain
includes source kind, model and nonzero identity; source configuration and
calibration revisions; declared acceleration and angular-rate ranges; accepted
record-schema and normalization-contract revisions; one qualification-contract
revision; and one source-to-qualification-frame mapping.

Every incoming record must match that complete domain. A familiar model label,
matching checksum, or plausible vector cannot substitute for one mismatched
field. Changing a source, range, revision, or mapping requires an explicit
reset and a separately configured policy. “Source selection” therefore means
configuration before an attempt, never probing two addresses and accepting
whichever answers.

Copied records carrying physical-family labels remain fixtures at E0. They
exercise mismatch and unsupported-source seams without establishing that a
physical adapter exists.

## One shared proper-rotation rule

`SourceAxisMapping` maps source X/Y/Z into the qualification frame. Its three
`SignedAxis` selections must use each source axis exactly once and form a
right-handed rotation. Exactly 24 of the 216 possible signed-axis triples are
valid.

The same mapping is applied to acceleration and angular rate. Duplicate axes,
left-handed reflections, and a mapping that would negate `INT32_MIN` reject
atomically. The implementation may neither invoke signed overflow nor
saturate an unrepresentable value into apparently valid evidence.

Lesson 044 already uses the same mathematical rule for its presentation
frame. Before Lesson 068 is implemented, that rule is factored into one
foundational helper while the published Lesson 044 names, angle behavior,
thresholds, and results remain unchanged. Qualification does not acquire
pitch/roll presentation as a side effect.

## A bounded terminal attempt

Construction copies configuration but acquires nothing. After
`initialize()`, a caller begins one qualification attempt with a nonzero
attempt identity and supplies records one at a time with an explicit `now`.
The configured stationary window contains 2 through 32 accepted records.

The lifecycle is deliberately terminal:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Idle` | no active attempt has authority |
| `Collecting` | a valid attempt has accepted fewer than the required records |
| `Qualified` | the complete required window passed |
| rejected reason | the first attributable unhealthy record ended the attempt |

At exactly `N-1` accepted records, the result remains collecting. The `N`th
accepted record qualifies the attempt. A semantic failure at any position
ends it immediately. Later records cannot repair, replace, or outvote that
evidence; explicit reset and a new attempt are required.

Reset clears the window, ordering history, terminal evidence, and attempt
authority while retaining validated configuration. Shutdown leaves an
inactive, unqualified result. Lifecycle generations never wrap silently to
zero.

## Every record must be stationary

Mapping happens before stationary checks. Each mapped acceleration axis must
lie inside its configured inclusive interval around the expected stationary
gravity component. Each mapped angular-rate axis must lie at or below its
inclusive positive and negative limit.

Every individual record must pass. A quiet average cannot conceal one moving,
saturated, stale, or faulted sample. Exact configured bounds pass; one unit
beyond fails. Sums, differences, absolute values, and means use checked widened
integer arithmetic so signed extremes cannot overflow or depend on compiler
behavior.

The policy retains running extrema and widened sums rather than storing all 32
records internally. Any reported mean or bias is descriptive evidence for
this attempt. It does not write calibration, alter later records, or establish
physical sensor accuracy.

## Time and order stay explicit

The component reads no clock. Each call supplies `now`, while each record
retains its original observation time and producer sequence.

Age at the configured maximum remains admissible; one tick older is stale.
An inter-record gap at the maximum passes; one tick larger fails. Future time,
backward time, and exact-half-range ambiguity are explicit failures under the
repository’s modular-time rules.

Sequences follow the same disciplined ordering. `UINT32_MAX` to `0` is the
valid one-step rollover. A byte-identical duplicate is an idempotent no-op: it
does not increase the count or extend the window. Changed content at the same
sequence, regression, a gap, or exact-half-range ambiguity ends the attempt
with attributable ordering evidence. Zero is an ordinary sequence value after
natural wrap, not a sentinel.

## Fault precedence is deterministic

Each update validates the complete candidate before changing a count, sum,
extremum, or retained result. The controlling order is:

1. lifecycle and attempt authority;
2. record structure, enums, status domain, schema, and normalization revision;
3. exact source identity, model, ranges, configuration, and calibration;
4. supplied-time chronology and age;
5. sequence ordering and duplicate identity;
6. time-gap and sequence continuity;
7. producer status;
8. data-ready state;
9. saturation;
10. mapping representability;
11. acceleration bounds;
12. angular-rate bounds; and
13. checked accumulation and window completion.

Malformed structure or invalid lifecycle use a non-`Ok` `Status` and leave
both policy state and caller output unchanged. A structurally valid source
mismatch, producer fault, not-ready record, saturation, stale record, ordering
fault, or stationary-bound failure is different: it becomes visible terminal
domain evidence. Such a semantic rejection may have operation status `Ok`
while carrying a specific non-success qualification reason.

A producer fault dominates not-ready and saturation because its payload is not
trusted. For an `Ok` producer, not-ready dominates saturation and numeric
checks. Saturation dominates stationary arithmetic. If acceleration and rate
both exceed bounds, acceleration supplies the scalar reason while the retained
record preserves the full collision.

## Evidence binds the decision

The result is one copied envelope, not a Boolean. It retains the configured
source and mapping; lifecycle generation and attempt identity; accepted
count; first and last record sequences and times; maximum observed age and
gap; mapped per-axis extrema and widened sums; terminal reason and operation
status; and the complete attributable terminal record.

On success, the envelope also contains the qualification-frame record derived
from the terminal accepted source-frame record. Lesson 069 consumes this
complete immutable envelope. It does not read separate mutable snapshots,
remap the source record, or combine a record from one attempt with
qualification from another.

A checksum or future digest may detect accidental corruption, but it cannot
replace fieldwise provenance comparison, authenticate a sensor, or prove
durability.

## Deterministic replay

The canonical Mega 2560 sketch is compile-only. It feeds one
stationary synthetic trace and one independently faulted trace, then expose
the copied qualification envelope through named volatile result cells as the
non-Serial observation path.

| Stage | Predict | Observe | Interpret |
|---:|---|---|---|
| begin | `Collecting`, count zero | attempt, generation, reason, and count | one explicit attempt acquired authority |
| stationary records | count advances once per new record | mapped vectors, extrema, sums, age, gap, and sequence | accepted evidence remained attributable |
| identical duplicate | no state change | unchanged count and envelope | replay did not extend the window |
| final accepted record | terminal `Qualified` | complete source domain, mapping, first/last evidence, and terminal record | the configured copied trace met the E0 contract |
| fault trace | first bad record terminalizes | precise reason, producer status, and offending provenance | unhealthy evidence was not skipped or averaged away |
| post-terminal update | rejection without mutation | output canaries and prior terminal envelope | a later record could not rewrite the decision |

Before each call, predict the state, count, reason, and unchanged-output
behavior. Optional Serial output may summarize those same cells, but it is
never the only proof.

Resource-acquisition evidence at E0 is exactly zero pins, timers, interrupts,
buses, endpoints, sensors, and power resources. Separate safe-state evidence
is the absence of any hardware intent. Neither observation proves that a
physical sensor is disconnected, stationary, calibrated, or safe to power.

## Verification and resource scope

The host matrix must classify all 216 mappings with an independent oracle and
accept exactly 24. It also covers every source and producer state; each single
domain mismatch; mapping application and signed extrema; window sizes 2 and
32; exact and one-unit-over age, gap, acceleration, and rate limits; ordinary
and rollover sequence progress; duplicate, regression, ambiguity, and
collision precedence; every reset and shutdown point; terminal immutability;
output canaries; and replay equivalence after Lesson 067 image decoding.

Promotion includes strict C++11 warnings, sanitizers, packaging inventories,
ordinary Mega compilation, deterministic host replay, the lesson build, and
the site and PDF gates. The canonical Mega replay measures 14,908 bytes of
flash and 766 bytes of static SRAM. These establish bounded E0 policy only.
They do not establish physical identity, electrical compatibility, axis
mounting, conversion scale, stationary-fixture quality, noise, drift,
accuracy, or calibration.

## What remains open

An exact MPU6050 or QMI8658 source needs an independently qualified adapter and
specimen. Before power is applied, that work must identify the exact chip and
carrier revision from primary documentation; regulator, level-shifter,
pull-up, and address-strap population; supply and logic levels; identity,
reset, and range registers; byte order and conversion rules; data-ready and
saturation semantics; interrupt behavior; cadence; mounting; and named
electrical observation points.

Evidence for one family cannot qualify the other. Runtime probing, voting,
automatic fallback, hot switching, adaptive thresholds, hidden warm-up
discard, persisted prior qualification, display authority, and recorder
durability remain outside Lesson 068.

Every non-schematic lesson visual will use pencil-drawing presentation. A
future electrically authoritative formal schematic requires an identified
powered specimen and the separate formal-schematic classification gate.
