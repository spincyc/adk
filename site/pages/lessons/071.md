# Lesson 071 — three-leg module characterization

Status: **Published and host verified at E0; exact powered specimens and bench
acceptance remain open**

Energy class: **E0 copied descriptor and frame evidence with volatile policy
state only**
Safety boundary: **no module, ADC channel, comparator pin, pull resistor,
supply rail, potentiometer, stimulus, timer, interrupt, bus, display, clock,
storage medium, or powered fixture**

Lesson 071 turns a bounded stream of copied Lesson 070 frames into honest
threshold-characterization evidence:

`ascending leg + descending leg + independent verification leg → sampled transition brackets + conservative intervals + comparator relation`

`ModuleCharacterizationPolicy` does not operate a sensor or control a sweep.
The caller supplies each complete point and an explicit `now`. The policy
checks identity, provenance, order, freshness, direction, and comparator
meaning before it admits the point.

The result is deliberately less dramatic than a single “threshold” number.
A finite sampled run can prove only that a transition occurred between two
adjacent observations. It cannot reveal the exact switching voltage, convert
raw codes to a physical quantity, or diagnose a module from a rail value.

## Why there are three legs

The lesson separates learning from verification:

| Leg | Required direction | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `Ascending` | `Increasing` raw analog code | retain one adjacent comparator-transition bracket |
| `Descending` | `Decreasing` raw analog code | retain the reverse transition bracket |
| `Verification` | `Unordered` | compare new points only with the conservative regions learned from both brackets |

The legs occur in that order and use distinct, forward, nonzero leg
identities. Ascending and descending each permit exactly one clean comparator
transition. Verification never moves either learned bracket.

This separation prevents circular evidence. A point cannot teach the policy a
boundary and simultaneously serve as independent proof that the same boundary
predicts comparator behavior.

Each successful learning leg must cover the complete declared raw domain.
Ascending begins with an accepted point exactly at `rawDomain.lower` and ends
exactly at `rawDomain.upper`; descending begins at the upper endpoint and ends
at the lower endpoint. A missing endpoint is `DirectionViolation`, so the
policy never extrapolates a comparator state beyond the sampled sweep.

## The copied-evidence boundary

Configuration copies one complete `ModuleThresholdDescriptor`. Lesson 071
accepts only an `AnalogAndComparator` descriptor with an explicit
`ActiveHigh` or `ActiveLow` comparator polarity. Analog-only,
comparator-only, and unspecified-polarity declarations cannot support this
comparison.

Every accepted `ModuleCharacterizationPoint` carries:

- nonzero session, run, and leg identities;
- a one-based control ordinal;
- the active leg and its required direction;
- source identity and source-configuration revision; and
- one complete `ModuleThresholdFrame`.

The frame must match the configured descriptor schema, identity, revision,
declared specimen reference and revision, and declared
electrical-evidence revision. Both channels must be present and current, both
producer statuses must be OK, and `comparatorAsserted` must be canonical for
the declared polarity.

Warm-up and settling authority remains in the complete Lesson 070 frame.
Lesson 071 accepts no second pair of readiness flags and never waits, sleeps,
reads a clock, or decides that a physical module is ready. A structurally
attributable frame whose copied declaration says warm-up was not satisfied
terminalizes as `WarmupUnsatisfied`; when warm-up is satisfied but settling is
not, it terminalizes as `SettlingUnsatisfied`.

## Bounded streaming, not a hidden sample array

`requiredPointsPerLeg` is configurable from 2 through 16, inclusive. Every
leg finalizes at exactly the configured count. Control ordinals are contiguous
`1..N` within each leg, while source sequences remain contiguous across the
whole accepted run.

The policy stores streaming aggregates instead of retaining all points. The
only complete points kept are the two endpoints of each learned transition
bracket—four complete points in total. First, last, and offending observations
use compact witnesses.

A fieldwise-identical duplicate is an idempotent no-op. It consumes neither an
ordinal nor a point. A changed duplicate, gap, regression, identity drift, or
time ambiguity cannot be repaired by accepting later evidence. A point after
the configured leg capacity returns `CapacityExceeded` atomically; capacity
is an API result, not a fabricated characterization reason.

## Brackets are not exact thresholds

On the first clean comparator state change in a learning leg, the immediately
adjacent accepted points become a `ModuleTransitionBracket`. The policy does
not interpolate, average, sort, reverse, discard, or select a convenient
midpoint.

The following outcomes preserve what the samples actually show:

| Observation | Published reason |
|---|---|
| a second comparator reversal | `Chatter` |
| comparator toggle at an equal raw code | `Chatter` |
| raw codes move against the declared leg direction | `DirectionViolation` |
| no transition and comparator always asserted | `NoObservedTransitionActive` |
| no transition and comparator always inactive | `NoObservedTransitionInactive` |
| endpoint-only, no-transition leg | `AtLowerRail` or `AtUpperRail` |

The no-transition names report only the copied comparator state observed
during the finite leg. They do not prove that an output is physically stuck.
Likewise, `AtLowerRail` and `AtUpperRail` are endpoint-only finalization
results, not open-circuit, short-circuit, saturation, or damage diagnoses.

A healthy point at either declared raw endpoint is otherwise ordinary
evidence. It may count, establish order, or participate in a transition
bracket.

There is one deliberate exception to complete-domain coverage. If every
accepted point in a no-transition learning leg is the same exact declared
endpoint, finalization reports `AtLowerRail` or `AtUpperRail` before checking
coverage. That endpoint-only result is terminal: it cannot advance to another
leg or produce intervals.

## Conservative interval evidence

The two transition brackets freeze three inclusive
`ModuleAnalogInterval` values:

- `guaranteedInactiveInterval`;
- `guaranteedActiveInterval`; and
- `ambiguityInterval`.

The bracket orientations must be complementary. The ascending bracket's
`before` assertion must equal the descending bracket's `after` assertion; the
ascending `after` assertion must equal the descending `before` assertion; and
those two state groups must differ. Otherwise descending finalization records
`TransitionOrientationMismatch` and publishes no intervals.

For complementary brackets, let `lowState` be the common
ascending-before/descending-after assertion state. Let `lowProved` be the
lesser raw code of those two points, and let `highProved` be the greater raw
code of ascending-after and descending-before. The exact inclusive partition
is:

- `[rawDomain.lower, lowProved]` has `lowState`;
- `[highProved, rawDomain.upper]` has the opposite state; and
- `[lowProved + 1, highProved - 1]` is the ambiguity interval when its lower
  endpoint does not exceed its upper endpoint; otherwise ambiguity is absent.

Arithmetic is widened before the `+1` and `-1` tests.
`guaranteedInactiveInterval` and `guaranteedActiveInterval` receive the first
two intervals according to `lowState`.

The brackets may be disjoint, touching, overlapping, or identical. Overlap
does not automatically mean a fault; it may show only that the sampling step
was too coarse to resolve the transition region.

There is no public exact-threshold scalar, bracket midpoint, or signed
hysteresis scalar. `Increasing` and `Decreasing` describe raw analog-code
order only. They do not mean increasing light, heat, sound, magnetic field,
distance, danger, concentration, or any other physical quantity.

## Verification without circular reasoning

Verification begins only after both learning brackets and all three intervals
are frozen. Each unordered verification point is classified using only those
regions:

| Point location and comparator state | Relation evidence |
|---|---|
| matches inside a guaranteed region | contributes `Consistent` |
| contradicts inside a guaranteed region | terminal `AnalogComparatorDisagreement` and `Disagrees` |
| lies inside the ambiguity interval | contributes `Ambiguous` |

An ambiguity point never becomes disagreement merely because it is near a
sampled transition. If every verification point lies in ambiguity, the final
relation is `Ambiguous`, not `Consistent`.

## Lifecycle and public API

Construction is inert and copies configuration. The policy is noncopyable,
nonmovable, heap-free, callback-free, clock-free, and I/O-free.

```cpp
explicit ModuleCharacterizationPolicy (
    const ModuleCharacterizationConfig& config) noexcept;

Status initialize   (TimePoint now) noexcept;
Status beginSession (TimePoint now, uint32_t sessionId,
                     uint32_t runId) noexcept;
Status beginLeg     (TimePoint now, uint32_t legId,
                     ModuleCharacterizationLeg leg,
                     ModuleSweepDirection direction) noexcept;
Status observe      (TimePoint now,
                     const ModuleCharacterizationPoint& point) noexcept;
Status finalizeLeg  (TimePoint now) noexcept;
Status reset        (TimePoint now) noexcept;
Status shutdown     (TimePoint now) noexcept;
Status evidence     (ModuleCharacterizationEvidence& output) const noexcept;
```

The lifecycle is:

```text
initialize
  → beginSession
  → beginLeg(Ascending, Increasing) → observe... → finalizeLeg
  → beginLeg(Descending, Decreasing) → observe... → finalizeLeg
  → beginLeg(Verification, Unordered) → observe... → finalizeLeg
  → Complete
```

Initialize and reset advance a nonzero lifecycle generation without wrapping.
Sessions and runs use forward nonzero identities. A completed or rejected
session is terminal; later calls cannot rewrite its evidence. Reset begins a
new lifecycle, while shutdown prohibits further work for that object
lifecycle.

Every successful lifecycle call retains its supplied `now`. Later
`beginSession`, `beginLeg`, `finalizeLeg`, `reset`, and `shutdown` calls
require forward-or-equal modular time. Backward or exact-half-range ambiguous
time rejects atomically. `initialize()` after shutdown returns
`NotInitialized`; resuming requires a newly constructed policy object, not a
reset or reinitialization of the shut-down object.

`ModuleCharacterizationEvidence` copies the complete descriptor and
correlation fields, all three counts, both full transition brackets, all three
intervals, comparator relation, exactly four compact witnesses
(`firstWitness`, `lastWitness`, `offendingBefore`, and `offendingAfter`),
terminal state and reason, and `Status`. `firstWitness` and `lastWitness` are
the sole canonical owners of the run-wide first and last sequence/time
attribution; there are no duplicate top-level first/last sequence or time
fields. The two brackets remain the sole owners of exactly four complete
points.

## Atomic rejection and fault precedence

Invalid configuration, malformed encodings, lifecycle misuse, and correlation
mismatch return a non-OK API status without mutating policy evidence or caller
output. A structurally attributable unhealthy point instead publishes a
terminal domain reason with API `Ok`.

The controlling interpretation order is:

1. configuration and structural validity;
2. lifecycle, session, run, leg, ordinal, and correlation;
3. channel and producer failure;
4. supplied-time and source-sequence discontinuity;
5. `WarmupUnsatisfied`, then `SettlingUnsatisfied`;
6. direction and transition behavior; and
7. verification relation or complete evidence.

Producer failure dominates comparator interpretation. Time and sequence
discontinuity dominate direction and transition meaning. Direction violation
dominates an apparent crossing. Chatter prevents the policy from selecting
convenient points to manufacture a clean bracket.

Every mutation uses a staged candidate. A failed operation leaves the prior
object state, caller output, and any caller-owned fixture unchanged.

## Deterministic replay

The compile-only Mega 2560 lesson sketch supplies one synthetic three-leg
trace point by point. Before each call, predict the state, count, bracket,
interval, relation, and unchanged-output behavior.

| Stage | Predict | Observe | Interpret |
|---:|---|---|---|
| begin session | `Idle` becomes ready for the ascending leg | generation, session, run, and zero counts | one explicit run acquired authority |
| ascending stream | one adjacent transition bracket freezes | count, before/after raw codes, assertion, sequence, and time | sampled increasing evidence bounded one transition |
| descending stream | the reverse bracket freezes | second bracket and both learning counts | the reverse leg remained independent |
| verification ambiguity | relation is not promoted to agreement | verification count, ambiguity interval, and relation | coarse evidence stayed visibly uncertain |
| guaranteed-region check | matching state contributes `Consistent` | relation and compact witnesses | independent evidence agreed only where the brackets guaranteed a state |
| conflicting check | terminal `Disagrees` | reason and offending witnesses | contradiction remained attributable |
| post-terminal call | rejection without mutation | output canary and prior evidence | later input could not rewrite the decision |

Named volatile evidence cells are the E0 non-Serial observation path.
Optional Serial output may summarize the same values but cannot replace them.

Resource-acquisition evidence is exactly zero endpoint and registry calls.
Safe-state evidence is separately the absence of hardware intent. Neither
proves that a physical module is disconnected, unpowered, stable, or safe.

## Verification and resource scope

The host matrix exercises 2-, 3-, and 16-point legs, the attempted seventeenth
point, and early finalization. For three-point legs it covers both possible
adjacent transition positions under both comparator polarities. It also
covers representative inclusive interval boundaries, equal-code toggles,
repeated reversals, no-transition outcomes, rail endpoints, full-domain
endpoint coverage, the endpoint-only terminal exception, direction
violations, complementary and mismatched transition orientations, and
consistent, ambiguous, and disagreeing verification. Identity drift,
duplicate and ordering cases, producer/stale/warm-up/settling collisions,
lifecycle time regression and ambiguity, lifecycle exhaustion, reset,
shutdown, post-shutdown initialization, terminal immutability, and proof that
no point array is retained complete the reduced-domain matrix. This is not a
claim that every Cartesian combination of those domains is enumerated.

Publication passed strict C++11 warnings, sanitizers, package inventories,
ordinary Mega compilation, deterministic replay, the lesson build, site and
PDF gates, exact target-ABI object-size checks, and the complete
compiler/core/flag fingerprint.

## What remains open

Lesson 071 provides no wiring, pin assignment, endpoint adapter, stimulus
driver, powered sweep, potentiometer control, display, storage, or formal
schematic. It does not establish physical identity, electrical compatibility,
ADC conversion accuracy, comparator thresholds, hysteresis in volts, response
time, repeatability, noise behavior, or any semantic relationship between raw
code and a physical quantity.

Those claims require one exact E1 fixture with authoritative electrical
evidence, current-limited first energization, named AO/DO/VCC/GND test points,
a bounded stimulus, an independent visible validity/fault indicator, separate
resource-acquisition and safe-state evidence, and recorded bench acceptance.

No finite copied run authorizes open/short diagnosis, unknown-gas or flame
exposure, physiological use, food-safety claims, body contact, mains, high
voltage, pyrotechnic ignition, launcher control, or unknown-protocol replay.

[Lesson 072](072.md) consumes one complete immutable characterization envelope
to drive inert presentation intent and prepare one volatile record image. Its
published E0 boundary does not retroactively qualify the Lesson 071 evidence,
and durable storage remains outside this arc.
