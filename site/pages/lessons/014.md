# Character display and stable records

Prerequisites: **Lessons 001–013**

Energy class: **E1** — USB-powered, low-current display circuit

Lesson 014 keeps four concerns separate:

`climate state → presentation choice → character display`

`climate state → stable machine record`

`Hd44780Display` owns RS, E, and D4–D7 on an HD44780-compatible 16×2 LCD.
Its caller-time startup is explicit, and each later `update(now)` flushes at
most one changed cell. `show(row, text)` changes the desired presentation; it
does not change the climate sample.

`formatClimateRecord()` emits fixed-order ASCII fields with integer units:

```text
seq=42,state=valid,temp_centi_c=-125,rh_permille=456,at_ms=4294967290
```

It allocates nothing, does not use locale, distinguishes every sample state,
and reports a small caller buffer. Serial is an optional carrier for that
record, not the circuit's only evidence.

## Predict, observe, interpret

Build it in quick wins: power and contrast, RS/E, then four data wires. A
250 ms D13 blink says its own pin and all six display pins were acquired.
`LCD READY` is the first reward. Two value screens advance, a visible
`SENSOR TIMEOUT` follows, and the next screen recovers automatically.

Interpret the three paths separately:

- The short D13 blink proves setup acquired its resources.
- LCD text proves the current human presentation became visible.
- TP14 pulses prove transport activity, not readable contrast or correct data.

Press RESET to replay the entire visible story. Unplugging and reconnecting
also starts cleanly from the welcome screen.

## Wiring

Use a Mega 2560 and an identified HD44780-compatible 16×2 module:

| LCD function | Mega path |
|---|---|
| VSS / VDD | GND / 5 V |
| VO | wiper of 10 kΩ potentiometer between 5 V and GND |
| RW | GND |
| RS | D22 |
| E / TP14 | D23 |
| D4–D7 | D24–D27 |
| D0–D3 | unconnected |
| A / K (pins 15 / 16) | unconnected in this lesson |

The family listing does not identify a controller or electrical revision.
Continue only after the actual LCD independently shows a parallel 16-pin row
labeled, in order, VSS, VDD, VO, RS, RW, E, D0–D7, A, K. An I2C backpack,
missing labels, or a different order is not this lesson's specimen. A/K remain
disconnected, so the lesson makes no backlight-current assumption. Disconnect
USB for every wiring change.

## Make it yours

Replace the welcome text with a family message, turn the four fixed screens
into a tiny animation, or unplug during the timeout view and reconnect to see
the story restart. Automated lifecycle and fault checks stay behind the scenes.
