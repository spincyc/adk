# Lesson 041 — presence and passage

Status: **host verified E0; powered composition and bench acceptance open**

Energy class: **E0 copied-value policies**
Safety boundary: **evidence composition, not occupancy or protection**

This lesson keeps four observations separate while asking one bounded question:
is there fresh, qualified evidence of presence, agreement, or a completed beam
passage?

`PIR observation + beam observation + optional finish guard + timed range evidence → presence snapshot`

No supported circuit, wiring table, or powered adapter is published here.
`HC-SR501`, interrupted-light, reflective, and `HC-SR04` family names authorize
planning only. Exact markings, pin order, supply, output rails, polarity,
pull-ups, emitter behavior, current, and primary electrical sources remain
prerequisites to powered work.

## Two narrow policies

`PirObservationPolicy` qualifies copied digital samples. Warm-up must finish
before motion is eligible. Continuous motion may become `StuckMotion`; that is
retained evidence, not proof that a person is present. Clearing and recovery
use their own dwell boundaries.

`PresenceModel` consumes already-qualified source values. It does not resample,
vote, or replace one source with another. Every source retains identity,
status, validity, age, and provenance. A valid old value is `Stale`, not false.
A mismatch that lasts through the configured agreement window becomes
`Disagreement`; the model does not select a winner.

The E0 implementation owns copied state only. It owns no pin, endpoint, bus,
timer, interrupt, emitter, heap, callback, or hidden clock. Time enters in each
input frame.

## Passage is one qualified transition

A passage candidate starts with a valid beam interruption. A valid restoration
emits `passageEvent` only when it closes that candidate inside
`beamPassageWindow` and the complete required frame is fresh, valid, and not in
disagreement. A long, stale, faulted, or disagreeing frame clears the candidate
without inventing an event.

Range evidence preserves two clock domains:

- `measurementStartedAt` and `measurementLatency` describe acquisition in
  microseconds.
- `startedAt` and `completedAt` record when that outcome was started and copied
  into the course millisecond clock.

Completion stamping does not make an older range measurement simultaneous with
newer PIR or optical evidence. `Timeout`, `OutOfRange`, and source failure
remain distinct.

## Four-frame visible replay

Use the canonical sketch in replay mode; it must not energize a sensor.

1. Predict two clear frames, one active frame, then one restoration frame.
   The sketch supplies already-qualified `ReadyClear`, `Motion`, and
   `ReadyClear` PIR observations; it does not run PIR warm-up qualification.
2. Observe D30 turn on only after both LED resources and `PresenceModel`
   initialize.
   This is the acquisition signal. Observe D31 (`TP-PRESENCE`) remain off for
   the first two clear frames, then turn on for the active frame and remain on
   for the restoration frame's passage event.
3. In the active frame, predict qualified PIR eligibility, beam interruption,
   finish-guard activation, and a 250 mm valid approach. Interpret D31 on as a
   combined presence presentation, not source identity.
4. In the restoration frame, predict clear PIR, restored beam, inactive guard,
   and 800 mm range, plus exactly one qualified `passageEvent`. Interpret D31
   remaining on as passage evidence.

Resource acquisition and safe electrical state are separate checks. Replay
exercises their presentation but is not a physical measurement. D30 reports
successful acquisition. D31 is selected off before policy initialization and
shuts down to high impedance on the fault path. `evidenceCell`, `qualityCell`,
and `rangeCell` are debugger-readable supporting detail, not additional
non-Serial indicators.

## Evidence limits and open gates

Host tests cover warm-up, retrigger, stuck-motion recovery, per-source age
boundaries, disagreement, beam passage, range tuple validation, same-time
identity, rollover, half-range rejection, reset, and deterministic replay.
Those are host-test exercises, not behavior demonstrated by the four-frame
sketch. The E0 object gate measures `PresenceModel` at exactly 128 AVR bytes.
The canonical Mega replay compiles at 8,818 bytes flash and 226 bytes static
SRAM with the pinned AVR toolchain.

The following remain open:

- exact specimen identification and authoritative electrical evidence;
- powered adapters and transactional pin acquisition;
- wiring tables and formal schematics;
- powered aggregate pin, current, cadence, SRAM, and stack evidence;
- every E1 bench observation and physical acceptance record.

No compilation, synthetic trace, illustration, or PDF closes those gates. This
lesson makes no lux, distance-accuracy, occupancy, security, navigation,
collision-avoidance, traffic-enforcement, identity, or life-safety claim.
