# Observable greenhouse trainer

Status: **host verified; physical bench evidence not claimed**

Lesson 024 composes the previous two lessons into one deterministic project:

`observe moisture → decide watering → actuate one LED → present view → record decision`

The names are intentionally literal. `GreenhouseController` samples a
`MoistureSensor`, delegates bounded pump intent to `WateringController`, and
routes it through `PanelPumpOutput` into the three-channel `InertLoadPanel`.
The project writes a 16×2 `CharacterDisplay` and publishes a `StableRecord`
through a `RecordSink`. The sketch's sink accepts a deterministic simulated
RTC reading, appends the record, and synchronizes it to fixed storage.

The released circuit is not an irrigation product. “Pump” means one
resistor-limited LED on D38. There is no relay, motor, pump, heater, water,
external load supply, or mains connection.

## Object narrative

Objects appear in dependency order:

1. `Runtime` owns the resource registry.
2. `AnalogInput` owns A0; `MoistureSensor` borrows it.
3. Three `IndicatorPump` objects own D40, D38, and D41.
4. `InertLoadPanel` constrains those fan, pump, and heater indicators;
   `PanelPumpOutput` exposes only pump intent to `WateringController`.
5. `Hd44780Display` owns D30–D35.
6. `SimulatedRtc` supplies explicit deterministic clock states.
7. `FixedStorage` stages and synchronizes bounded records in RAM.
8. `EvidenceRecordSink` joins RTC, storage, and D13 acknowledgement.
9. `RgbLed` owns D5–D7; `GreenhouseHealthPattern` presents every mode without
   duplicating pattern timing in the sketch.
10. `GreenhouseController` coordinates lifecycle and the five loop stages.

The controller initializes moisture, watering, display, and records in that
order and rolls back in reverse. Its destructor performs the same safe cleanup
as `shutdown()`. Dependencies are caller-allocated and must outlive it.

## E1 circuit

Remove USB before wiring. Use a 10 kΩ linear potentiometer between Mega 5 V and
GND; connect its wiper to A0/TP-M. Every LED channel needs its own resistor.

| Evidence | Mega pin | Connection |
|---|---:|---|
| Moisture simulator / TP-M | A0 / D54 | potentiometer wiper |
| Simulated fan | D40 | 220 Ω, LED anode; cathode to GND |
| Simulated pump | D38 | 220 Ω, LED anode; cathode to GND |
| Simulated heater | D41 | 220 Ω, LED anode; cathode to GND |
| Record acknowledgement | D13 | Mega built-in LED; add no external D13 LED |
| Mode red | D5 | 330 Ω, RGB red anode |
| Mode green | D6 | 330 Ω, RGB green anode |
| Mode blue | D7 | 330 Ω, RGB blue anode |
| LCD | D30–D35 | RS, E, D4, D5, D6, D7 |
| LCD power | 5 V / GND | module rating and contrast network |

## Four quick wins

![Four dependent visible builds: moisture dial, watering LED, status dashboard, and record beacon](../downloads/assets/024-greenhouse-progress.png)

1. Wire the potentiometer and LCD. Turning the dial must move the displayed
   moisture value.
2. Add D38 and its resistor. Hold the dial dry: after the minimum-off interval
   D38 lights. Turn wet and it goes dark.
3. Add RGB. Its pattern and the LCD words must agree with D38. Every
   eight seconds, the sketch creates an amber, D38-dark inhibited intermission.
4. Watch built-in D13. It changes only after a complete record is appended and
   synchronized in the fixed RAM model.

Each later reward depends on the earlier signal chain being correct. At 120
seconds the sketch shuts every owned circuit down; press Reset for another run.

## The five stages

`observe(now)` performs at most one due analog sample. The first call anchors
all schedules and samples immediately. Missed periods coalesce; they do not
cause a burst.

`decide(now, input)` derives freshness and policy. `wateringAllowed` is a
software operator-inhibition input, not an electrical interlock. In the sketch
it alternates every eight seconds so the inhibited state is visible without an
extra input circuit.

`actuate(now)` is the only stage that may select the panel's pump channel.
`PanelPumpOutput` maps `On` to `SimulatedLoad::Pump` and `Off` to
`SimulatedLoad::None`; fan and heater remain off.

`present(now)` publishes an already-decided view when its interval is due.
A display failure cannot enable watering.

`record(now)` formats a versioned, locale-independent record. A failed append
keeps one immutable pending record; retry cannot replace it with a newer
decision. Sequence advances only after acceptance.

## State and precedence

| Mode | Meaning | RGB evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `Starting` | lifecycle acquired; schedules not yet observed | one blue pulse |
| `Monitoring` | valid sample, D38 off | one green pulse |
| `Watering` | policy requests D38 on | one cyan pulse |
| `Inhibited` | operator policy forces D38 off | steady amber |
| `SensorFault` | invalid, unavailable, or stale moisture | two red pulses |
| `OutputFault` | pump command failed and is latched | steady red |
| `DisplayFault` | view update failed | three amber pulses |
| `RecordFault` | record append/sync failed | four red pulses |
| `MultipleFaults` | output plus another current fault | steady red |

Output failure dominates and remains latched until reinitialization. Sensor
failure and inhibition always suppress watering. Display and record failures
do not alter a valid moisture value and cannot enable D38.

## Stable record

Version 1 is bounded ASCII with LF:

```text
adk-gh,1,17,42000,valid,472,340,on,dry,watering
```

Fields are format, version, sequence, decision milliseconds, sample state, raw
ADC reading, moisture permille or `-`, requested pump state, decision reason,
and greenhouse mode. The project uses the fixed-capacity storage implementation
to teach append versus sync and restart recovery. It does **not** claim SD-card
durability.

The first-class `Rtc` boundary distinguishes valid time, unset time, stopped
oscillator, and transport fault. The example's deterministic `SimulatedRtc`
returns a fixed epoch plus a sequence. The sink rejects a bad status or
non-valid clock state before append and sync. No physical RTC adapter has been
selected; the stable record intentionally retains injected `TimePoint`
milliseconds.

## Deterministic trace

With dry/wet calibration 800/200, thresholds 350/600, maximum-on 5000 ms, and
minimum-off 2000 ms:

| Time | Raw | Allowed | Expected result |
|---:|---:|:---:|---|
| 0 ms | 500 | yes | valid sample, pump off; off timer anchored |
| 1999 ms | 700 | yes | dry but minimum-off still active |
| 2000 ms | 700 | yes | watering requested; D38 on |
| 4500 ms | 440 | yes | wet threshold reached; D38 off |
| 6500 ms | 700 | yes | watering may start again |
| 8000 ms | 700 | no | inhibited; D38 off |
| 16000 ms | 700 | yes | policy may resume after timing checks |

Behind the scenes, automated replay checks snapshots, LCD lines, record bytes,
sequence numbers, pump commands, rollover, and injected failures. None of that
interrupts the build adventure.

## Read the visible story

Three independent lights make a wrong connection obvious:

- **Acquisition:** after startup, the LCD begins its nonblocking initialization
  and the RGB indicator leaves its starting pattern.
- **Safe state:** D38 is dark before watering, after inhibition, after invalid
  moisture, and after shutdown.
- **Record boundary:** D13 changes only after the fixed storage accepts and
  synchronizes a complete stable record.

Turn the dial slowly and watch the LCD, D38, RGB, and D13 tell the story. If a
later reward appears without the earlier one, remove USB and retrace that
stage's rows against the drawing.

## Behind-the-scenes fault coverage

Repository tests inject every fault without learner rewiring:

| Injection | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| invalid calibration or stale sample | `SensorFault`, D38 off |
| output command failure | latched `OutputFault`, best-effort off |
| display failure | watering policy unchanged; RGB reports display fault |
| storage append or sync failure | one record retained for byte-identical retry |
| delayed scheduler call | one coalesced operation, never a burst |
| shutdown from any mode | records, display, watering, and moisture shut down |

Never imitate those injections by shorting or hot-plugging pins.

## Safe finish

At the fixed deadline the controller closes records, display, watering,
moisture, and their owned outputs in dependency-safe order. Remove USB before
changing wires. An LED and fixed-storage model do not prove pump behavior, SD
durability, RTC accuracy, water delivery, plant health, or unattended safety.
No physical RTC, media adapter, relay, pump, heater, water path, or bench result
is claimed.
