# Lesson 001: Mega built-in LED

Make the board's own LED produce a repeatable pattern, then distinguish what
the program commands from what your eyes actually establish.

## Outcomes

By the end, you can:

- use `adk::led::Mono` with `LED_BUILTIN`;
- initialize the current ADK compatibility API;
- calculate the period and frequency of a timed on/off pattern;
- record repeated observations; and
- support a claim with evidence while stating measurement limits.

## Prerequisites

- [Local setup](../start.md) completed
- Ability to compile and explicitly upload a Mega 2560 sketch
- No prior breadboard experience required

## Bill of materials

- Arduino Mega 2560
- Known-good USB data cable
- Arch Linux computer with this ADK checkout

No breadboard, jumper wire, resistor, external LED, battery, or RF equipment is
needed.

## Safety

Use the bare board on a dry, nonconductive surface. Before inspection, remove
USB and every other power source. Keep loose metal away from headers. Stop and
disconnect upstream power for heat, smoke, odor, unexpected power flicker,
connector damage, or repeated host over-current disconnects.

## Current API used

```cpp
adk::led::Mono led (LED_BUILTIN);

adk::initialize ();
led.on          ();
led.off         ();
```

The supplied sketch commands 100 ms on and 100 ms off. One commanded cycle is
therefore 200 ms, or 5 Hz, before accounting for execution overhead and clock
tolerance.
