# Lesson 039 — percussion sequencer

Status: **host verified; reference-fixture bench acceptance open**

Energy class: **E0 deterministic engine; provisional E1 reference fixture**
Safety boundary: **contact timing and relative acoustic envelope only**

Four qualified contacts become one bounded pattern:

`contact attacks → simultaneous group → acoustic association → quantized hits → frame`

The reference fixture uses four C&K/Littelfuse `RB-220-07A R` rolling-ball
contacts. Each contact closes its named test point to ground; an individual
external 10 kΩ resistor pulls that node to the Mega 5 V rail. The adapter uses
`Pull::None` and active-low contact policy. One SparkFun `SEN-12642`, visibly
matching hardware `V10`, supplies `ENVELOPE` and active-high `GATE`; `AUDIO`
is disconnected. These named references do not establish conformance of a
look-alike kit part.

## Deterministic pattern

The first attack freezes the recording epoch and tempo. Attacks inside the
inclusive simultaneous window form one atomic group. One eligible completed
acoustic interval supplies relative intensity to the group; timeout supplies
zero with explicit `AssociationTimeout` provenance.

Hits use a 4–16-step sixteenth-note grid. Exact half-step ties round forward.
Fixed storage retains at most 32 hits. A group that cannot fit is rejected
atomically. `hit(index)` returns a copy, including its association provenance.

Playback derives its current step from supplied time. A sparse update jumps to
the current frame and never emits missed cues late. Four resistor-limited
surface LEDs, a one-digit step display, state and envelope-capture LEDs, and an
identified passive piezo present evidence. Zero-frequency intent does not
prove that a physical circuit or room is quiet.

## Reference connections

| Node | Mega | Reference connection |
|---|---:|---|
| TP-C0…TP-C3 | D40…D43 | each 10 kΩ to 5 V; one RB contact to GND |
| TP-E | A8 | SEN-12642 `ENVELOPE` |
| TP-T | D44 | SEN-12642 `GATE`, no pull-up |
| TP-BPM | A9 | 10 kΩ potentiometer wiper |
| surface evidence | D45…D48 | one LED and 1 kΩ to GND per lane |
| step display | D49/D50/D51 | 74HC595 data/clock/latch |
| state/capture | D52/D53 | ready/heartbeat and hardware-sample interval |
| bounded cue | D6 / timer 2 | identified passive piezo |
| play/clear | D38/D39 | buttons to GND, internal pull-ups |

Power the SEN-12642 and Mega together. Never leave a module output driven while
the Mega is unpowered. Disconnect USB before changing wiring. Stop for heat,
odor, reset, unstable rail, or unexpected output.

The sketch defaults to an absent-hardware replay with
`ADK_LESSON039_USE_REFERENCE_HARDWARE=0`. Define that compile-time macro as
`1` only after exact incoming conformance, unpowered inspection, and a
separately approved powered plan. No physical behavior, current, output range,
sound pressure, force, or performed rhythm has been verified.

## Predict, observe, interpret

1. In replay, predict three stored hits, then play from step zero.
2. Observe D45–D48 latch accepted hits for 180 ms and show current playback
   surfaces; skipped frames never flash late.
3. Observe D52 steady as ready outside playback and changing once per beat in
   playback, including empty steps. Replay leaves D53 inactive; a qualified
   reference build toggles it on each coalesced 20 ms hardware sample.
4. On a source or timing fault, predict retained host attribution while D52,
   D53, the display, surface LEDs, and piezo all become inactive.
5. On a qualified bench, measure each TP-C closed current and both SEN-12642
   output ranges and the aggregate current budget before recording acceptance.
6. After shutdown, verify cue outputs are inactive separately from acquisition
   status.
