# Lesson 046 — copied tactile and directional intent

Status: **E0 copied-evidence replay; powered adapters and bench acceptance open**

Energy class: **E0 pure policy**
Safety boundary: **fixed fixture values and in-memory result records only**

`InteractionIntentPolicy` combines one copied contact sample and one copied
directional observation into a coherent interaction record:

`copied contact + copied direction → qualify → classify → publish intent`

The policy owns no sensor, pin, ADC, timer, interrupt, bus, clock, callback, or
powered observation path. It privately reuses `ContactDynamics` for contact
qualification, release, refractory, and stuck-active behavior. Directional
values are already-calibrated permille evidence, not raw joystick readings.

## Evidence identity

Every accepted record preserves two independent source identities. Each
identity contains a source kind, nonzero source ID, and nonzero configuration
revision. Contact and directional timestamps and sequences remain separate, so
the caller can see which evidence produced the combined intent.

The E0 source kinds are synthetic fixtures and copied evidence. They do not
claim an electrical sensor, specimen equivalence, or a generic live-input
interface. In particular, a product label such as “Metal Touch” does not
establish capacitive behavior, active polarity, voltage, output level,
debounce needs, or safety.

A source-domain change starts a fresh event baseline. Within one domain,
modular sequence deltas `1..INT32_MAX` advance; a half-range delta is
ambiguous, and larger deltas regress. An exact repeat may age, but it does not
emit another event. Changed payload at an already accepted same-domain sequence is
rejected.

## Transactional update

Updates use an opaque preview:

1. `preview()` validates both complete inputs and derives a candidate without
   changing committed state.
2. `canCommit()` confirms that the candidate still belongs to this policy and
   its current generation.
3. `commit()` advances the private contact policy once and publishes the
   complete interaction atomically.

Foreign, stale, reused, and reset-invalidated previews cannot commit.
Structural failures—including unknown source values, malformed status,
out-of-range axes, future observations, time regression, sequence regression,
half-range ambiguity, and changed same-sequence evidence—leave the previous
snapshot unchanged.

`reset()` restores an initialized, event-free policy baseline. It is not
endpoint shutdown and performs no electrical action.

## Contact and direction

The canonical configuration uses active-high contact, 8 ms qualification,
8 ms release, an 80 ms refractory period, a 2000 ms stuck-active limit,
100 ms maximum age for each evidence stream, a 300-permille direction-engage
threshold, and a 200-permille release threshold.

Directional magnitude is `max(abs(x), abs(y))`, with each axis constrained to
`[-1000, 1000]`. Engage/release hysteresis prevents a small change near the
threshold from repeatedly toggling direction. Below the release threshold,
the result is `Neutral`.

The eight non-neutral sectors use a fixed integer boundary. For major and
minor absolute axes, `minor × 1000 <= major × 414` selects the principal axis;
exact equality belongs to that axis. Otherwise, the signs select a diagonal.
Equal nonzero axes are diagonal. No floating point or division is required.

A direction event occurs only when the engaged direction changes. Returning
to neutral emits one release-direction event. Exact repeats, source changes,
and recovery from stale or fault evidence establish event-free baselines
instead of fabricating actions.

Directional saturation is valid extreme evidence: its provenance flag is
retained and the direction remains usable. A value outside the declared
permille range is malformed and rejected.

## Quality and precedence

| Admitted evidence | Published quality | Event behavior |
|---|---|---|
| contact source failure | `SourceFault` with the complete contact status | no usable touch or direction event |
| directional source failure | `SourceFault` with the complete directional status | no usable touch or direction event |
| qualified contact remains active too long | `StuckActive` | contact policy remains observable, but no fault is hidden |
| either copied input exceeds its maximum age | `Stale` | repeated evidence emits no new event |
| both inputs are healthy and within age | `Current` | qualified contact and direction events may publish |

When source failures collide, contact status has precedence while both source
statuses remain in the snapshot. Structural chronology faults are rejected
before publication; the reserved `TimingFault` quality is not produced by
this lesson.

Age equal to its configured maximum remains current. One tick beyond it is
stale. Fresh valid evidence after a fault or stale interval begins a new,
event-free baseline.

## Deterministic replay

The canonical compile-only sketch replays neutral contact, qualification,
cardinal and diagonal direction, saturation, direction release, contact
release, and a directional producer failure. It copies every result into
named memory cells.

| Fixture | Predict | Observe in the host harness | Interpret |
|---|---|---|---|
| neutral, inactive contact | `Current`, `Neutral`, no events | both source identities and sequences are retained | the facade accepted two copied streams |
| contact attack held for 8 ms | qualified touch event | `touchActive` and one `touchEvent` | existing contact qualification was reused |
| 600-permille positive X | engaged `East` | bounded magnitude and one direction event | copied directional intent, not a physical joystick read |
| equal positive X and Y | `NorthEast` | diagonal sector at the exact tie | deterministic octant classification |
| saturated 1000/1000 | usable `NorthEast` with saturation retained | `directionalSaturated` remains true | saturation is explicit extreme evidence |
| direction below 200 permille | `Neutral` with one direction event | magnitude and release transition are retained | hysteresis released the engaged direction |
| directional producer failure | `SourceFault`, no events | complete producer status | failed numeric evidence did not authorize an action |

Reset and replay the same fixtures, then compare every public field. Equal
field values are deterministic evidence; comparing struct padding bytes is
not.

The named memory cells are the non-Serial observation path for E0. Policy
initialization is the separate resource-acquisition result: it confirms only
that configuration and private policy state were accepted. Reset confirms
only the memory safe state. Neither result is evidence of an electrical
acquisition, driven output, or physical shutdown.

## Resource and powered-work gates

The policy is fixed-storage, allocation-free, and bounded. The canonical Mega
2560 sketch built with Arduino AVR core 1.8.8 and AVR GCC 7.3 measures
**6,956 bytes of flash** and **733 bytes of static SRAM**, below its
16,384-byte flash and 2,048-byte SRAM ceilings. Compilation is packaging and
size evidence only.

Do not execute the E0 sketch on a powered Mega or inspect it through a powered
hardware debugger as E0 evidence. This lesson publishes no pin assignment,
wiring table, voltage, current, live GPIO or ADC operation, electrical test
point, or formal schematic.

E1 remains open for exact touch/contact and joystick specimens, authoritative
electrical records, polarity and level qualification, powered adapters,
non-Serial electrical observation points, resource-acquisition evidence,
shutdown and power-removal evidence, an authoritative formal schematic, and
bench acceptance. Lesson 046 makes no capacitive-sensing, gesture, proximity,
heartbeat, pulse, physiological, or specimen-equivalence claim.
