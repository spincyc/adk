# Lesson 051 — inert tabletop parts carousel

Status: **E0 synthetic project replay; exact adapters, durable media, powered
motion, and bench acceptance open**

Energy class: **E0 transactional policy**
Safety boundary: **caller-owned byte images and inert actuator intent only; no
RFID transaction, storage write, energized coil, servo pulse, or mechanism**

`InertPartsCarousel` coordinates local identity, explicit confirmation,
bounded logical homing, logical step sequencing, gate intent, presentation
status, and fixed audit records:

`copied evidence + acknowledged audit image + explicit time → closed/off or bounded carousel intent`

The project handles named paper-bin records in a deterministic fixture. An
identifier is not authentication, a credential, or a person. A logical bin
coordinate is not physical position. Gate-open intent is not a servo command,
and an acknowledged caller-owned record image is not proof of nonvolatile
durability.

## Composition without hidden hardware

The coordinator borrows one Lesson 049 `LocalIdentityRegistry`, one Lesson 050
`BoundedHomingPolicy`, fixed audit slots, and one audit-candidate buffer. It
exclusively coordinates those borrowed policies while initialized and owns
one fixed-configuration Lesson 047 logical coil sequencer. It owns no endpoint,
driver, bus, clock, storage transport, RFID reader, keypad, display, motor,
servo, supply, or moving mechanism.

Construction is inert. Initialization validates the complete bin map, key
encoding, time bounds, child configuration, audit extent, and canonical audit
prefix before starting the children. Failure rolls initialized children back
in reverse order. Shutdown first publishes closed gate and zero-coil intent,
invalidates authorization and home state, then shuts down the policies. It is
idempotent.

The public snapshot keeps phase, typed fault, requested bin, authorization
state, logical-position evidence, complete intent, audit record and pending
reconciliation state distinct. The project phases cover idle, confirmation,
homing, positioning, ready, gate intent, completion, cancellation, stop, and
fault without collapsing their causes into one generic status.

## One admitted stage per update

Each `update(now, frame)` performs bounded work and commits at most one
transaction stage and one logical motion transition. A frame retains explicit
source identity, occurrence time, sequence, status, and canonical absence for
identity, key, home, stop, and presentation evidence.

The ordinary operation is:

1. admit a known local identifier;
2. require the exact configured digit sequence and confirm action before its
   deadline;
3. reserve adjacent start and terminal audit slots;
4. export the authorization-start record and remain motion-inhibited;
5. accept only a synchronized, reread, byte-identical acknowledgement;
6. establish a fresh session-local logical home;
7. advance toward the confirmed bin by at most one logical transition per
   admitted frame;
8. publish bounded gate-open intent only at the known target; and
9. restore closed/off intent before exporting the terminal audit record.

A repeated source sequence cannot add a digit or create another event.
Leading zeros in confirmation codes are significant. Cancel dominates a
same-frame confirm/cancel collision and grants no authority. A new identity
cannot replace an operation that is awaiting confirmation.

## Gate-open invariant

The semantic gate intent may be open only when every term is true:

```text
known local identity
&& exact confirmation remains current
&& authorization-start audit record was durably acknowledged
&& home was established in this session
&& known logical position equals the confirmed bin position
&& independent stop is inactive
&& every admitted source is healthy and current
```

Opening lasts no longer than the configured gate-intent duration. Closed
intent is restored before completion. Presentation failure cannot authorize,
extend, or disguise gate or motion intent.

For each ordinary non-stop motion frame, the coordinator first retains a
Lesson 050 preview. If that preview requests a signed step, the coordinator
commits it and applies one exact Lesson 047 step only after the remaining
preflight succeeds. Project-fixed `holdAtRest = false` advances the child's
logical coordinate and immediately returns its intent all-off, so Lesson 051
intentionally publishes `coilBits == 0`. Nonzero staged coil vectors remain a
Lesson 047 teaching surface; their absence here is a specified result, not
missing observation evidence.

The gate value is only `Open` or `Closed` semantic intent with an expiry.
Neither it nor the intentional zero-coil result proves an endpoint write,
coil current, shaft movement, servo position, or a part being dispensed.

## Independent stop and home synchronization

A separately valid active stop is examined before unrelated frame fields. It
clears coil intent, closes the gate, and cancels new authority even when an
identity, key, home, or presentation field is malformed. A malformed stop
cannot be trusted as inactive, so it rejects the complete frame.

When the private Lesson 047 child is live, stop uses its direct stop path. When
the child is idle, the coordinator avoids a synthetic cancel and preserves
the child's generation. An unexpected child-stop failure invokes the all-off
reset fallback and latches a position fault.

A qualified home edge is a separate synchronization boundary. The coordinator
stops a live child, resets its logical coordinate to native zero, verifies
inactive phase and zero coil intent, and only then commits the Lesson 050 home
result. No accepted home epoch can coexist with a stale pre-home coordinate.
An independent stop colliding with home follows only the stop path.

These are deterministic software ordering guarantees. Software stop intent is
not emergency-stop hardware or actuator-power removal.

## Fixed audit-image transaction

Audit storage is a caller-owned append-only image with an even capacity of
two, four, six, or eight records. Each record has a normative 40-byte
fieldwise encoding with magic `0x4341`, schema version, project and operation
identity, authorization epoch, record sequence, occurrence time, kind, phase,
bin, non-security identity digest, committed binding revision and image
generation, home epoch, typed status, zero reserved bytes, and
CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE checksum.

An operation reserves adjacent start and terminal slots before exposing its
start candidate. The project accepts an acknowledgement only when candidate
ownership, generation, operation, slot, checksum, view metadata, synchronized
status, reread validation, and every reconciled byte match. Failed or
indeterminate evidence retains reconciliation-required state and inhibits
new input work.

Restart scans only a canonical valid prefix. A valid start with its reserved
terminal slot still erased becomes an inhibited interrupted operation and
prepares a recovery terminal for that same operation. Corruption,
unsupported schema, noncanonical tails, unmatched records, and ambiguous
sequence order fail closed.

This proves reconstruction from supplied bytes. It does not prove write
atomicity, power-loss survival, retention, endurance, or the behavior of an
EEPROM, flash, or SD adapter.

## Deterministic replay

The compile-only Mega sketch supplies synthetic identity, key, home, stop,
presentation, and audit-image fixtures. It records phase, fault,
authorization, logical position, coil bits, gate intent, audit state, and
operation identity in fixed result cells.

Executable host tests replay:

- every bin, confirmation success, conflict, cancel, and expiry boundary;
- release-first homing, bounded positioning, interruption, and explicit
  re-home;
- exact logical position transitions, intentional zero-coil publication, and
  closed/open gate intent;
- stop collisions with identity, confirmation, home, due step, arrival,
  gate request, expiry, presentation failure, and full audit storage;
- future, stale, skewed, repeated, regressed, rollover, and mutated evidence;
- valid, corrupt, torn, full, indeterminate, and interrupted audit images;
  and
- two independent instances producing byte-identical snapshots and audit
  images.

Named memory cells are the E0 non-Serial observation path. Compilation proves
only that the API and fixture fit the Mega package; it does not prove that a
powered board executed the trace.

## Resource and evidence gates

E0 owns exactly zero pins, ADC channels, timers, interrupts, buses,
resource-registry entries, electrical endpoints, supplies, storage
transports, or moving hardware. The project coordinator measures **380 bytes**:
it misses its reviewed **320-byte target** and passes its **384-byte hard
ceiling**. This project-composition exception does not relax the **128-byte**
ceiling for reusable children. The canonical Mega sketch measures **26,014
bytes flash** and **1,933 bytes static SRAM**, inside its respective
**28,672-byte** and **2,048-byte** ceilings. Its conservative live stack/ISR
path is **742 bytes**, rounded to a **768-byte** reserve; that leaves **5,517
bytes** on the exact path or **5,491 bytes** with the rounded reserve, above
the required **1,024-byte** margin.

E1 remains open for exact RFID, keypad, home, stop, indicator, display, and
nonvolatile-media adapters. Each requires primary evidence, a pin and resource
map, an authoritative formal schematic, rollback and safe-state proof,
powered observation, media measurements, and a bench record. E1 still does
not authorize motor or servo power.

E2 remains open for exact stepper/driver and servo identities, independently
switchable current-limited actuator power, reviewed winding and clamp
topology, mechanical restraint, independent stop and power removal, current
and thermal limits, and measured homing, position, gate, and motion acceptance.
