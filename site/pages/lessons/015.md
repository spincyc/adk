# Build an environmental station

Turn two earlier wins into one quick story:

`climate light → LCD hello → numbered live record → fault → recovery`

This is an E1, USB-powered learning instrument. It is not a calibrated weather
station, alarm, or life-safety monitor.

## Before power

This build conditionally uses:

- an independently identified, labeled 5 V DHT11 module whose documentation
  establishes its `+`, DATA/S, `-`, current, and pull-up arrangement;
- an independently identified parallel LCD1602 whose 16 pins are documented
  as VSS, VDD, VO, RS, RW, E, D0–D7, A, K;
- a known common-cathode RGB LED with one 330 Ω resistor per channel.

The Elegoo family listings authorize DHT11 and LCD1602 families, but do not
establish the revision or physical pin order in your hand. Leave LCD A/K open
unless its exact backlight rating and resistor are known. Unplug USB before
every wiring change.

## Four visible wins

1. Keep the Lesson 013 climate-light breadboard. Replay that sketch and wait
   for a valid RGB climate color.
2. Add the Lesson 014 LCD circuit in columns 31–57 on the right half of the
   same 830-point breadboard. Use D30–D35 rather than Lesson 014's D22–D27,
   because D22 remains DHT11 DATA. With USB unplugged, continuity-check both
   long rails and bridge each same-polarity center split only if your board
   has one.
3. Upload Lesson 015. D13 flashes for 250 ms, then the LCD says `STATION
   STARTING`. The first complete sensor frame unlocks a numbered record.
4. With USB unplugged, lift only DATA, repower, and see the explicit red fault
   rhythm. Unplug, restore DATA, and repower to recover the hello and live
   record.

The RGB LED reacts to accepted humidity: green below 55% RH, cyan through
69.9% RH, and amber at 70% RH or above. A missing or rejected frame produces
red pulses instead of counterfeit values.

## Literal pin map

| Part | Mega connection |
|---|---|
| DHT11 DATA | D22 |
| LCD RS, E | D30, D31 |
| LCD D4, D5, D6, D7 | D32, D33, D34, D35 |
| LCD RW | GND |
| LCD VO | center wiper of a 10 kΩ contrast potentiometer |
| RGB red, green, blue | D5, D6, D7 through separate 330 Ω resistors |
| acquisition light | built-in D13 LED |

LCD D0–D3 remain open in four-bit mode. LCD A/K remain open in this lesson.
The PDF has four pencil plates: the actual Mega paired-header locations, the
left and right regions of the one breadboard with numbered holes, and the
expected visible progression.

## The bounded run

`EnvironmentalStation` samples only at explicit caller-supplied deadlines.
Each record keeps its values, validity, sensor status, timestamp, and sequence
together. A late loop makes one observation rather than a burst.

After 120 seconds, the LCD says `STATION RESTING` and `PRESS RESET`. The sketch
allows the message to finish, turns off the indicators, shuts down the sensor
and display, and releases every owned pin. RESET replays the complete story.

Automated host checks remain behind the scenes. They cover exact deadlines,
faults, stale data, recovery, rollback, rollover, restart, repeated shutdown,
and deterministic replay without turning those checks into learner chores.
