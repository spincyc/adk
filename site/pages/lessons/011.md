# Timed traffic states

Status: **engine host verified; hardware adapter experimental**<br>
Energy class: **E1** — USB-powered, resistor-limited indicator LEDs

This lesson separates a timed decision from its electrical presentation:

`button event + explicit time → TrafficJunction → signal snapshot → LEDs`

The application samples the pedestrian button, supplies one complete
`TrafficInput`, then presents the returned `TrafficSignals`. `TrafficJunction`
owns no pins and consults no hidden clock. `update(now, input)` makes every
transition replayable, including timestamp wrap.

Predict the next legal phase and its deadline. Observe D30–D35 for the two
vehicle directions, D36–D37 for walk and stop, and D13 for successful resource
acquisition. Interpret the complete signal pattern rather than one LED alone:
both vehicle greens must never appear together, a green direction requires red
on the conflicting direction, and a circuit-health fault must present all red.

D22 uses the Mega internal pull-up and a button to GND. D30–D37 each drive one
LED through its own 330 Ω resistor. D13 is a separate ready indication; it
proves that the adapter acquired its resources, not that the traffic phase is
electrically correct.

Host tests replay every phase, requests at boundaries, invalid configuration,
time reversal, rollover, restart, and fault entry. The Mega firmware compiles
(5,404 bytes flash, 335 bytes static RAM); physical phase timing, all-red
behavior, current, reset, and shutdown observations remain open.
