# Separate authorized family scope from exact electrical identity

The authorized component-family set is the deduplicated union of the official
Elegoo Mega 2560 Most Complete Starter Kit and official Elegoo 37-in-1 Sensor
Modules Kit listings. Each source retains its own stated quantities and
provenance; duplicate families are not silently quantity-summed.

This listing evidence authorizes curriculum scope and removes the inventory
task's global photograph blocker. It does not establish board revision,
connector pin order, polarity, component marking, voltage/current limits, or
other electrical facts absent from the listing. Lessons must cite primary
sources or exact physical evidence for those claims and retain a narrow
inventory block where ambiguity affects safety or behavior.
