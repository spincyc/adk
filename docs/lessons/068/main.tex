\lessonbox{\textbf{Responsibility.}
\texttt{InertialRecordQualificationPolicy} maps and evaluates a bounded
stationary window from exactly one configured copied record source. It
publishes one attributable terminal evidence envelope. It does not discover,
probe, select, vote, fail over, configure, or physically calibrate a sensor.}

\section{Draw the one-source boundary}
The configured domain includes source kind, model and ID; source configuration
and calibration revisions; ranges; record schema and normalization revisions;
qualification revision; mapping; sample count; age and gap limits; and
stationary bounds. Every record must match the whole domain.

\section{Map both vectors through one proper rotation}
\texttt{SourceAxisMapping} chooses one signed source axis for each destination
axis. Each source axis appears exactly once and the transform must be
right-handed. Exactly 24 of all 216 signed-axis triples pass. The same mapping
applies to acceleration and angular rate.

\section{Keep time supplied and ordering explicit}
The policy reads no clock. Each call supplies \texttt{now}; every record
retains its observation time and nonzero sequence. Age and gap at their exact
configured maxima pass. One tick beyond fails. Future, backward, and exact
half-range time are explicit failures.

Sequence rollover from \texttt{UINT32\_MAX} to 1 is valid because zero is
reserved. A fieldwise-identical duplicate is idempotent and does not increase
the count. A changed duplicate, gap, regression, zero, or half-range ambiguity
ends the attempt with attributable evidence.

\newpage
\section{Let every record prove stationary behavior}
Mapping happens before bounds checks. Each mapped acceleration component must
remain inside its inclusive deviation around the configured expected
stationary acceleration. Each mapped angular-rate component must remain
between its inclusive positive and negative limit.

Every individual record must pass. A plausible average cannot conceal a
single moving, saturated, stale, or faulted record. Exact limits pass; one
unit beyond fails. Differences, absolute values, sums, and means use checked
widened arithmetic.

\section{Separate operation failure from unhealthy evidence}
Malformed configuration, invalid record structure, or lifecycle misuse returns
a non-OK \texttt{Status} and changes nothing. A well-formed but unhealthy
record is meaningful domain evidence: it may return operation status OK while
publishing \texttt{Rejected} and a precise qualification reason.

\newpage
\section{Apply one deterministic precedence}
The implementation stages the complete candidate before changing accepted
count, extrema, sums, or evidence. When failures collide, the same ordered
rule always chooses the scalar reason while the terminal record retains the
complete collision.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.97\linewidth,height=2.55in,keepaspectratio]
 {068-precedence-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: a graphite checklist orders lifecycle,
source domain, chronology, producer quality, mapping, numeric bounds, and

\end{center}

A producer fault dominates not-ready and saturation because its numeric
payload is not trusted. With producer status OK, not-ready dominates
saturation; saturation dominates stationary arithmetic. Acceleration
out-of-window supplies the reason before angular rate when both fail.

\lessonbox{\textbf{Atomicity experiment.}
Place recognizable canaries around the evidence object. Submit a changed
duplicate, an unrepresentable mapping, and a post-terminal record. Verify that
each rejected operation leaves the previous evidence and both canaries
byte-for-byte unchanged.}

\section{Read the complete evidence, not a Boolean}
The copied envelope retains attempt and lifecycle generation, qualification
revision, mapping, state and reason, accepted count, first/last sequences and
times, maximum age and gap, mapped means, extrema and widened sums, the
terminal source-frame record, mapped terminal record, and status.

Lesson 069 consumes this complete immutable envelope. It must not combine a
record from one attempt with qualification from another or remap a mutable
child snapshot.

\newpage
\section{Replay two traces without touching hardware}
The Mega sketch follows acquire, configure, start, then observe, decide,
actuate. It normalizes copied synthetic samples, qualifies one three-record
stationary trace, resets explicitly, and rejects one independently faulted
trace. ``Actuate'' means publish inert memory values.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth,height=2.30in,keepaspectratio]
 {068-replay-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: three sketched cards prompt the learner to
predict state and count, observe named volatile evidence cells, and interpret
one qualifying trace plus one rejected fault trace without a hardware claim.}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item \textbf{Predict} the state, reason, count, source ID, and first/last
       sequence before each call.
 \item \textbf{Observe} the two named volatile result cells and completion
       cell with a debugger, simulator, or disassembly. Serial is optional.
 \item \textbf{Interpret} the stationary result as E0 contract evidence and
       the producer-fault result as proof that unhealthy input was retained.
\end{enumerate}

The memory cells are the non-Serial observation path. Resource-acquisition
evidence is separately zero pins, timers, interrupts, buses, endpoints,
sensors, and supplies. Safe-state evidence is the absence of hardware intent.
Neither proves that a physical unit is attached, stationary, or safe to power.

\section{Test the finite matrix}
\begin{itemize}
 \item Classify all 216 mappings with an independent determinant oracle:
       exactly 24 pass.
 \item Exercise every identity/revision mismatch, producer state, readiness,
       saturation, signed extreme, and mapping failure.
 \item Test window sizes 2 and 32; exact and one-over age, gap, acceleration,
       and rate limits; duplicates, rollover, regression, and collisions.
 \item Test reset and shutdown at every count, terminal immutability, output
       canaries, and replay equivalence after Lesson 067 decode.
\end{itemize}

\section{Exercises}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Explain why source selection means configuration, not runtime probing.
 \item Design a collision where producer fault, not-ready, and saturation are
       all present. Which reason wins, and what evidence remains visible?
 \item Explain why a passing mean cannot excuse one acceleration excursion.
 \item Describe an identical duplicate and a changed duplicate. Why does only
       the first leave the attempt collecting?
 \item List the exact E1 evidence needed before claiming support for one
       physical MPU6050 or QMI8658 carrier.
\end{enumerate}
