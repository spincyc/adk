\lessonbox{\textbf{Responsibility.}
\texttt{BoundedLowSideDriverPolicy} validates one immutable declaration,
performs checked conservative current and duty arithmetic, and publishes
bounded active-high or all-off logical intent. It owns no GPIO, endpoint,
transistor, diode, load, supply, timer, clock, or storage.}

\section{Start with authority, not a transistor picture}
This lesson deliberately contains no circuit or schematic. A familiar part
number does not fix pin order, gain, package, voltage, current, carrier
topology, or whether the populated device is genuine. PN2222 and S8050 remain
separate variants. The E0 policy can check copied declarations; it cannot
identify or energize a physical specimen.

\section{Declare one topology precisely}
The sole modeled topology is an active-high bare-NPN low-side drive.
\texttt{outputLevelHigh} means the logical level a future endpoint would be
asked to produce; it is never a pin observation. There is no active-low,
high-side, PNP, MOSFET, Darlington, H-bridge, relay-module, or generic
three-pin interpretation.

A resistive-indicator declaration requires flyback to be not required and
every diode field to be canonically absent. An inductive-inert declaration
requires a present diode plus nonzero identity, revision, orientation,
return, reverse-voltage, and forward-current fields. E0 checks completeness
and conservative rating arithmetic only; it does not authenticate placement
or observe clamping.

\toprule
Declaration & Structurally valid? & Physical authority?\\
\midrule
resistive, diode fields all absent & yes & none\\
resistive with a stray diode identity & no & none\\
inductive with one missing diode field & no & none\\
complete copied inductive declaration & possibly & still none\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Bind the whole declaration}
Schema, specimen family and specimen identity, revisions, evidence source,
configuration, topology, protection, every current/voltage bound, resistor
tolerance, maximum duration, duty window, and duty limit enter one canonical
descriptor identity digest. Zero is a valid digest value, not a missing-value
sentinel.

An accepted request additionally binds lifecycle generation, session, run,
step, request, source, source-configuration revision, sequence, observation
time, duration, active state, requested current, and complete producer status.
Identity drift, changed duplicates, gaps, regressions, or half-range
ambiguity reject atomically.

\section{Do conservative current arithmetic}
All current values are integer microamps, voltage values are integer
millivolts, and resistance is integer ohms. Multiplications widen before use.
Required base current rounds up; load current supported by admitted base
current rounds down.

\[
 I_{\mathrm{base,req}} =
 \left\lceil\frac{I_{\mathrm{load,req}}G_d}{G_n}\right\rceil
\qquad
 I_{\mathrm{base,res}} =
 \left\lfloor\frac{\max(0,V_{\mathrm{OH,min}}-V_{\mathrm{BE,max}})1000}{R_B}\right\rfloor
\]
\[
 I_{\mathrm{load,adm}} = \min(I_{\mathrm{load,req}},
 I_{\mathrm{supply,avail}}, I_{\mathrm{device}},
 I_{\mathrm{fixture}}, \lfloor I_{\mathrm{base,adm}}G_n/G_d\rfloor)
\]

\end{enumerate}

Zero divisors or required ceilings, overflow, underflow, an unrepresentable
result, and any exceeded binding ceiling never become a plausible partial
command. They reject without mutation and publish no hardware fact.

\newpage
\section{Bound duration and rolling duty}
An active request supplies a nonzero duration no greater than the configured
maximum. Admission freezes \texttt{expiresAt}; replay never extends it.
\texttt{update(now)} receives time from the caller and returns to canonical
off at deadline equality or later. The policy reads no clock.

Eight fixed history entries retain admitted intervals. Early off or
cancellation shortens the current interval, but the used interval remains in
the rolling duty history. Old intervals are pruned only when their ends lie
at or before the window start.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.97\linewidth,height=2.28in,keepaspectratio]
 {079-duty-window-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: a graphite time line shows an early-closed
interval, retained history, supplied now, and a candidate interval; a brace
marks that only rolling-window overlaps count toward duty.}
\end{center}

The widened admission inequality is
\[
 \mathrm{reservedTicks}\times1000
 \leq \mathrm{windowTicks}\times\mathrm{maximumDutyPermille}.
\]
Equality passes; one tick or permille over rejects. A ninth still-overlapping
interval reports \texttt{Rejected/CapacityExceeded}, preserves all eight
history entries, and publishes off intent for that request. This finite
boundary is a domain result, not hidden allocation.

\toprule
Case & Expected interpretation\\
\midrule
deadline minus one tick & request may remain active\\
deadline exactly & \texttt{Expired}, canonical off\\
duty equality & admitted if every other check passes\\
one unit over duty & rejected, history remains attributable\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Make all-off a complete result}
Logical inactive requests, rejection, cancellation, producer fault, expiry,
reset, and shutdown all publish active-high topology's canonical off intent:
\texttt{logicalActive=false}, \texttt{outputLevelHigh=false}, and zero
admitted currents. The reason and producer status remain attributable.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth,height=2.25in,keepaspectratio]
 {079-all-off-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: sketched rejection, cancellation, and expiry
cards converge on one canonical off card with false logical activity, low
output intent, and zero admitted current; the future physical endpoint is
shown as absent.}
\end{center}

Cancellation uses a fully correlated control value. If off is confirmed, the
terminal result is \texttt{Cancelled}. If an attributable producer failure
prevents confirming off, the result is \texttt{Fault}; cancellation remains
the cause and cannot hide failed safe return. A well-formed producer fault is
domain evidence and may return operation status OK while intent remains off.

\lessonbox{\textbf{Atomicity experiment.}
Place recognizable canaries around the output intent. Submit a changed
duplicate, a sequence gap, a backward time, and a malformed request. Confirm
that every API rejection leaves the policy, output, and canaries byte-for-byte
unchanged. Then contrast a valid budget rejection, which commits an
attributable off/rejected domain result.}

\section{Replay without touching hardware}
The Mega sketch constructs one copied descriptor, begins one session, applies
bounded active and off requests, exercises one rejection and expiry, and
copies results into named volatile cells.

\begin{enumerate}
 \item \textbf{Predict} state, reason, output level, required base current,
       admitted load current, and expiry before each call.
 \item \textbf{Observe} the named volatile intent cells with a debugger,
       simulator, or disassembly. Serial is optional and never sole evidence.
 \item \textbf{Interpret} agreement as deterministic E0 policy evidence.
       It does not show that a physical pin changed or a load drew current.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Test the complete contract}
\begin{itemize}
 \item Exhaust enum encodings, load/flyback cross-products, canonical absence,
       all identity/revision mismatches, and one-field digest mutations.
 \item Exercise zero divisors and ceilings, rounding vectors, widened
       multiplication boundaries, exact/one-over budgets, and output canaries.
 \item Test duration, expiry, early close, eight-entry pruning, ninth-entry
       exhaustion, duty equality/one-over, rollover, and half-range time.
 \item Test inactive/active intent, source ineligibility, producer fault,
       cancellation/fault collision, reset, shutdown, and idempotence.
\end{itemize}
