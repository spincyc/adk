\lessonbox{\textbf{Responsibility.}
\texttt{ModuleCharacterizationPolicy} streams one ascending learning leg, one
descending learning leg, and one independent verification leg. It publishes
sampled transition brackets, conservative intervals, witnesses, and relation
evidence. It owns no endpoint, clock, stimulus, power, storage, or display.}

\section{Ask only what copied evidence can answer}
A finite sweep cannot reveal an exact threshold or prove that a physical pin
is stuck. It can show that one copied comparator change occurred between two
adjacent copied raw codes. Lesson 071 preserves that uncertainty instead of
averaging it away.

\section{Use the frame as readiness authority}
The complete Lesson 070 frame is the sole copied warm-up and settling
authority. A point does not add another readiness flag. Both channels must be
present and current, both producer statuses must be OK, and comparator
assertion must agree with explicit active-high or active-low polarity.

\lessonbox{\textbf{Vocabulary discipline.}
``Increasing'' and ``decreasing'' describe raw ADC-code order only. They do
not mean increasing light, heat, sound, distance, hazard, or concentration.}

\newpage
\section{Learn one bracket in each direction}
Ascending accepts \texttt{Increasing}; descending accepts
\texttt{Decreasing}. On the first canonical comparator change, the immediately
adjacent points become the complete \texttt{ModuleTransitionBracket}.
The policy never sorts, interpolates, reverses, retries, or discards points.

\section{Retain intervals, not invented precision}
Every learning leg must cover the complete declared raw domain. Ascending
begins exactly at the lower bound and ends exactly at the upper bound;
descending begins at the upper bound and ends at the lower bound. A missing
endpoint records \texttt{DirectionViolation}, except when every accepted point
is the same exact declared endpoint. That endpoint-only no-transition special
case records \texttt{AtLowerRail} or \texttt{AtUpperRail} before the coverage
check, terminalizes the run, cannot advance to another leg, and produces no
intervals. Every successful learning leg therefore covers the full domain,
preventing outer guaranteed intervals from extrapolating a state beyond
observed sweep coverage.

Before intervals exist, the two brackets must have complementary orientation:
ascending-before equals descending-after, ascending-after equals
descending-before, and those two states differ. Otherwise descending
finalization records
\texttt{TransitionOrientation\allowbreak Mismatch}.

Let \texttt{lowState} be the common ascending-before/descending-after state.
Let \texttt{lowProved} be the lesser raw code of those two points, and
\texttt{highProved} the greater raw code of ascending-after and
descending-before. The policy freezes inclusive
\texttt{guaranteedInactive\allowbreak Interval},
\texttt{guaranteedActive\allowbreak Interval}, and
\texttt{ambiguityInterval} values.
The interval from the declared lower bound through \texttt{lowProved} proves
\texttt{lowState}; the interval from \texttt{highProved} through the declared
upper bound proves the opposite. The closed middle
\texttt{[lowProved + 1, highProved - 1]} is ambiguity only when its lower
endpoint does not exceed its upper endpoint. Arithmetic widens before adding
or subtracting. The two outer intervals are assigned active/inactive according
to \texttt{lowState}. The public contract has no bracket midpoint, exact
threshold, or signed hysteresis scalar.

\newpage
\section{Verify without learning from the answer}
Verification accepts \texttt{Unordered} copied points only after both brackets
and intervals are frozen. It never moves either learned bracket.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.95\linewidth,height=2.20in,keepaspectratio]
 {071-conservative-intervals-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: a pencil number line separates guaranteed
inactive and guaranteed active regions with a cross-hatched ambiguity region;
matching marks sit in guaranteed regions, an uncertain mark stays ambiguous,
and one contradiction is circled.}
\end{center}

\begin{itemize}
 \item A matching state in a guaranteed region contributes
       \texttt{Consistent}.
 \item A contradictory state in a guaranteed region produces
       \texttt{AnalogComparatorDisagreement} and \texttt{Disagrees}.
 \item A point inside ambiguity contributes \texttt{Ambiguous}, never
       disagreement merely because it is near a sampled transition.
 \item If every verification point is ambiguous, the relation is
       \texttt{Ambiguous}, not \texttt{Consistent}.
\end{itemize}

Predict the relation for a contradictory point inside ambiguity:
\longblank. Why is that answer conservative? \hrulefill.

\section{Follow the lifecycle in dependency order}
Construction is inert. The canonical flow is:

\begin{enumerate}
 \item \texttt{initialize(now)}, then
       \texttt{beginSession(now, sessionId, runId)};
 \item begin, observe exactly N points, and finalize \texttt{Ascending};
 \item repeat for \texttt{Descending};
 \item repeat for \texttt{Verification}; and
 \item copy immutable terminal evidence with \texttt{evidence(output)}.
\end{enumerate}

Leg IDs are nonzero, distinct, forward modular, and ordered ascending,
descending, verification. Initialize and reset advance a nonzero lifecycle
generation without wrap. Every successful lifecycle call retains its supplied
\texttt{now}; later session, leg, finalization, reset, and shutdown calls
require forward-or-equal modular time. Backward and exact-half-range ambiguous
times reject atomically. Shutdown is permanent for that object:
\texttt{initialize()} after shutdown returns \texttt{NotInitialized}; only a
newly constructed policy begins another object lifecycle. An incomplete
finalize or a point beyond N rejects atomically; capacity is not a fabricated
domain observation.

\section{Apply failure precedence before interpretation}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item malformed configuration or encoding;
 \item lifecycle, identity, correlation, or ordinal mismatch;
 \item attributable producer failure;
 \item attributable time or sequence discontinuity;
 \item attributable \texttt{WarmupUnsatisfied} or
       \texttt{SettlingUnsatisfied} copied readiness evidence;
 \item direction, transition, chatter, interval, or relation outcome; and
 \item complete verification evidence.
\end{enumerate}

Analog producer failure dominates comparator interpretation. Comparator
producer failure dominates chatter and relation analysis. Ordering failure
dominates an apparent crossing. Every failed API call leaves policy evidence
and caller output unchanged.

\section{Test the edges, collisions, and non-mutation}
The current deterministic host matrix exercises a reduced raw domain at its
lower, middle, and upper boundaries; complete ascending, descending, and
verification flow; clean complementary brackets; inclusive guaranteed and
ambiguity boundaries; consistent, ambiguous, and disagreeing verification;
readiness failures; endpoint-only terminal results; identity, ordering,
capacity, and lifecycle rejection; and output non-mutation. It does not claim
every possible bracket ordering or every value in the full 16-bit raw domain.
Additional exhaustive families remain explicit promotion work when not
represented by those boundary partitions.

\section{No circuit and no powered stimulus at E0}
There is no wiring diagram or formal schematic because Lesson 071 has no
circuit. Never connect or power an unidentified module. Exact E1 work must
qualify the specimen and declarations, supply and logic limits, connector,
pull/output topology, ADC reference, source impedance, inactive default,
harmless family-specific stimulus, observation points, rollback, power
removal, and measured acceptance before powered acquisition exists.

No gas exposure, heater, flame, combustion, medical or physiological claim,
body contact, capacitive-touch assumption, immersion, mains, high voltage,
laser, ignition, pyrotechnics, alarm authority, or unattended stimulus is
admitted.
