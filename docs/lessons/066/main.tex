\lessonbox{\textbf{Responsibility.}
\texttt{ThermalGradientMapper} projects two to four configured identities into
fixed spatial order, derives conservative adjacent intervals, and emits inert
page and caller-owned record intent. It does not poll a bus, drive a display,
read a clock, write storage, or establish a physical temperature gradient.}

\section{Give each identity one place}
Lesson 065 publishes four stable identified slots. Lesson 066 chooses an
ordered subset of two through four of those identities. This list means
left-to-right or near-to-far because configuration says so. Search order,
arrival order, ROM number, current temperature, and page order never become
spatial evidence.

\section{Compare complete intervals}
Each current Lesson 065 probe carries a closed signed raw-sixteenth interval.
For adjacent configured slots \(L\) and \(R\), widened arithmetic computes:
\[
 [G_{\mathrm{low}},G_{\mathrm{high}}]
 =
 [R_{\mathrm{low}}-L_{\mathrm{high}},
  R_{\mathrm{high}}-L_{\mathrm{low}}].
\]
The subtraction uses 32-bit fields even though each endpoint is 16-bit. This
prevents an extreme pair from overflowing before classification.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth,height=1.82in,keepaspectratio]
 {066-adjacent-interval-subtraction-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: four graphite gradient intervals sit around
negative and positive threshold marks on one number line, showing complete
interval examples classified as flat, rising, falling, and indeterminate.}
\end{center}

For a positive threshold \(T\), \texttt{Rising} requires
\(G_{\mathrm{low}}\geq T\), while \texttt{Falling} requires
\(G_{\mathrm{high}}\leq -T\). \texttt{Flat} requires the complete interval to
remain strictly between the two boundaries. An interval that crosses a
boundary is \texttt{Indeterminate}; the mapper never rounds it into a
direction.

\midrule
\([16,20]\) & lower equals \(T\) & \texttt{Rising}\\
\([-20,-16]\) & upper equals \(-T\) & \texttt{Falling}\\
\([-15,15]\) & wholly inside & \texttt{Flat}\\
\([15,17]\) & crosses \(T\) & \texttt{Indeterminate}\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Aggregate without hiding disagreement}
Any mapped probe or pair fault makes overall health \texttt{Fault}.
Otherwise, an indeterminate pair or a mixture of rising and falling pairs
makes \texttt{Disagreement}. At least one directional pair makes
\texttt{Gradient}; all-flat pairs make \texttt{Stable}.

Equal extrema use the lowest mapped slot for primary attribution while tie
masks preserve all equal slots. Calculate the interval for
\(L=[400,407]\), \(R=[416,419]\): \blankline\ through \blankline.

\section{Let faults dominate every affected page}
A probe contributes a number only while its copied quality is
\texttt{Current} and its inclusive \texttt{freshThrough} bound admits the
mapper's supplied \texttt{now}. Equality remains fresh; the next
representable millisecond is stale. Missing, pending, CRC-failed,
resolution-mismatched, stale, implausible-step, and transport-fault evidence
must never appear as zero, ``cold,'' flat, or cached current data.
The mapper coherence-checks the copied input age, then recomputes every
emitted page age and record age from that probe's \texttt{observedAt} to the
mapper envelope's \texttt{now}. A control edge cannot refresh either time.

\section{Keep pages inert}
Page order is fixed: overall, mapped probes in spatial order, then adjacent
pairs. A fresh \texttt{nextEdge} advances once and wraps from the final page.
The intent includes mutually exclusive selectors: \texttt{selectedSlot} is
meaningful only on a probe page, while \texttt{selectedGradient} is meaningful
only on an adjacent-gradient page; both are canonical zero on the overall
page, and the inapplicable selector is zero on either detail page. LCD content
choices, an active-slot LED mask, and grayscale-safe health/fault meaning are
values, not hardware operations.

A control has its own owner, source, configuration revision, sequence, time,
and status. Changed duplicates, regressions, future controls, half-range
ambiguity, stale controls, and exhaustion reject atomically.

\section{Create record intent, not a stored record}
A fresh \texttt{recordEdge} may fill exactly one bounded caller-owned
\texttt{ThermalMapperRecordImage}. The edge can capture a changed or unchanged
accepted probe frame. Record creation belongs to the control edge, not to a
temperature change.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth,height=1.72in,keepaspectratio]
 {066-record-intent-notebook-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: accepted update and fresh record-edge cards
cross a validation barrier into a caller-owned notebook with a witness digest;
a separate crossed-out storage claim remains outside the owned result.}
\end{center}

The record preserves owner and lifecycle, configuration revision, nonzero
record sequence, complete edge attribution, source set identity and cycle,
mapping time, every mapped ROM and interval-or-fault, every adjacent gradient,
overall health and mask, all four configured source ROMs including permitted
unmapped identities, and a deterministic digest covering that complete
witness. Full fields remain authoritative; the digest is not a substitute for
them.

\lessonbox{\textbf{Volatile boundary.}
\texttt{hasRecord} means ``the caller received one record intent in this
synchronous update.'' It does not mean a file was opened, bytes were stored,
a clock was accurate, media acknowledged a write, or data survived reset or
power loss.}

\section{Make the whole update atomic}
The mapper validates the complete copied envelope before committing policy or
touching the caller's result. A rejected update preserves every policy byte
and every result canary. A byte-identical duplicate is idempotent: it cannot
advance a page, emit a second record, refresh evidence, or extend freshness.
An exact envelope replay may republish the identical current intent image to
the caller, but it creates no new record and consumes no record sequence.

Record-edge and record-sequence exhaustion fault before zero. Reset starts a
new lifecycle and clears page and record history. Shutdown makes outputs
inactive and is idempotent; reinitialization must accept a new complete frame.

\section{Replay the project without touching hardware}
The canonical Mega sketch constructs copied Lesson 065 evidence, maps its
identities, supplies copied page and record edges, and places selected results
in named volatile cells. The sketch follows acquire, configure, start, then
observe, decide, actuate---where actuate means publish inert intent.

\begin{enumerate}
 \item \textbf{Predict:} write mapped order, three adjacent qualities, overall
       health, page index, and whether one record intent should appear.
 \item \textbf{Observe:} compile for Mega 2560 and inspect the named volatile
       result cells in a debugger, simulator, or disassembly. Serial may
       support the observation but is not required.
 \item \textbf{Interpret:} compare identities, intervals, fault mask, page,
       record edge, and sequence with the copied fixture. Explain any
       disagreement before changing evidence.
\end{enumerate}

This is the E0 non-Serial observation path. It establishes deterministic
software acquisition and publication separately from an electrical
safe-state experiment. E0 owns no endpoint, so powered resource acquisition,
released-line state, display behavior, and probe behavior remain future E1
checks.

\section{Draw the boundary around the result}
There is no wiring diagram or formal schematic at E0. A future powered
boundary must first qualify exact probe specimens and supply mode, bus loading
and pull-up, exact LCD/LED endpoints, pin and resource ownership, electrical
safe state, independent observation, and measured acceptance. Any RTC or
media work separately needs exact devices, ownership, bounded transactions,
receipts, corruption and power-loss recovery, capacity, wear, and durability
evidence.

No version of this lesson authorizes immersion, an unknown ``waterproof''
assembly, a hot surface, heater, flame, ignition, food or medical judgment,
preservation control, or unattended thermal stimulus.

\section{Exercises and acceptance reasoning}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Scramble all discovery/input permutations for three identities. Show
       why the complete mapper image remains in configured spatial order.
 \item Test interval boundaries immediately below, at, and above both
       thresholds. Explain every indeterminate result without rounding.
 \item Make the middle probe stale. Mark both incident fault gradients and
       the healthy nonincident evidence that remains attributable.
 \item Advance from the last page to overall. Replay the identical control,
       then change one copied control field at the same sequence.
 \item Emit a fresh record edge over an unchanged qualified snapshot. List
       what the record proves and five durability claims it cannot prove.
 \item Replay ordinary rollover, future time, exact half-range ambiguity,
       record exhaustion, reset, shutdown, and reinitialize. Confirm every
       rejected caller image remains byte-for-byte unchanged.
\end{enumerate}
