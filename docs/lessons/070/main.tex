\lessonbox{\textbf{Responsibility.}
\texttt{ModuleThresholdDescriptor} states the copied electrical declaration
for one declared fixture reference. Stateless functions validate its internal
structure, validate one correlated copied \texttt{ModuleThresholdFrame}, and
interpret comparator level only under declared polarity. They do not identify,
probe, energize, sample, calibrate, or qualify a physical board.}

\section{Require identity before traits}
Many boards expose an analog output, a comparator output, or both. Similar
connectors and seller names do not make their voltage, pinout, polarity,
output stage, pull, adjustment, or timing interchangeable. Lesson 070 begins
with nonzero descriptor, declared-specimen, declared-electrical-evidence, and
revision fields rather than a universal ``sensor module'' guess.

\section{Keep the two channel contracts separate}
\texttt{ModuleChannelTopology} is \texttt{AnalogOnly},
\texttt{ComparatorOnly}, or \texttt{AnalogAndComparator}. The analog lane
declares supply and signal millivolt ranges plus a raw domain. The comparator
lane declares output stage, pull requirement and rail, polarity, threshold
control kind, and direction. One lane never supplies a missing fact for the
other.

\texttt{Unspecified} is a valid E0 declaration, not permission to choose a
plausible default. Invalid underlying enum encodings reject. A pull rail is
unspecified when no pull is declared and must be specified when a pull is
declared. Unused fields take their canonical values.

\section{Treat ranges as declarations}
Ordered \texttt{ModuleMillivoltRange} and \texttt{ModuleRawDomain} fields
describe copied claims. Structural acceptance does not prove a rating or
authorize an ADC reference. Raw endpoints are codes; Lesson 070 does not call
them open, shorted, or saturated.

\section{Interpret comparator level only under polarity}
\texttt{moduleComparatorAsserted(descriptor, comparatorLevelHigh)} returns a
\texttt{Result<bool>}. With \texttt{ActiveHigh}, a high copied level is
asserted. With \texttt{ActiveLow}, it is not. \texttt{Unspecified} polarity
returns failure rather than guessing.

\section{Bind the whole copied frame}
\texttt{ModuleThresholdFrame} repeats descriptor correlation and declared
specimen/electrical provenance, then carries source identity, source
configuration revision, sequence, supplied observation time, analog value and
status, comparator level/presence/assertion and status, producer statuses, and
copied declared-duration satisfaction.

\texttt{ModuleChannelStatus} distinguishes \texttt{NotPresent},
\texttt{Current}, \texttt{Stale}, and \texttt{ProducerFault}. Analog-only
frames use the canonical comparator-absent representation; comparator-only
frames use the canonical analog-absent representation. Presence, status,
producer status, and derived assertion must agree with topology.

If analog producer status fails while the copied raw value happens to lie in
range, the channel status must be \longblank. Why may the raw value not repair
the failure? \hrulefill.

\newpage
\section{Declare time; never hide a clock}
\texttt{ModuleDeclaredDuration} pairs \texttt{Known} or \texttt{Unknown} with
a \texttt{Duration}. Known zero means the declaration explicitly requires no
delay. Unknown is canonically paired with numeric zero and blocks declaration
completeness. The frame's copied warm-up or settling satisfaction must be
false when the corresponding duration is unknown.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil

hourglass, an adjustment gesture, a settling hourglass, and finally reaches

Lesson 070 reads no clock, waits for nothing, and does not turn a
potentiometer. The frame's warm-up-satisfied and settling-satisfied fields are
copied declarations bound to its exact provenance. Lesson 071 will apply
supplied time to a characterization run.

\section{Do not promote completeness into authority}
\texttt{moduleDescriptorDeclarationsComplete()} reports only whether every
structural declaration required by the descriptor contract is present. Even a
true result is not E1 admission, authorization to power, specimen conformance,
or bench acceptance. Exact identity, primary sources, traced topology,
ratings, measurements, and safe-state evidence remain open.

\lessonbox{\textbf{Vocabulary discipline.}
Say ``declaration complete enough for E1 review,'' never ``this module is
safe,'' ``qualified,'' or ``supported.''}

\newpage
\section{Pass every structural gate}
\texttt{validateModuleThresholdDescriptor()} checks encoding, identity and
revisions, ordered ranges, topology-specific output and pull fields, declared
durations, and threshold-control semantics. It is stateless and repairs
nothing.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.91\linewidth,height=2.18in,keepaspectratio]
 {070-validation-funnel-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: five hand-drawn sieves check identity and
revisions, ranges, topology and pulls, timing, and adjustment semantics; only
one coherent descriptor exits while malformed cards remain outside.}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Reject invalid enum encodings or zero required identities/revisions.
 \item Reject inverted millivolt ranges or raw domains.
 \item Enforce topology-specific comparator, pull, and canonical-unused fields.
 \item Enforce known/unknown duration canonicality.
 \item Enforce fixed/potentiometer direction cross-fields.
\end{enumerate}

\texttt{validateModuleThresholdFrame()} next checks exact descriptor,
declared-specimen/electrical provenance, source, topology, presence, statuses,
producer statuses, assertion, and duration-satisfaction correlation. Invalid
structure returns status; an attributable \texttt{ProducerFault} remains a
valid copied frame rather than being rewritten as malformed input.

\section{Exercise collisions}
Predict the first disposition for each pair: invalid polarity encoding plus
producer fault \longblank; descriptor revision mismatch plus stale analog
channel \longblank; coherent producer fault plus an otherwise in-range value
\longblank.

stages, pulls, polarity, threshold controls, directions, and timings.

\section{Keep earlier lesson meaning intact}
Lesson 070 does not retrofit module identity into \texttt{AnalogInput},

the E0 quality classifications in Lessons 034--042 or 061--063 as electrical
qualification. Lesson 071 owns empirical supplied-sweep characterization.

\newpage
\section{Replay the stateless contract}
The canonical Mega sketch acquires copied descriptor and frame fixtures,
configures their declared identity, starts no hardware, validates them, and
publishes named volatile topology, polarity, raw, comparator assertion,
channel-status, duration-satisfaction, provenance, declaration-completeness,
and returned-status cells.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth,height=2.00in,keepaspectratio]
 {070-replay-ledger-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: identical descriptor and frame card pairs pass
through two stateless validation runs and produce matching named output cells;
crossed-out power and wiring marks remain outside the replay.}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item \textbf{Predict:} write the expected topology, polarity, comparator
       assertion, channel statuses, duration flags, declaration-completeness
       result, and returned statuses.
 \item \textbf{Observe:} compile for Mega 2560 and inspect named volatile cells
       with a debugger, simulator, or disassembly. Serial is optional support.
 \item \textbf{Interpret:} compare two runs field by field. Structural
       agreement proves deterministic copied behavior, not fixture truth.
\end{enumerate}

The named result cells are the E0 non-Serial observation path. E0 claims zero
pins, ADC channels/references, timers, interrupts, buses, rails, endpoints,
power resources, or registry entries. Therefore resource acquisition and
electrical safe-state evidence remain distinct open E1 checks.

\section{Deterministic test matrix}
Cover every valid and invalid enum encoding; topology/output/pull/rail/polarity/
control/direction cross-product; zero, one, and maximum identities/revisions;
equal, ordered, and inverted ranges; known-zero, known-nonzero, and unknown
durations; all three channel topologies; active-high/low assertion; producer
fault collisions; provenance drift; canonical absent fields; and proof that
no accepted or rejected fixture invokes an endpoint, clock, registry,
transport, or power operation.

\section{Exercises}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Construct an analog-only descriptor and list every comparator field's
       canonical unused value.
 \item For one high copied comparator level, predict assertion under all three
       polarity values.
 \item Explain why \texttt{OpenDrain} plus \texttt{PullUp} still cannot choose
       a resistor value or rail implementation.
 \item Create two descriptors with identical behavior but different declared
       specimen references. Explain why they are unequal.
 \item Explain why true declaration completeness is not physical admissibility.
\end{enumerate}

\section{No circuit at E0}
There is no pin-by-pin connection list, wiring diagram, or formal schematic
because Lesson 070 has no circuit. Never connect an unidentified module.
Future E1 work must record exact chip and PCB markings, front/back photographs,
primary sources or a traced circuit, connector pin order, supply and logic
limits, regulator, comparator stage, pull rail and resistance, onboard LED
loading, adjustment direction, warm-up and settling measurements, ADC
reference/source impedance, current, named analog/digital/power/ground test
points, inactive default, rollback, power removal, and bench observations.

This lesson admits no register device, unidentified emitter, gas exposure or
heater, concentration or alarm claim, physiological claim, mains, ignition,
laser, pyrotechnics, generic analog-temperature board, generic capacitive-touch
board, or family absent from the authorized inventory.
