\lessonbox{\textbf{Responsibility.}
\texttt{InertModuleCharacterizationBench} validates one atomic copied
envelope, advances a bounded review script, and prepares one canonical
192-byte caller-owned volatile image. It does not identify, power, sample,
calibrate, display, persist, accept, or declare safe a physical module.}

\section{Compose evidence without widening authority}
Lesson 070 publishes a declared fixture contract. Lesson 071 characterizes
copied samples for that exact descriptor and run. Lesson 072 consumes both
together in one immutable \texttt{ModuleCharacterizationEnvelope}. It never
reads two mutable child objects and hopes they describe the same instant.

\section{Bind one exact declared fixture}
\texttt{ModuleBenchConfig} fixes bench, envelope, record, descriptor,
specimen, electrical-evidence, digest, and control-source revisions. The
authoritative descriptor appears once inside the evidence. A changed
descriptor, stale run, source mismatch, malformed enum, or digest mismatch
rejects before session state or caller bytes change.

\texttt{beginSession()} applies lifecycle/configuration checks first, then
structural envelope and descriptor/evidence validation, exact correlation,
digest equality, and terminal admissibility. A structural, correlation, or
digest failure rejects atomically even when another field resembles a
producer fault. Only a fully correlated, digest-correct attributable
rejection is admitted as fault-dominant evidence.

An \texttt{Unspecified} declaration may remain readable at E0, but it cannot
be promoted into electrical admission. Light, sound, Hall, thermal,
flame/radiant, Metal Touch, vibration, and obstacle-family labels are merely
allowlisted copied-evidence shapes. They do not prove identity or connection.
A flame/radiant label authorizes no flame, heater, ignition source, or hot
target.

Suppose plausible evidence names a different electrical-evidence revision.
Should the session begin? \blankline. Which information remains authoritative:
the digest alone or the complete fields? \longblank.

\section{Review exactly five steps}
The bench starts \texttt{Ready}. One valid terminal envelope begins a
\texttt{ScriptActive} session at \texttt{InspectDeclaration}. Each accepted
\texttt{Advance} visits one next named step. There is no hidden acquisition
loop and no duplicated begin/finish-leg protocol.

\newpage
\section{Keep chronology and control separate}
A \texttt{ModuleBenchControl} binds source/configuration identity, session,
sequence, observation time, command, and producer status. Evidence time,
control time, sample sequence, run identity, lifecycle generation, and script
step remain different quantities.

An unchanged duplicate is idempotent. A changed duplicate, regression, future
control, stale control, required sequence gap, exact half-range ambiguity, or
sequence exhaustion rejects without mutation. Producer failure dominates a
plausible command. Direct \texttt{reset()} alone discards the saved compact
snapshot, advances the nonzero generation, and returns to \texttt{Ready}.
\texttt{Shutdown} is terminal and inert.

\section{Preserve characterization meaning}
Raw analog values remain integer codes in the declared ADC domain. Lesson 072
does not convert them into temperature, light, sound, gas concentration,
distance, or touch capacitance. Comparator physical level remains separate
from descriptor-interpreted assertion, so active-low is not inverted twice.

Ascending and descending brackets, guaranteed-active and
guaranteed-inactive intervals, ambiguity, verification relation, chatter,
no-transition, stale, disagreement, and endpoint-only rail results stay
attributable to Lesson 071. Healthy rail points are ordinary evidence.
\texttt{AtLowerRail} and \texttt{AtUpperRail} can arise only from
endpoint-only finalization; neither means \texttt{OpenLike} or
\texttt{ShortLike}.

\lessonbox{\textbf{Fault dominance.}
A plausible threshold cannot erase an identity, producer, age, rail,
disagreement, or correlation fault. Diagnostic values may remain inspectable
only when their invalid or diagnostic status remains explicit.}

\section{Prepare one normative volatile image}
\texttt{ModuleCharacterizationRecordCodec} is the only encoder and decoder.
The bench derives a compact semantic record, stages all 192 bytes, validates
the result, and only then copies it into caller-owned output. No coordinator
side path hand-encodes a second format.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth,height=2.22in,keepaspectratio]
 {072-record-card-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: a rough graphite record card is divided into
ADMC framing, compact declarations, run evidence, and CRC-16 columns. Notes
state that it is exactly 192 little-endian bytes with reserved bytes zero and
is not a certificate or media receipt.}
\end{center}

The image begins with ASCII \texttt{ADMC}, version, length 192, record and
bench revisions, lifecycle/session identity, then compact descriptor
declarations. It retains run identity and revision, three leg counts,
ascending and descending brackets, three conservative intervals, comparator
relation, witness and envelope digests, first/last sequences, terminal
state/reason/status, script step, descriptor/envelope revisions, and control
source/configuration. Reserved bytes are zero. CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE covers bytes
0--189 and occupies bytes 190--191.

The image intentionally omits terminal leg, current leg ID, observation
times, control ordinals, directions, complete points, and full producer
statuses. Those authoritative values remain in Lesson 071 evidence and are
bound by its digest. The compact image cannot reconstruct the full envelope
or a Lesson 071 result.

Decode checks exact span size, then framing, CRC, semantic/canonical fields,
and only then assigns the staged output. Thus only a non-192-byte span is
\texttt{BadLength}; wrong magic, version, or encoded length is
\texttt{BadFraming}; a CRC mismatch is \texttt{BadIntegrity}; and a CRC-valid
semantic defect is \texttt{BadSemanticValue}. Encode validates semantics
before capacity, stages the image, zeroes reserved bytes, computes CRC last,
and copies only on success. Compact-record digests are opaque and are not
recomputed by the codec.

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Change one reserved byte. Predict decoder outcome: \longblank.
 \item Prepare twice after \texttt{RecordPrepared}. Byte difference:
       \blankline.
 \item Reset after preparation. Is the former image durable evidence?
       \blankline.
\end{enumerate}

\lessonbox{\textbf{Volatile boundary.}
The image records copied E0 review state during this process lifetime. It is
not a hardware acceptance record, calibration certificate, conformance
report, safety approval, filesystem commit, or power-loss durability claim.}

\section{Replay without touching hardware}
The canonical Mega sketch constructs one descriptor and terminal evidence,
forms their copied envelope, begins one session, advances the review script,
prepares the record, and copies state, step, relation, fault, and
\texttt{recordPrepared} into named volatile result cells.

\begin{enumerate}
 \item \textbf{Predict:} write the expected bench state, script step,
       comparator relation, fault state, prepared flag, and first four image
       bytes.
 \item \textbf{Observe:} compile for Mega 2560 and inspect the named volatile
       cells and caller-owned image in a debugger, simulator, or disassembly.
       Serial may support observation but is not required.
 \item \textbf{Interpret:} compare the complete envelope, script progression,
       record framing, and fault precedence. Explain any disagreement before
       changing the fixture.
\end{enumerate}

This is the E0 non-Serial observation path. It proves deterministic software
publication separately from resource acquisition and released-line evidence.
E0 owns zero pins, ADC channels, timers, interrupts, buses, supplies, displays,
LEDs, power switches, or storage endpoints, so physical safe-state evidence
remains open.

The canonical repository Mega build measures 24,860 B flash and 1,998 B
static SRAM ordinarily. The isolated no-LTO build measures 27,354 B flash
plus 2,002 B static SRAM. Measured stack is 740 B, the bench object is 436 B,
each image is exactly 192 B, staged plus destination images are exactly
384 B, and residual SRAM is 5,322 B. The no-LTO flash result exceeds the
24 KiB target but remains below the independently reviewed 32 KiB hard limit.
All other reported measures pass their targets and hard limits. This reviewed
software target miss is not hidden, and passing the hard gates cannot be
presented as physical acceptance.

\newpage
\section{Draw the powered acceptance boundary}
There is no wiring diagram or formal schematic at E0. Before any powered
claim, one exact specimen needs traceable markings and revision, primary
electrical evidence, supply and signal limits, output-stage and pull
requirements, ADC reference/domain, warm-up and settling declarations,
potentiometer direction, and an electrically authoritative formal schematic.

The E1 bench card must separately record prediction; where and when to observe
a non-Serial result; interpretation; resource-acquisition evidence; safe-state
evidence; ascending, descending, and verification runs; comparator/analog
agreement; rail and chatter outcomes; and acceptance disposition. A compiled
sketch, host fixture, family name, or plausible curve is not that record.

No heater, flame, laser, ignition source, pyrotechnic control, launcher,
unknown-protocol replay, energized moving load, or unattended hazard is
authorized. An unidentified board stays unpowered.

\section{Exercises and acceptance reasoning}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Pair one descriptor with terminal evidence from another run. Prove
       every state value and caller byte remains unchanged.
 \item Exercise both comparator polarities and both ramp directions. Show
       where physical level and interpreted assertion remain distinct.
 \item Drive each named script step, then prove \texttt{Advance} alone never
       writes the record image.
 \item Corrupt framing, enum, reserved, payload, and CRC fields. Confirm the
       decoder never partly publishes a record.
 \item Replay chatter, no transition, a healthy rail point, endpoint-only
       rail finalization, stale evidence, and disagreement. Explain why none
       becomes a physical root-cause diagnosis.
 \item List the exact E1 evidence needed before connecting one declared
       module, and distinguish resource acquisition from safe-state proof.
\end{enumerate}
