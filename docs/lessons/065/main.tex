\section{Configure exactly four stable identities}
Configuration contains four distinct complete ROM codes. Each must use family
byte \texttt{0x28} and pass the Dallas CRC-8 over its first seven bytes. The
algorithm begins at zero, processes each byte least-significant bit first,
uses reflected polynomial \texttt{0x8c}, and applies no final XOR.
\footnote{\href{https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ds18b20.pdf}{Analog Devices, \emph{DS18B20 Programmable Resolution 1-Wire Digital Thermometer}, Rev. 6.}}

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth,height=1.85in,keepaspectratio]
 {065-four-identity-slots-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: four numbered graphite storage bins each hold
a deliberately different token shaped like a small probe, a visual metaphor
for four identities that retain their assigned slots rather than depictions of
qualified physical specimens.}
\end{center}

Each slot also configures 9-, 10-, 11-, or 12-bit resolution, a conversion
deadline, maximum age, inclusive signed raw-sixteenth range, and maximum
per-cycle step magnitude. There is no runtime count, empty wildcard, or
replacement identity. Search order cannot change configured order.

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l l l X@{}}
\toprule
Resolution & Undefined raw bits & Maximum conversion & Represented interval\\
\midrule
9 bit  & 3 & 94 ms  & \([m,m+7]\) sixteenths\\
10 bit & 2 & 188 ms & \([m,m+3]\) sixteenths\\
11 bit & 1 & 375 ms & \([m,m+1]\) sixteenths\\
12 bit & 0 & 750 ms & \([m,m]\) sixteenths\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

Here \(m\) is the signed raw value after masking undefined low bits. The closed
interval describes digital code space, not sensor accuracy or uncertainty.
The final API requires the configured deadline to equal the resolution maximum
for its slot; a shorter or longer value rejects configuration.

\section{Make search continuity prove presence}
The builder retains at most four
\texttt{Ds18b20NormalizedSearchPass} records. Each record contains the
normalized transaction reference plus its requested and completed
\texttt{OneWireSearchState}.
The first request uses empty search state. Every later request must equal the
immediately preceding completed result, including ROM,
\texttt{lastDiscrepancy}, and \texttt{lastDevice}. No pass may follow a
terminal result. The request/result chain is the bounded policy rendering of
the manufacturer's discrepancy-tree search procedure.\footnote{\href{https://www.analog.com/en/resources/app-notes/1wire-search-algorithm.html}{Analog Devices, \emph{1-Wire Search Algorithm}, Application Note 187.}}

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth,height=1.95in,keepaspectratio]
 {065-search-chain-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: eight distinct graphite beads form one
connected chain, ending in a bead with a check mark; a magnifying glass above
suggests inspecting each linked search result and its terminal evidence.}
\end{center}

A complete nonempty enumeration ends with \texttt{lastDevice} true. One
through four returned devices may therefore prove configured identities
missing. A zero-pass cycle cannot prove all four missing because it contains
no completed search witness. Four results ending nonterminal may explicitly
prove over-capacity; the fifth device is never silently dropped.

collapsed.

\section{Bind conversion and read evidence}
For each returned device, conversion start, conversion-status continuation,
and scratchpad read must agree on exact ROM, owner, lifecycle, configuration,
predecessor transaction, and one nonzero conversion generation. A crossed
witness rejects the entire cycle atomically.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth,height=1.55in,keepaspectratio]

Completion requires a correlated conversion-status receipt whose copied bit
is high. Elapsed time alone does not prove completion. A high bit may arrive
before the configured maximum. A low bit exactly at or after that maximum, or
a high bit after it, is transport-fault evidence.

\section{Decode scratchpads without inventing precision}
A DS18B20 scratchpad is exactly nine bytes. Byte eight must equal the Dallas
CRC-8 of bytes zero through seven before temperature or resolution is used.
Configuration byte bit 7 must be zero and bits 4--0 must be one; bits 6--5
encode resolution and must match the configured slot.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth,height=1.95in,keepaspectratio]
 {065-scratchpad-interval-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: a pencil points to a graphite notebook strip
of ordered byte-like cells whose final cell bears an integrity shield and
check mark, emphasizing validation before interpretation.}
\end{center}

The published numeric unit remains a signed sixteenth of a degree Celsius.
Negative values use the signed 16-bit scratchpad representation. Widened
arithmetic forms intervals and absolute step differences so endpoints do not
overflow.

Raw \(+85^\circ\mathrm{C}\) is not automatically bad. Without exact correlated
completion it is the reset-default quality; after correlated completion it is
a possible current reading. Likewise, an implausible step is a domain warning,
not proof that a physical event was impossible. The new valid sample becomes
the next comparison baseline.

\section{Preserve trusted values through pending and missing states}
Freshness begins at the caller-supplied policy-clock time passed with the
correlated scratchpad. Lesson 065 also retains transaction-clock times, but
does not invent an epoch mapping between the two clocks. Age equal to
\texttt{maximumAge} remains current; one millisecond older is stale.

\begin{center}
% ADK visual: pencil
\includegraphics[width=0.96\linewidth,height=1.95in,keepaspectratio]
 {065-quality-history-pencil.png}

\small\textit{Alternative text: four numbered positions support separate

\texttt{ConversionPending} retains the last trusted raw value, interval,
observation time, and increasing age. It does not read an incomplete
scratchpad or refresh freshness. \texttt{Missing} likewise retains the stable
identity and prior trusted value. A later fully correlated current cycle
returns that same identity to its original slot.

Set-level structural and transport decisions precede per-slot qualities. A
valid cycle commits all four slots together, so one CRC fault does not erase
three current readings. Status precedence follows configured slot order after
set-level failures; status and quality remain separate facts.

\section{Reject structural changes atomically}
Cycle sequence is nonzero and modularly forward; \texttt{UINT32\_MAX} followed
by 1 is its defined nonzero rollover. Zero, regression, backward time, and an
exact half-range ambiguity reject. An equal sequence accepts only a
field-for-field identical completed builder image.

A fieldwise-identical normalized duplicate is idempotent: it cannot refresh age, extend a
pending conversion, advance step history, or change disappearance evidence.
A changed duplicate rejects without modifying policy state or the caller's
output. Ingestion failure leaves every byte of the staged builder unchanged;
failed finalization leaves policy and output unchanged.
